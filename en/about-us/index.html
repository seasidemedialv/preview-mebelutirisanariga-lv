About us: how we work

Mēbeļu Tīrīšana Rīga cleans sofas, upholstered furniture, chairs, mattresses and car seats at the customer's place in Riga and the Riga region. We take requests through the form: send a photo, and a specialist will tell you the price. We publish our prices and say what may stay before the work starts.

What we do

  • Sofa cleaning: from €35.
  • Upholstered furniture cleaning: armchairs, pouffes, headboards, from €10.
  • Chair cleaning: from €7.
  • Mattress cleaning: from €30 per side.
  • Furniture cleaning for businesses: the same unit prices, larger volumes by agreement.
  • Car seat cleaning: seats, child car seats and prams, from €12.

We work with flat and house owners, landlords and businesses. The minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45. Travel is included in Riga and up to about 15 km outside Riga; further out it is from €10.

How a request works

  1. You send the form Give your name, phone number and address, choose the service and briefly describe the situation. A photo helps us give a more exact price.
  2. We contact you within 5 minutes After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes, confirm the furniture, the fabric and the stain and tell you what you can expect.
  3. We agree the price We agree the price before the job. If the price is not acceptable, the request places no obligation on you.
  4. Cleaning at your place The specialist works at your home, in your office or where the car is parked. Afterwards the furniture has to be left to dry.

What we tell you honestly

Not every stain comes out and not every smell goes. We say this straight away, not in the small print. Everyday dirt and fresh stains usually come out. An old stain, urine in the foam, a smoke smell, faded colour and wear may stay. A specialist tells you, from a photo or on the spot, what you can expect from your furniture, and then you decide.

That is why we do not quote percentages, do not promise a specific drying time and make no health promises. More detail: what can come out and what may stay.

What we do not do

  • We do not clean carpets.
  • We do not clean the whole car interior, only seats.
  • We do not treat allergies and do not promise that cleaning will ease them.
  • We do not get rid of bedbugs and fleas: that is a job for pest control.
  • We do not promise that mould inside foam will go. It often means the piece has to be replaced and the damp dealt with.
  • We do not repair or re-cover furniture.

How we set the price

Prices are published on the prices page as “from” prices per piece. They depend on size, fabric, how dirty the furniture is, smell and distance. You always find out the price before the job.

Why only a form

The price of a piece of furniture depends on details: size, fabric, stain and address. In a written request with a photo they do not get lost, and the specialist can answer more exactly in the first conversation. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes, so everything can also be talked through.

Languages

The site is in Latvian, English and Russian. You can write your request in any of these languages.

Frequently asked questions

Does a request place any obligation on me?

No. A request simply asks us to get in touch and give a price. We agree the job and the time when we speak.

Who cleans my furniture?

The job is done by a specialist, who confirms the furniture and the fabric before the job and tells you, from a photo or on the spot, what you can expect.

Do you also clean in rental flats?

Yes. The furniture in a rental flat can be cleaned between tenants, and the owner can give the tenant's contact details in the request: rental flat furniture cleaning after tenants.
