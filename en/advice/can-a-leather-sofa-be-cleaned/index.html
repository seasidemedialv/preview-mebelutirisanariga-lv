Can a leather sofa be cleaned

Can a leather sofa be cleaned? Yes, but not in the same way as fabric: leather is harmed by a lot of water, by heat and by methods that are normal for textiles. Finished furniture leather is cared for by wiping off the dust and, from time to time, cleaning it lightly with mild soap and warm water. Creams, saddle soap and oil should not be used. Deeply soaked-in stains and loss of colour may stay.

What is allowed on a leather sofa and what is not

Do Do not
wipe off dust with a dry soft cloth do not pour water on leather or soak it
on finished leather, occasionally use mild soap and warm water, and wipe off the foam do not use saddle soap, because it is alkaline
wipe dry and buff with a soft cloth do not use creams or oil on finished leather
blot spilled liquid immediately do not dry with a hairdryer or heater: heat dries leather out, makes it brittle and deforms it
check the manufacturer's care instructions do not rub a stain using methods meant for fabric

Sources on leather do not fully agree. Virginia Cooperative Extension material allows mild soap and water on finished furniture leather, while the Canadian Conservation Institute writes about museum objects that direct contact with water damages most leathers. The overall conclusion is simple: as little water as possible, and soak nothing.

What kind of leather you have

Not every sofa that looks like leather really is a leather sofa, and everything else depends on that.

Material What it means
Finished furniture leather gentle cleaning, very little water, no creams or oil
Suede do not clean stains, they are a job for a specialist; if it gets wet, air-dry it away from heat
Faux leather it is not leather; some imitations do not withstand dry cleaning, and the film or glue deteriorates
Unknown look at the manufacturer's documents or send a photo, because what is allowed depends on this

The coating on faux leather may peel over time, and cleaning cannot repair that. We do not say that cleaning causes or prevents it. More detail: faux leather sofa cleaning. Faux leather has no separate price: we charge the fabric sofa prices.

What to do about a stain on leather

  1. Blot. Lift the liquid with a clean cloth; do not rub. Leather absorbs liquid readily, and the stain soaks into the material itself.
  2. Do not wet it further. A lot of water harms leather, so do not try to rinse the stain out.
  3. Do not use solvents at random. Methods meant for textiles can damage the structure of leather.
  4. Ink is a separate case. The University of Georgia's advice does not promise that ink can be removed from leather, so we do not promise it either.
  5. Send a photo. A specialist will tell you what to expect from this stain.

What cleaning will not do for leather

Usually comes out

  • Dust and surface dirt: usually come out.
  • A fresh, surface stain: often becomes less visible.

May stay

  • A stain that has soaked into the leather.
  • Ink: the result cannot be promised.
  • Worn colour and cracks: these are damage, not dirt.
  • Peeling faux leather.

So before the work it is important to understand what is dirt and what is wear. A specialist will tell you from a photo or on site what to expect.

What harms leather day to day

  • Heat: keep the sofa away from a heater, because heat dries leather out and deforms it.
  • High humidity: according to Canadian Conservation Institute data on museum objects, air humidity above 65% encourages mould on leather.
  • Alkaline soap and strong substances: they are not meant for leather.
  • Stains left for a long time: leather absorbs them.

What to write on the form

  • whether it is leather, suede or faux leather, if you know;
  • what got on the sofa and when;
  • whether anything has already been used on the stain;
  • whether there are cracks, scuffs or peeling;
  • the size of the sofa and how many seats it has.

Attach an overall shot and a close-up. It is better to show cracks and peeling straight away, so that it is clear before the work what is damage and what is dirt.

Frequently asked questions

Can a leather sofa be cleaned with a damp cloth?

A slightly damp cloth is acceptable on finished leather if the leather is wiped dry afterwards. It must not be soaked. For your particular sofa, see the manufacturer's instructions.

Does leather need cream after cleaning?

Creams and oils are not recommended for finished furniture leather. If the manufacturer recommends something else, follow its instructions.

How can I tell leather from faux leather?

It is best to go by the documents or the manufacturer's description. If you do not have them, tell us where the sofa was bought and attach a photo.

Why did the leather go stiff after wet cleaning?

It may have been too wet or dried in heat. Heat dries leather out and makes it brittle, and cleaning does not reverse this change.
