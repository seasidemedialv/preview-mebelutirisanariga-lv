Child's urine on a mattress

A child's urine on a mattress is an ordinary situation, and the most important thing is to soak up the liquid quickly, not to wash the mattress. A fresh stain can often be reduced, but repeated urine stains and a smell in the core may stay. The main thing to avoid is moisture staying in the mattress: it can encourage mould.

Where liquid goes in a mattress

What to do in the middle of the night

  1. Take off the bedding and the cover Wash the cover only if the label allows it.
  2. Blot with towels Use a dry towel to blot gently, without rubbing, and keep changing it until it no longer gets wet.
  3. Leave it uncovered If possible, leave the mattress uncovered and air the room. If your child still has to sleep, it is better to move them elsewhere.
  4. Look at it in the morning If needed, gently dampen only the stained spot with a cloth moistened in cool water, then blot. Do not use hot water and do not saturate the mattress.

Sources: TEMPUR UK (towels, covers according to the label), IKEA (moisture), Emma (dampen only the spot), Virginia Cooperative Extension (cool water). Checked 07.11.2026

What is better kept off a child's mattress

Chlorine bleach and strong substances change the colour, and they can make latex brittle. Home remedies differ between sources: IKEA and the University of Georgia recommend different solutions. So we give no recipe, only what the sources agree on: blot, cool water, no heat, and a test in a hidden spot before any substance.

We have no verified data on scented products. If your child has an allergy or sensitive skin, ask a doctor. Cleaning is not allergy treatment, and we do not claim that any product suits all children.

Why moisture in a mattress matters more than the stain

The stain is what you see, but the problem is the moisture. Foam can hold moisture in the core, and in springs it can cause rust. Emma warns that excess moisture increases the risk of mould, and the Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) names infants and small children among the groups most sensitive to mould and damp.

So if the mattress has been damp for a long time, photograph both sides and check for dark patches. More detail: mould in a mattress. If you see mould, it is no longer a question of a stain but of replacing the mattress.

Sources: IKEA, Emma, Health Inspectorate. Checked 07.11.2026

Coconut fibre, foam, latex

Filling What matters
Foam Only a little moisture, only on the stained spot
Springs As little liquid as possible, moisture causes rust
Coconut fibre Sensitive to moisture; do not place on a floor where moisture builds up
Latex No strong substances, no direct sun or heat

The label shows what kind of mattress your child has. If there is none, send an overall photo and a close-up of the edge. A manufacturer's own instructions may set other rules, so read those first.

Sources: IKEA, Kurlon. Checked 07.11.2026

Airing afterwards

After an accident, leave the mattress uncovered for as long as the night and your child's routine allow. The Health Inspectorate recommends airing the bedroom every morning for at least 15 minutes, and the US Environmental Protection Agency notes that a fan speeds up drying. This does not replace soaking up the liquid, but the humidity in the room also affects how long the mattress stays damp.

Sources: Health Inspectorate, US EPA Mold Course. Checked 07.11.2026

If the accident happened in a child car seat or pram

A child car seat has stricter rules than a mattress. The straps and buckle are safety parts, and manufacturers warn that soaking and strong soap can weaken the fibres, while cleaning substances can affect the buckle. Act only according to your own car seat's instructions. Service: child car seat cleaning.

Source: Britax, How to Clean a Car Seat. Checked 07.11.2026

If it keeps happening

If it happens several times, yellow shadows and a smell may stay on the mattress. A mattress cannot be cleaned every night, so judge its overall condition from time to time.

Usually comes out

  • A fresh stain that was soaked up quickly
  • A light smell on the surface
  • The cover, if the label allows washing

May stay

  • Yellow shadows after repeated stains
  • A smell from the core
  • A damp spot that did not dry in time

If the smell stays after cleaning or the spot has been damp for a long time, the honest answer may be that the mattress has to be replaced. The specialist tells you before the work what you can expect.

Frequently asked questions

Can my child sleep on the mattress after cleaning?

Go by whether the mattress is completely dry. We do not promise a drying time: it depends on the air, the room and the mattress. It is not a good idea to sleep on a damp mattress.

Does cleaning destroy bacteria?

We do not claim that. Cleaning reduces the stain and the smell on the surface, but it is not disinfection.

How often should I order cleaning if my child regularly wets the bed?

There is no set number. First aid is usually enough, and cleaning is ordered when a stain or smell stays. On frequency in general: how often to clean a mattress.
