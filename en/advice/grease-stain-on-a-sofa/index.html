Grease stain on a sofa

A grease stain on a sofa calls for one thing at first: blot it with a clean, absorbent cloth or paper instead of rubbing. Do not use hot water or a lot of water. Then find the care label, because it says whether water or solvent may be used on the fabric. How much of the stain comes out depends on the fabric and on what has already been done to it, so we do not promise a result.

What to do in the first few minutes

  1. Blot. Use a clean, absorbent cloth or paper to blot the stain gently, without rubbing. Repeat with a clean part of the cloth.
  2. Do not soak it. If you use anything, use very little. Liquid can reach the upholstery, and a water ring may stay where the spot was too wet.
  3. Do not use hot water. Hot water can set the stain. That also applies to a fresh stain that looks quite small.
  4. Find the label. It is often hidden under the cushions or on the underside of the furniture. The letter on it tells you more than any advice on the internet.
  5. Take photos. The stain, the label and the whole sofa in separate shots: how to photograph a sofa for a price.

Why solvent on a sofa is a risk

The University of Georgia's textile advice recommends a dry-cleaning solvent for a greasy stain after blotting. In the same place there is a warning: solvents can damage the foam upholstery of furniture. So on a sofa this advice is not simple: the fabric may look good after cleaning, while the upholstery beneath it may be damaged.

The second reason is the label. Solvents are forbidden on some fabrics, water on others, and on some no liquids may be used at all.

Code on the label What it means What it means for a grease stain
W water-based foam, sparingly, no dry-cleaning solvents do not use solvent
S only a mild water-free solvent in a well-ventilated room, tested beforehand do not use water
WS or S-W a mild solvent or foam from a mild washing solution, tested beforehand the fabric and the stain decide
X no liquid at all, only vacuuming and light brushing; any other cleaning has to be decided separately do not use liquid
No code the codes are voluntary, so some furniture has none test in a hidden spot first or ask a specialist

A test means a small amount in a hidden spot, to see whether the colour comes off. Chlorine bleach and strong substances can change the colour of the fabric, so do not use them on a sofa. Home remedies vary from source to source, so we do not give them here.

What to expect from a grease stain

Usually comes out

  • A fresh stain that has only been blotted and not heated: it can usually be worked on further.
  • A stain on a fabric whose label allows the cleaning it needs: it often becomes less visible.

May stay

  • A stain set by hot water.
  • A water ring around the stain, if the spot was too wet.
  • Colour changes after bleach or strong substances.
  • Crushed pile on velour, where the stain was rubbed or pressed.

A water ring appears because too much moisture moves the fabric finish, and the finish stays at the edge of the wet area. Water and pressure can crush viscose flock irreversibly, and it must not be rubbed. More on velour: velour sofa cleaning. On limits in general: what comes out and what may stay.

If the sofa is leather or faux leather

Leather absorbs liquid readily, and the stain soaks into the leather itself. Methods that suit textiles can damage the structure of leather. Do not try to clean a stain on suede; leave it to a specialist. Be careful with solvents on faux leather too, because some imitations do not withstand dry cleaning: the film or glue may deteriorate. Frequently asked questions

The stain has already dried. Is it still worth doing anything?

Yes, but do not pour on any more liquid at random. Find the label and take a photo of the stain. We cannot promise it will disappear, but a specialist will tell you from a photo what to expect.

Can a stain be dried with a hairdryer or ironed?

No, there is no need for heat. Heat tends to set stains, and it harms leather: leather dries out, becomes brittle and loses its shape.

Why is there a ring around the spot I cleaned?

Most likely the spot was too wet. A lot of moisture can move the fabric finish, and a water ring forms. Even more water may make it bigger, so it is better to send a photo.

Can a zipped cushion cover be taken off and washed?

This is not usually done. After separate washing a cover may shrink or change colour, its backing may be damaged, and then it no longer matches the rest of the sofa.
