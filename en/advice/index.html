If the accident has just happened

For most fresh stains the first steps are the same. Gently blot the liquid with a clean, dry cloth or towel, without rubbing. If needed, lightly dampen the cloth in cold water, but do not soak the fabric, so that the liquid does not reach the filling. Do not use hot water, a hairdryer or an iron. If you want to try anything else, read the label first and test it in a hidden spot.

How we check the advice

Our care facts come from university extension services, textile and mattress manufacturers, the Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) and veterinary sources. We do not use cleaning companies' texts as sources. If we cannot confirm a fact, we do not write it, and the sources are listed at the end of the articles. Checked 07.11.2026

We are neither doctors nor vets. Cleaning does not cure allergies, does not remove damp from a home and does not deal with a cat's behaviour. Bedbugs and fleas are a job for pest control. Frequently asked questions

Does the advice replace a specialist's assessment?

No. Advice helps you act in the first few minutes and understand what you can expect. A specialist can assess a particular stain and fabric from a photo or on site.

Why don't you give home remedies?

Because sources differ, and the wrong mixture on fabric can cost more than the cleaning. We write only what sources agree on: gently blot, do not rub, cold water, no heat.

What should I do if the stain has already been treated?

Do not try again with a different mixture. Photograph the stain, note what you used and put that on the form.
