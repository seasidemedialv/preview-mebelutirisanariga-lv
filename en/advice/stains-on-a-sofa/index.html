Stains on a sofa

Stains on a sofa usually come out more easily if they are fresh and have not been rubbed or heated. The first step is the same for every stain: blot with a clean cloth, do not rub, do not use hot water. Old stains, stains set by heat and stains soaked into the upholstery may partly stay. What happens under the fabric

First aid for any stain

  1. Blot Use a clean, absorbent cloth or paper to blot gently, without rubbing.
  2. Read the label There is often a code under the seat cushions or on the underside of the sofa: W, S, WS or X. Code X means no liquid at all.
  3. Cool water, very little If the code allows it, gently blot the spot with a cloth dampened in cool water. Do not soak it, because the liquid can reach the upholstery.
  4. Test in a hidden spot Test any product in a hidden spot first, to see whether the colour bleeds.

Sources: University of Georgia Extension, Virginia Cooperative Extension, American Leather. Checked 07.11.2026.

Pen and felt-tip

There is no single solution for ink. The University of Georgia mentions solvent for upholstery, but solvents can harm foam, and red ink is treated differently. So do not rub and do not use several products one after another. The outcome of an ink stain on leather cannot be promised. Tell us on the form what kind of ink it was, if you know.

Vomit, chocolate, candle wax and sweat

We have no separate checked source for these stains, so we write only what applies to them all: remove the excess, do not rub, do not use hot water and read the label before using any product. For leather and faux leather, send a photo first. Tell us on the form what it is, and a specialist will tell you what to expect.

Why a ring stays after cleaning

A ring is often not a stain but a sign of overwetting. If fabric is overwetted, the finish moves and leaves a ring. When textiles get soaked, the colour may change or bleed and the fabric may shrink. That is why very little water should be used.

Chlorine bleach and strong substances can turn a small stain into a bigger problem, because they change the colour. The wrong product can make the colour bleed, and solvents can harm the upholstery. If this has already happened, say so on the form.

Sources: University of Georgia Extension, NC State Extension, NMSU G-402. Checked 07.11.2026.

Old stains

An old stain is not always hopeless, but the outcome cannot be promised. A stain that has been exposed to heat, or one that someone has tried to remove with several products, is harder. On light fabric a shadow is more visible: light-coloured and white sofa cleaning.

On velour and chenille some “spots” are not stains at all but pressure marks, which are a property of the fabric: velour sofa cleaning. More on the limits: what comes out and what may stay.

Source: Long Eaton Sofas care guide. Checked 07.11.2026.

Do you need a specialist for a small stain?

If the stain is small and fresh, blotting and a little cool water are often enough. It is worth bringing in a specialist if the stain is old, large or on a light or delicate fabric, if the label says S or X, or if the spot smells.

  • A slight smell on the surface

  • Colour changes from an earlier product
  • Pressure marks that are not a stain

Frequently asked questions

Does an old stain come out at all?

Sometimes, in part. The older the stain and the more heat or products it has been exposed to, the harder it is. Send a photo and tell us what it is, and a specialist will tell you what to expect.

Why is there a light or dark ring on the sofa after washing?

Usually because the fabric was overwetted and the finish has moved. The ring may stay, and it does not always come out. That is why a lot of water is not the answer.

Is hot water not better at removing a stain?

No. Hot water can set the stain. Use cool water on a fresh stain.

Can I leave the stain until the specialist comes?

Yes, if it has been blotted and nothing else has been put on it. That is better than several products one after another.
