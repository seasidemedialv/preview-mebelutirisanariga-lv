What to do about urine on a sofa or bed

Urine on a sofa or bed calls for one thing: soak up the liquid as soon as you can, without rubbing or heating. These first steps decide whether the urine stays in the fabric or reaches the upholstery. Below are the steps in order, the mistakes to avoid and what you can expect from cleaning.

What happens under the fabric

The steps in order

  1. Remove blankets and bedding Lift the seat cushions, throw or bedding so the liquid does not spread further. Wash the bedding separately.
  2. Blot Use a clean, absorbent cloth, paper towels or a towel to blot gently, without rubbing, and change the cloth until it stops getting wet.
  3. Cool water, very little Gently blot the stained spot with a cloth dampened in cool or lukewarm water, then with a dry one. Do not soak it: the liquid can reach the upholstery.
  4. Air, not heat Open a window if the weather allows and keep the air moving. A fan speeds up drying. There is no need for a hairdryer, iron or heater.
  5. Send a photo While everything is still clear, take a photo of the spot and the label. Tell us what happened and how much liquid there was.

Sources: Virginia Cooperative Extension (cool or lukewarm water, do not soak, never hot), University of Georgia Extension (blot, do not rub), TEMPUR UK (towels for a mattress), US EPA (fans). Checked 07.11.2026.

The most common mistakes

Mistake What may happen
Rubbing Sources advise blotting, not rubbing; flock fabric must not be rubbed
Hot water May set the stain
A lot of water A ring may form, the colour may bleed, the fabric may shrink; the liquid reaches the upholstery
Chlorine bleach, strong substances Change the colour, may harm the upholstery
Taking the cover off and washing it It may shrink, the backing may be damaged or the colour may change

The last point surprises many people. New Mexico State University materials advise against taking furniture cushion covers off for separate washing, even if they have a zip. After washing, a cover may no longer fit or may differ in colour from the rest of the sofa.

Sources: NMSU G-402, University of Georgia Extension, NC State Extension, Brentano Fabrics. Checked 07.11.2026.

Sofas and beds are different

In a sofa, liquid passes from the fabric into the foam, and the piece cannot be turned over or washed. If the seat cushions are not attached, you can lift them so that air reaches them from underneath.

A bed often has several layers: bedding, mattress protector, mattress topper, mattress. Check each one separately and say on the form which one got soaked. Wash a cover only if the label allows it. Mattresses are sensitive to moisture: in foam it can stay in the core, and in springs it can cause rust. More detail: urine smell in a mattress.

Sources: IKEA, TEMPUR UK. Checked 07.11.2026.

If the stain has already dried

Do not pour water on it. Blotting no longer helps a dried stain, and a lot of water can carry the liquid further into the upholstery. Take a photo of the spot and tell us how long ago it happened.

Old urine is harder to deal with than fresh, and the smell may stay. More detail: what comes out and what may stay.

What happened: an adult, a child or a pet

  • A child: child urine on a mattress.
  • A cat: cat urine smell in furniture. If the cat does it repeatedly, consult a vet first.
  • A dog: dog urine smell in furniture.
  • Someone ill or in care: mattress cleaning after illness.

The other steps are the same in every case. The only differences are how often it happens and whether the cause also has to be dealt with elsewhere.

What to write on the form

  • What happened and how much liquid there was.
  • Where exactly: seat, cushion, middle of the mattress, edge.
  • How long ago it happened and whether the spot has dried.
  • What has already been done: water, a product, heat.
  • The material: fabric, leather, faux leather, the type of mattress if you know it.

These five lines let a specialist give you a price and judge whether any urine may stay behind.

What you can expect from cleaning

Usually comes out

  • A fresh, quickly blotted stain on fabric
  • A smell on the surface
  • A one-off accident that has not gone through to the upholstery

May stay

  • A smell, if the urine has reached the foam or the core of the mattress
  • A shadow on light fabric
  • A spot that has been soaked many times

Frequently asked questions

Should I take off the sofa cover and put it in the washing machine?

Usually not. The cover may shrink, the backing may be damaged or the colour may change, even if it has a zip. If the care label explicitly allows washing, follow it.

Can I wait a while before cleaning?

It is better not to put off the first steps: soaking up the liquid is the most important part. You can request the cleaning whenever it suits you, but do not leave the spot damp for long, because mould may appear in damp upholstery over time.

What comes first, water or a cleaning product?

Blotting first, then cool water, very little. Before using any product, read the label and test it in a hidden spot. Sources on home remedies disagree, so we do not give one.

Can I sit on the sofa after an accident?

It is better to wait until the spot has dried, and to air the room in the meantime. Moisture must not stay in the upholstery.
