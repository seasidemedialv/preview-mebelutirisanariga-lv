Urine smell in a mattress

Urine smell in a mattress is harder to deal with than in a sofa, because a mattress cannot be washed and its core may be hard to dry. Fresh urine can be soaked up and the smell can often be reduced, but whatever has soaked into the core may stay. First soak up the liquid with towels and find out what kind of mattress you have.

Where liquid goes in a mattress

What to do straight away

  1. Remove the bedding If the cover is removable, wash it only if its label allows it.
  2. Blot with towels Use clean towels to blot gently, without rubbing, and change them until they stop getting wet. TEMPUR recommends exactly this: soak up as much as possible.
  3. Dampen only the stain, not the mattress If needed, use a cloth dampened in cool water to lightly moisten only the stained spot, then blot again. Large amounts of liquid must not be used.
  4. Dry without heat Open a window if the weather allows and leave the mattress without bedding. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) recommends airing the bedroom every morning for at least 15 minutes. A hairdryer or heater is not the answer: latex must be kept away from direct heat, and heat can set the stain.

Sources: TEMPUR UK, IKEA, Emma, University of Georgia Extension, Health Inspectorate. Checked 07.11.2026.

What not to do

  • Do not pour water directly on the mattress and do not try to rinse it.
  • Do not rub; only blot gently.
  • Do not use hot water, a hairdryer or an iron.
  • Do not use chlorine bleach or strong substances: they change the colour and can make latex brittle.
  • Do not put clean bedding on a damp spot.

If the bed has a mattress topper or a removable cover, check those too and say on the form exactly where the stain is.

Why a mattress cannot simply be washed

The main reason is the filling, not the cover. Foam is sensitive to moisture: too much water can damage the structure or leave moisture in the core. Excess moisture also raises the risk of mould. That is why manufacturers recommend cleaning only individual spots with a small amount of moisture.

NC State Extension writes about flood-soaked mattresses that after soaking they are almost impossible to dry completely before mould starts to grow. A stain from one night is not a flood, but the principle is the same: the more water, the greater the risk. More on this: mould in a mattress.

Sources: IKEA, Emma, NC State Extension (floods, applied to the general principle). Checked 07.11.2026.

Differences by mattress type

Mattress What to bear in mind
Foam mattress Sensitive to moisture; only a little moisture, only on the stained spot
Spring mattress Moisture can cause rust in the springs; as little liquid as possible
Latex mattress No strong substances, they can make latex brittle; no direct sun or heat
Coconut fibre mattress Breathable, but sensitive to moisture and dust; do not place on a floor where moisture builds up
TEMPUR The manufacturer writes that the material cannot be washed, sponge-cleaned or dry-cleaned; check the manufacturer's care instructions first

If you do not know what kind of mattress you have, take a photo of its label. The label says what may be done, and it also decides whether cleaning with liquid is allowed at all.

Sources: IKEA, TEMPUR UK, Kurlon. We have not checked the rules for Dormeo and other brands. Checked 07.11.2026.

What may stay

Usually comes out

  • A fresh stain that was soaked up quickly and has not gone through the top layer
  • A slight smell on the surface
  • A stain on a removable cover, if the label allows washing

May stay

  • A smell coming from the core
  • A yellow shadow after repeated stains
  • A spot that has soaked up urine many nights in a row

If the smell stays after cleaning or the spot has been damp for a long time, the honest answer is that the mattress may need replacing. NC State Extension says the same: if the smell does not go, throw the mattress away. If there is mould in the mattress, it is a matter of replacement or a separate assessment.

Frequently asked questions

Can a mattress be put out in the sun to get rid of the smell?

A latex mattress must be kept out of direct sun. We have no checked source for other mattress types, so we do not promise this as a solution.

Can the cover be washed in a washing machine?

Many removable covers can be, but not all. The cover's label decides. TEMPUR says that some covers must not be washed.

Do both sides of the mattress need cleaning?

If the urine has gone through the mattress and there is a stain or smell on the other side, yes. Otherwise one side is usually enough. Second side of the mattress +50%.

Can I sleep on the mattress while it is still damp?

It is better not to. Bedding on top holds in moisture, and moisture in a mattress raises the risk of mould. How long it stays damp depends on the air, the room and the mattress, so we do not promise a number of hours.
