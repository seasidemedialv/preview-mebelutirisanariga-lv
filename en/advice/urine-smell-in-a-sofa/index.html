Urine smell in a sofa

Urine smell in a sofa is often not just in the fabric, because liquid soaks in beyond the surface. If the urine has stayed in the fabric, the smell can usually be reduced. If it has reached the foam, the smell may stay or come back, and nobody can promise otherwise. Below are first aid and when cleaning makes sense.

What happens under the fabric

What to do straight away

  1. Blot the liquid Use a clean, absorbent cloth or paper to blot gently, without rubbing, and change it until it stops getting wet.
  2. Dampen lightly Gently blot the stain with a cloth dampened in cool water, then with a dry one. Do not soak the fabric, because excess liquid reaches the upholstery.
  3. Do not heat No hairdryer, iron or hot water. Hot water can set the stain.
  4. Air the room Open a window if the weather allows and keep the air moving. A fan speeds up drying.

Before using any product, read what the label allows and test it in a hidden spot. Chlorine bleach and strong substances can change the colour.

Sources: University of Georgia Extension (blot, do not rub, do not overwet), Virginia Cooperative Extension (cool water, do not soak), US EPA (fans dry faster). Checked 07.11.2026.

Why the smell stays even when the surface is clean

Sofa fabric is thin, but beneath it there is foam or other upholstery. Foam is sensitive to moisture and can hold liquid in the core. The US Environmental Protection Agency says that upholstered furniture may be hard to dry completely. So a clean surface does not necessarily mean that everything beneath it is clean too.

The smell area may be larger than the visible stain. Vets who specialise in cats write that it may be up to three times larger than the stained spot. International Cat Care advises throwing heavily stained fabric away if possible, because it stays attractive to the cat even after cleaning. No source gives a measured limit for foam, so we give no figures.

Sources: IKEA (foam and moisture), US EPA Mold Course (drying upholstered furniture), Feline VMA, International Cat Care. Checked 07.11.2026.

If the sofa is leather or velour

Leather absorbs liquid readily, and stains soak into the leather itself. Direct water harms most leathers, so just blot the liquid lightly with a dry cloth, without rubbing, and do not wet the surface. Do not use heat at all, because it dries leather out and makes it brittle. On velour, blot the liquid from the edges towards the middle and do not rub: pressure can flatten viscose flock permanently.

We have no checked source on faux leather and urine, so there the label and a specialist's assessment from a photo decide. More: leather sofa cleaning and velour sofa cleaning.

Sources: Iowa State University Extension (leather), Canadian Conservation Institute (water and leather), US National Park Service (heat), Brentano Fabrics (flock). Checked 07.11.2026.

Whose urine: a child's, a cat's or a dog's

First aid is the same, but what follows differs. Cat urine has its own sharp smell and raises a behaviour question for a vet: cat urine smell in furniture. On dogs: dog urine smell in furniture. If the stain is fresh and you need every step: what to do about urine on a sofa.

When cleaning makes sense and when to think of something else

It usually makes sense if the urine is in the fabric or on the surface, if there is one stain and if the fabric allows cleaning. Cleaning then often reduces both the stain and the smell.

Other options need considering if urine has repeatedly soaked into one spot, if the spot has been damp for a long time or if the smell comes from the foam. Then the fabric or upholstery may need replacing, which is work for a furniture reupholstery workshop. If the spot has been damp for a long time, check for mould: mould in a sofa.

Frequently asked questions

The sofa looks clean but still smells of urine. Will it go away?

Not always. The smell may stay or come back even when the surface looks clean, especially if the liquid reached the upholstery. So we do not promise that it will disappear.

Do home remedies remove urine smell?

Home remedies vary from source to source, and they may change the colour or leave a ring. We can recommend only what all agree on: blot, cool water, no heat, and a test in a hidden spot before any product.

Do enzyme products help?

Veterinary sources mention them for pet urine: the ASPCA recommends cleaning the spot thoroughly with an enzyme product designed for pet odour. Test the product in a hidden spot before use. That does not mean the smell will disappear from the foam.

How long will the sofa stay damp?

That depends on air humidity, ventilation, room temperature, the fabric and the upholstery, so we do not promise a number of hours. More detail: how long a sofa takes to dry after cleaning.
