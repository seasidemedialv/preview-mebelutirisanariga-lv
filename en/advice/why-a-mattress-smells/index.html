A smelly mattress: why it happens and what to do

A mattress smells when moisture, urine, smoke or spilt liquid has got into it, and that decides whether cleaning will help. First air the mattress and the room, and find out whether the smell comes from the cover or the core. The surface can be cleaned, but the core is hard to dry, so the smell may stay. If it stays even when the mattress is dry, or there is mould in it, the honest answer is that the mattress usually has to be replaced.

Why a mattress smell is harder to deal with than a sofa smell

A mattress has a thick core that releases moisture poorly. IKEA points out that foam is sensitive to moisture: too much water can damage the structure or leave moisture in the core. Emma warns that excess moisture can increase the risk of mould. That is why a mattress is cleaned on the surface and with as little liquid as possible, and that is exactly why a smell that has soaked in deep may stay.

Different mattresses have different limits, and you should read the manufacturer's care instructions first.

Mattress What to know
Foam mattress little moisture, clean stains locally, with a slightly damp cloth
Spring mattress moisture can cause rust in the spring unit, so surface care only, with as little liquid as possible
Latex mattress a slightly damp cloth, no strong substances, no direct sun or heat; dry vacuuming suits delicate materials
Coconut fibre mattress breathable, but sensitive to moisture and dust; placed on the floor it can build up moisture
TEMPUR mattress manufacturer: the material must not be washed, sponge-cleaned or dry-cleaned, and must be protected from moisture; cover only as the label says

Sources: IKEA, Emma, Kurlon and TEMPUR care materials, checked 07.11.2026. The rules for a particular mattress are set by its manufacturer's instructions.

What to do now

  1. Take off the bedding. Wash the bedding separately. If the cover is removable, see its label: many can go in a washing machine, but not all.
  2. Air the room. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) recommends opening the bedroom window every morning for at least 15 minutes.
  3. Check the humidity. The comfort range is given as 40–60%. If the room is damper, cleaning does not deal with the cause.
  4. Look under the mattress. A mattress placed directly on the floor can build up moisture. If there are patches underneath, see the advice on mould.
  5. Do not soak it. Do not douse the mattress or try to wash it. Blot fresh liquid with towels as much as you can, without rubbing.

When to clean and when to replace

Cleaning is worth ordering if the smell is recent, a stain is visible and the mattress is dry. It usually improves the surface, but we do not promise the smell will disappear. Mattress cleaning takes place at your home in Riga and the Riga region, and the specialist tells you from a photo what you can expect.

A mattress usually has to be replaced if there is mould in it, if it has been soaked or if the smell does not go even after cleaning. North Carolina State University says it plainly about floods: if the smell does not go, the mattress is thrown away, and if the inside is not completely dry, mould comes back. That is a flood case, but the logic is the same.

What to write in the form

  • the type and size of the mattress and, if you know it, the manufacturer;
  • what it smells of and when it started;
  • whether a stain or patches are visible;
  • whether the cover is removable and what its label says;
  • whether the mattress has ever been soaked.

With this information, the specialist can give you the price and tell you whether cleaning makes sense at all.

Frequently asked questions

Can I put the mattress out in the sun to get rid of the smell?

A latex mattress should be kept out of direct sun and heat. For other mattresses, see the manufacturer's instructions. If the smell comes from urine or moisture in the core, the sun will not solve it.

Can my TEMPUR mattress be cleaned?

The manufacturer says the material must not be washed, sponge-cleaned or dry-cleaned. So first read your mattress's care instructions and tell us the model. The cover may be washed only if the label allows it.

Is it enough to turn the mattress over?

If the smell comes from the core, the other side will not fix it. If both sides need cleaning, the second side costs +50%.
