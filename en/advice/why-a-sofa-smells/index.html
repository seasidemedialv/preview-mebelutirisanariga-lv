A smelly sofa: why it happens and what to do

A sofa smells when urine, smoke residue, moisture or spilt liquid has stayed in the fabric or upholstery, and each cause has a different solution. First work out what it smells of and whether the liquid has reached the upholstery. Cleaning often reduces the smell, but we cannot promise it will disappear: the smell may stay or come back. With mould, cleaning is not the solution.

What to check before ordering

  1. Where the smell is strongest. Compare the seat, the backrest, the areas under the cushions and the underside of the sofa. Say so in the form.
  2. Whether there is a stain. A visible stain helps show where the liquid went. With cat urine, the area of smell can be up to three times larger than the soiled spot.
  3. What the label says. The letter W, S, WS or X sets what cleaning the fabric allows. If there is no code, test in a hidden spot before any cleaning.
  4. Whether the cause is still there. If people still smoke at home, the cat returns to the same spot or the room is damp, the smell may appear again after cleaning.

Why a smell may stay even after cleaning

Fabric is easier to clean than what lies beneath it. Foam can hold liquid and is hard to dry. If urine, a drink or water has reached the upholstery, cleaning the surface does not always reach the whole soiled area.

International Cat Care accepts this limit too: heavily soiled fabric should be thrown away where possible, because it stays attractive to the cat even after cleaning. No source gives a measured limit for foam, so we give no figures. Smoke is similar: the residue stays in the fabric, and the usual methods give only a short-lived result.

So the result depends on three things: the cause, the depth and whether the cause has been dealt with.

What is best avoided with a smelly sofa

  • Do not soak the fabric. This can cause water rings, shrinkage, colour changes and damp upholstery.
  • Do not use hot water on a fresh stain, as it can set the stain.
  • Do not use ammonia-based substances after cat urine, because to a cat they smell of urine.
  • Do not use chlorine bleach and strong substances, because they can change the colour.
  • Do not take off zipped covers to wash them separately: they may shrink or change colour.

If a cat does this repeatedly, consult a vet first, because health reasons have to be ruled out. Cleaning does not solve a behavioural cause, and the cat may return to the same spot.

When cleaning is not the right job

If there is mould in the sofa, the moisture has to be dealt with, and you may have to replace the sofa. If the smell is in the whole room rather than in the fabric, it is no longer a furniture cleaning job: we work only with textiles. Bedbugs and fleas are another matter; that is a job for pest control.

Frequently asked questions

Is it enough to air the sofa?

If the smell came from humid air, airing helps. If the cause is urine or smoke residue, these stay in the fabric and upholstery, so airing alone is usually not enough.

Can the smell come back after cleaning?

Yes, it can, especially if liquid has reached the upholstery or the cause is still there. That is why we make no such promise and tell you before the work what may stay.

Will the cat choose the same spot again?

It may. Heavily soiled fabric stays attractive to a cat even after cleaning, so the reason has to be dealt with alongside it: health, the litter tray and the environment. Veterinary sources mention cleaning with enzyme products made for cat smells, but that is only one of the steps.

How quickly will the smell go after cleaning?

We give no timescale, because the cause and the depth decide it. If the smell stays after cleaning even when the sofa is completely dry, it most likely comes from the upholstery.
