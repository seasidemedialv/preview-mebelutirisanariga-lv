Wine and coffee stains on a sofa

Wine and coffee stains on a sofa come out most easily while they are fresh: blot with a clean cloth, dampen slightly with cool water and do not soak. Do not rub and do not use hot water. Traces of colour may stay, especially on light fabric and with an old stain, so we do not promise the outcome.

What happens under the fabric

What to do in the first few minutes

  1. Blot Use a clean, absorbent cloth or paper to blot gently, without rubbing. Change it until the cloth stops picking up colour.
  2. Read the label Code X means no liquid at all, only vacuuming and light brushing. Code S means water must not be used.
  3. Cool water If the code allows it, gently blot the spot with a cloth dampened in cool water, then with a dry one. Do not soak it.
  4. Blot again Use a dry cloth to blot the spot gently, and let it dry without heat.

If the stain is small and fresh, these steps are often enough. Before using any product, test it in a hidden spot: this is the colourfastness test the University of Georgia recommends.

Sources: University of Georgia Extension (wine: blot, cool water, do not soak, test in a hidden spot), Virginia Cooperative Extension (never hot water, do not soak). Checked 07.11.2026.

Wine and coffee are not the same stain

Stain First step Next What may stay
Wine Blot, cool water Do not soak, test colourfastness Traces of colour, especially on light fabric
Coffee, tea Blot, check the label Foam, not liquid, if the label allows it A brown edge or ring

For coffee and tea on upholstery the University of Georgia mentions foam, blotting and checking the label. That matches the Virginia materials: foam rather than liquid helps avoid shrinkage and colour change. The most important thing in both cases: as little water as possible.

Sources: University of Georgia Extension (wine, coffee and tea), Virginia Cooperative Extension. Checked 07.11.2026.

By type of fabric

Fabric. If the label says W or WS, water-based cleaning is usually allowed, but sparingly. If the fabric is overwetted, the finish moves and a ring stays, and it may be more visible than the stain.

Velour and pile fabrics. Blot the liquid from the edges towards the middle. Viscose flock must not be rubbed, because water and pressure can flatten it permanently. Once the fabric has dried completely, it can be brushed in the direction of the pile. More detail: velour sofa cleaning.

Leather. Leather absorbs liquid readily and the stain soaks into the leather itself, so blot with a dry cloth and do not wet it. Do not spot-rub a stain on suede; leave it to a specialist. More detail: leather sofa cleaning and can a leather sofa be cleaned.

Sources: University of Georgia Extension, Brentano Fabrics, Iowa State University Extension. Checked 07.11.2026.

If the stain has already dried

Blotting no longer helps a dried stain, and a lot of water does not solve it: a ring may form around the stain as well. Send a photo and tell us what it is and how old, and a specialist will tell you what to expect.

If it spilled on a removable cushion

Do not take the cover off or put it in the washing machine, even if it has a zip, unless the label explicitly allows it. New Mexico State University warns that the cover may shrink, the backing may be damaged or the colour may change, and then it will differ from the rest of the sofa. A removable cushion has its own cleaning price, from €2. If it spilled on a bed: stains on a mattress.

Source: NMSU G-402. Checked 07.11.2026.

What not to do

  • Do not rub, even if the stain is dark.
  • Do not use hot water: it can set the stain.
  • Do not pour water directly on the sofa.
  • Do not use chlorine bleach: it changes the colour.
  • Do not use several products one after another.

What may stay and when to order cleaning

Usually comes out

  • A fresh stain blotted straight away
  • A small stain on dark fabric
  • Coffee on a fabric whose label allows water

May stay

  • An old red wine stain on light fabric
  • A ring around the stain after overwetting
  • Colour changes from bleach
  • A stain on viscose flock or leather

Cleaning is worth ordering if the stain is old or large, if the sofa is light-coloured, if the label says S or X, or if attempts have already spread the stain. In general: stains on a sofa. Frequently asked questions

Does salt or white wine remove a red wine stain?

The sources we checked contain no such recommendation. The University of Georgia recommends blotting and cool water for wine, so that is all we write.

Is coffee with milk harder to remove than black coffee?

We have no separate source on this, so we do not compare them. The first steps are the same: blot, cool water, no heat.

The stain dried a month ago. Is it worth trying?

It is worth sending a photo. An old stain is harder to remove and traces of colour may stay, so a specialist will tell you what to expect before you order.
