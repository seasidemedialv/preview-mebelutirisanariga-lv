New house, used sofa

Ādaži has newer private housing and apartment developments. For furniture this means one thing: the building tells you nothing about how much use a sofa has had. If a corner sofa came from a flat in Riga, it already has worn seats and older stains that nobody noticed during the move.

On light fabric in a new, bright room, every touch shows. A darker seat on a light sofa is usually dirt that has built up gradually. It often comes out, but an even tone across the whole sofa cannot be promised. There is more on this on the page light sofa cleaning in Ādaži.

Velour and chenille are a separate case. Pressure marks and changes of tone are a property of these fabrics, not damage, so cleaning does not remove them. Photos are best taken in daylight and from two angles.

Before you move in, or after

If a flat in a new development was bought with the previous owners' furniture, or the sofa is second-hand, it makes sense to sort the furniture out before you start sleeping and sitting on it. An empty room is the most convenient moment: every side can be reached, and the windows can be opened without worry. For this case there is furniture cleaning before moving in.

Another moment is after building work. Furniture that has stood next to renovation work gets covered in dust, so furniture cleaning after renovation is worth thinking about once the renovation is really finished.

The banks of the Vējupe and addresses in the municipality

The town grew up around the centre of the Gauja manor, and the newest part lies on the right bank of the Vējupe around the centre of the Rembergi manor. In practice this means one simple request: write the exact street on the form, not just “Ādaži”.

Ādaži municipality also includes coastal and rural areas. This page is about the town. If your address is in Kadaga or towards Carnikava, a request works in the same way; just give the village, and we will tell you the travel charge before the work.

Several pieces in one visit

In a private house, one visit may add up to a living room sofa, a children's room mattress and a few armchairs, and then the €45 minimum order is no longer an issue. The sums are simple:

  • A corner sofa from €50 already exceeds the minimum.
  • A two-seater sofa from €35 and an armchair from €20: together from €55.
  • A single mattress from €30 and a pouffe from €10 together start from €40, which is still below the minimum. With a dining chair from €7 it starts from €47.

If there is a child car seat in the house, it can be added: from €20. The straps and buckle are looked after as the seat's instructions require, usually with spot cleaning only. More: child car seat cleaning in Ādaži.

Businesses by the A1

Ādaži has commercial premises and public infrastructure, and the A1 leads to Riga and Saulkrasti. If an office, salon or café needs its chairs sorted out, the unit prices are the same: office chair from €10, dining chair from €7. For larger volumes we agree the price separately. On the form, write the number of chairs and when the premises are free.

Terrace cushions and the children's room mattress

If your private house has a terrace, its cushions can be added to the same order. A removable cushion costs from €2, and outdoor furniture cushions have a page of their own. Do not take zipped covers off to wash them separately unless the label allows it: they may shrink or change colour.

With a child's mattress, what usually matters most is how deep the liquid has gone. Foam holds moisture for a long time, so the mattress must not be soaked, and a deep smell may not come out. More: children's mattress cleaning in Ādaži.

After cleaning in a new house

In a new house with big windows it is easy to forget that the moisture has to leave the room. How long furniture takes to dry depends on the air humidity, ventilation, temperature, fabric and filling, so we do not name a number of hours. If the room has a fan, switch it on, and if the weather allows, keep a window open.

It is better not to let pets and children jump on a damp sofa until it has dried. If there is a dog or cat in the house, mention it on the form. More on animal marks: sofa cleaning after a cat or dog in Ādaži. If a cat keeps urinating in the same place, it is worth talking to a vet first, because cleaning does not solve the cause.

Frequently asked questions

Does a new sofa need cleaning at all?

If there are no stains and no smell, it does not. Vacuuming is enough. Cleaning makes sense when there is something you can see or smell, and that can be judged from a photo.

My sofa is faux leather. What can I expect from it?

There is no separate price for faux leather; the fabric sofa price applies. The surface can be cleaned, but if the coating has started to peel, cleaning will not repair it.

I live in Kadaga. Does this page apply to me?

The form is the same. Give the village and street, and the specialist will tell you what the travel charge is.
