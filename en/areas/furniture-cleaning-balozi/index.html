Baloži is not uniform: there are apartment buildings, private housing and houses that grew out of former garden cooperatives, and in each of them furniture has its own history.

Three kinds of Baloži and three different questions

Baloži grew up around the workers' settlement of a peat factory founded in 1947, one part of the town was newly built in the 1980s, and in 1991 the nearby garden cooperatives were added to the town. This says nothing about any individual house, but it helps you know what to ask.

  • In an apartment building, the size of the room decides everything. If one room is both living room and bedroom, the sofa bed is in use every day and every night.
  • In a former garden house, if it was converted from a summer house, the furniture may be the same pieces that stood in the cold, and it is worth asking about the damp there.
  • In the new private housing blocks and nearby estates such as Titurga: more rooms and more furniture, and a terrace too.

A sofa bed in a flat

A sofa that is slept on wears differently from a sofa that is sat on. The sleeping area collects what a person leaves behind at night, and the seats and armrests what is left during the day. Cleaning usually freshens the look, but it does not restore wear or faded fabric.

A sofa bed costs from €40, so on its own it does not yet reach the minimum order. You can add a pouffe or a chair. The page sofa bed cleaning in Baloži has more on what to show in the photo: both folded and pulled out.

The floor you live on does not matter. The work is done on site, so the sofa does not have to be carried down the stairs. Before you send a request, check that the sofa can be pulled out fully, and take the bedding off it before the work.

When a flat tends to be damp

In a flat where moisture condenses in the corners or laundry is dried in the room, furniture dries more slowly after cleaning. LSM has reported that one in five buildings in Latvia has damp problems, but that says nothing about your house. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) recommends airing the bedroom every morning for at least 15 minutes, and this habit is useful after cleaning too.

If the sofa stands right against a cold outer wall, look at the back of it before cleaning. Mould in the foam is a different matter, and cleaning does not solve it: the cause of the damp has to be dealt with, and the furniture assessed separately.

A rental flat or a garden house left by a tenant

A flat in Baloži may be let to people who work in Riga, and between tenants the landlord wants to see a clean sofa and mattress. The best moment is when the flat is empty: there is no waiting for someone to leave, and the windows can be kept open. Before cleaning, photograph the condition for your own records, as this is useful in a conversation with the tenant.

Wear is not a stain. If a tenant has left a large stain, the specialist will tell you from the photo what you can expect. If the sofa has simply aged, cleaning will not make it new. More: furniture cleaning in a rental flat after tenants in Baloži.

If someone has smoked in the flat, you have to allow for the fact that the smell may stay in the fabric and filling. Smoke residue lasts surprisingly long, so cleaning usually reduces the smell, but that cannot be promised. More in the article cigarette smoke smell in a sofa.

Baloži is a town in Ķekava municipality about 5 km from the Riga boundary, so travel is included. This applies to the town, not automatically to the whole municipality. The way from Riga usually runs along the roads towards Ziepniekkalns and Ķekava.

In apartment building yards, new housing blocks and the narrow lanes of the former gardens, the options for parking differ. In your request, say whether the house has a yard and whether there is room to turn round when leaving.

Frequently asked questions

I live in Titurga. Does that count as Baloži?

Yes, for this form the address is enough. Titurga is close by, and travel is included.

The sofa came from a garden house and smells of damp. Can that be fixed?

Send a photo and describe the smell. If the damp is only on the surface, cleaning and airing often help. If there is mould in the filling, it is more honest to think about replacing it.

My ground floor flat is damp. Won't cleaning make things worse?

Too much water harms fabric and filling, so furniture must not be soaked. What matters is airing the room and, while the furniture dries, not keeping it pressed against a cold wall.
