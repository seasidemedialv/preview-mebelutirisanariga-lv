Iecava is in Zemgale by the A7, outside Riga's nearest zone, so travel costs from €10, and we tell you the price before the work.

What Iecava means in an address

This page is about the town of Iecava, which is in Bauska municipality. Iecava parish and the former municipality are wider, and an address in the countryside differs from one in the centre. By the late 1970s the town had formed as the urban settlement merged with Dārtija, Rakmente and Pārupe, so on the form we ask you to write the street and, if the house is outside the town, the nearest settlement.

On travel: by road it is about 35 km from Iecava to the Riga boundary, so the charge is from €10. If the address is in the parish or on the edge of the municipality, the amount may change, and the specialist tells you before the work.

Multi-storey blocks: mattress, sofa and a narrow room

Multi-storey residential blocks were built in Iecava for factory workers. In flats like these, furniture is usually fitted to the room, and the sofa stands close against the wall. Before the specialist arrives, clear some space around it and take off blankets and cushions. If someone in the household sleeps on the sofa, think about where they will sleep while the fabric is still damp.

A mattress is another matter. It must not be soaked: too much liquid harms foam and springs, because the moisture stays in the core and the springs may rust. So a deep stain may not come out.

Read more: mattress cleaning at home in Iecava, double mattress cleaning and stains on a mattress. If your mattress is made of TEMPUR material, read the manufacturer's instructions first: it says the material must not be washed, sponged or dry cleaned.

A private house on the edge of town

In areas of private houses it is easier to put together an order that both covers the minimum and makes travel worthwhile. A two-seater sofa and two armchairs start from €75, a corner sofa and a single mattress from €80.

If you live in a private house with a garden, ask the family what else is standing around: a sofa moved to the veranda, chairs from the old kitchen, a pram or a child car seat. Before guests come or before a sale, it is convenient to sort everything out in one go. The page furniture cleaning in a private house in Iecava will help you count.

If a smell has soaked into the fabric, the result depends on how deep it has gone: on the surface it usually lessens, but it may not leave the filling.

What to write on the form so nothing has to be asked twice

For longer travel it is worth agreeing the price straight away, so a few specific facts in your request are useful.

  1. What it is The type of sofa and number of seats, the mattress size, and whether one side or both are to be cleaned.
  2. What happened A stain, a smell or general dirt, and how long ago it happened. A sofa's care label is usually hidden under the cushions or by the base: take a photo of it.
  3. Where it is The street and, if the house is outside the town, the nearest place and how to get there.
  4. What else is in the house Chairs, cushions and child car seats that could be added to the same trip.

If the label shows the letter X, it means no liquid at all, only vacuuming and light brushing, and any other cleaning has to be assessed separately. If there is no label at all, say so.

Businesses in Iecava

There are production sites around Iecava, and the town is a local service centre. If a company's break room has a worn sofa or office chairs, the unit prices are the same: office chair cleaning from €10, dining chairs from €7. For larger numbers the price is agreed according to volume. A production site may have its own access rules, so give a contact person and the entrance in your request.

Prices and the honest part

The minimum order is €45, and travel to Iecava is from €10. The price is affected by size, fabric, dirt, smell and distance. All prices are on the prices page.

Usually comes out

  • Dust, sweat marks and everyday dirt often become less visible.
  • Fresh liquid stains usually come out better.

May stay

  • Old, dried or heat-set stains may stay.
  • Smell from the filling may stay.
  • Tears and wear stay.

If a stain is fresh, blot it gently with a clean cloth without rubbing, and do not use hot water.

Frequently asked questions

The sofa's parts are loose, but the fabric is sound. Can it be cleaned?

It can be cleaned, but cleaning does not repair a loose frame. Send a photo and say what moves, so it can be decided whether it is worth it.

No, it starts from €10. You know the final amount before the work, and it can be agreed before anyone sets off.

I live towards Bauska. Do you travel there too?

I have a pouffe and three chairs at home. Is that enough?
