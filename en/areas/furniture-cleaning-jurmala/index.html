Historic wooden buildings and sea air

Jūrmala has historic wooden buildings, private houses, apartment buildings, hotels and properties used seasonally. In a wooden summer house, air and moisture behave differently from a new masonry house, and drying time is set by the weather, ventilation and filling, not by a promise from us.

If the house has not been heated in winter, the fabric may have a musty smell in spring. First air the house and see whether the smell goes. If it stays, send a photo. When the cause is damp in the room, cleaning does not solve it, and the smell may return. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) calls air humidity of 40–60% comfortable, and humidity can be measured with a hygrometer.

Sand is another feature of the coast. Before cleaning, it is better vacuumed out of the seams and from under the cushions, like hair and crumbs.

Seasonal lets and guest houses

Jūrmala is Latvia's largest resort town, and in the Soviet period sanatoriums and holiday homes were built in all its parts. Today, flats and houses are also let for the summer. Between summer guests and before the season there is time for furniture to dry in peace.

In a short-let flat, a sofa usually suffers from the same things as in a hotel: many different people, wine and greasy stains on the fabric. A greasy stain needs a different approach from wine, and the result depends on the fabric. More: rental flat furniture cleaning, hotel furniture cleaning in Jūrmala and hotel and hostel mattress cleaning.

For a business the unit prices are the same, but for larger volumes we agree the price separately. On the form, give the number of rooms, the mattress sizes and the times when the rooms are free. If a guest has noticed bedbugs, that is a job for pest control, not cleaning.

A family flat in the west of the town

The western part of Jūrmala has apartment buildings, and the questions there are the same as in flats elsewhere: one sofa that serves both day and night, and a mattress that has to be cleaned on site. A two-seater sofa from €35 does not yet reach the minimum order, but with two dining chairs it starts from €49. See sofa cleaning in Jūrmala and sofa bed cleaning.

Rehabilitation centres and clinics

Health and rehabilitation services are part of daily life in Jūrmala. In a waiting room or patient room, upholstered furniture cleaning is the same work as at home, only with a stricter schedule. More: clinic furniture cleaning and waiting room chair cleaning. We do not say that cleaning disinfects or meets hygiene requirements: those are separate matters, and they are set by the institution's rules.

Summer parking and access

In the summer season, traffic and parking near the beach and in the central parts differ from winter. On the form, say whether a car can stop by the house, whether the gate has a code, and whether there is a side of the yard you have to come in from. If the building is of cultural and historic value and the stairs are narrow, that does not change the cleaning, because the furniture stays where it is.

What you can order in Jūrmala and what may stay

Sofas, mattresses, chairs, armchairs, headboards and terrace cushions are available. A corner sofa starts from €50, a leather corner sofa from €50, a double mattress from €45 (one side). All prices: prices. The price is affected by size, fabric, dirt, smell and distance.

A greasy stain that has soaked into the fabric, old wine stains, smell in the filling and sun-faded fabric may stay. If something like that shows in the photo, the specialist will say so before the work.

Frequently asked questions

I do not live in Bulduri or Sloka. Will travel be included?

That depends on the address. Write the street or nearest station, and we will tell you exactly before the work.

The summer house has been closed up all winter, and the mattress smells like a cellar. Can that be fixed?

First air and dry the room. If the smell stays, send a photo. If the mattress has mould or is damp inside, it is usually more honest to replace it.

Can you sort out guest house furniture between guests?

That depends on the time between guests, because drying cannot be promised exactly. Say on the form how long the gap is, and the specialist will tell you what can be arranged.

Does sea air harm a leather sofa?

We have no verified data on that. Direct heat and direct contact with water harm leather, and above 65% humidity there is a mould risk, so air the house if it stands empty.
