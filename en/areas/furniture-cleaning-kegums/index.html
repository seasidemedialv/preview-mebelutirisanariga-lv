Ķegums is a small town on the Daugava, and the trip here costs money, so the first question is simple: is this sofa worth cleaning, or is it better to replace it?

Clean or replace: an honest assessment

Ķegums began to form as a settlement in 1936 with the building of the hydroelectric station, and the first houses were built for the power station's workers. The town has private houses, apartment buildings and wooded outlying areas. Homes are of different ages, and so is the furniture. An old sofa has to be judged by its condition, not its age.

  • Cleaning makes sense if the frame is sturdy, the fabric sound, and the trouble is dirt, stains or a slight smell.
  • Think about replacing it if the foam has crumbled, the fabric is tearing, springs can be felt through the seat, or there is mould in the filling.
  • Between the two are cases where only the specialist can tell from a photo what to expect: for example, urine that has reached the foam, or an old grease stain.

The specialist tells you before the work what you can expect, and we start when you agree.

Travel to Ķegums and how to spread the cost

By road it is about 30 km to the Riga boundary, so travel is from €10. The minimum order is €45. That is exactly why one chair from €20 as a separate order is not good value.

Walk round the house and see what else needs cleaning. A three-seater sofa from €45 reaches the minimum on its own. A two-seater sofa from €35 with two armchairs starts from €75, and a double mattress from €45 with a headboard from €35 starts from €80.

A flat and a sofa that doubles as a bed

In an apartment building with small rooms, a sofa bed may work as both a seat and a bed. It costs from €40. Nobody sees the pull-out part during the day, so it has to be shown separately in the photo: sofa bed cleaning in Ķegums.

The furniture does not have to be carried down the stairs, because the work is done in the flat. All that is needed is space around the sofa and fresh air after the work. Drying depends on air humidity, ventilation, temperature, fabric and filling, so think about where to sleep while the sofa is still damp.

A house by the forest and a smell that comes from damp

In a private house by a forest or water, the building itself sets the moisture level. If furniture stands in a room that is rarely heated, a musty smell may soak into the fabric. Cleaning helps the fabric, but it cannot change what happens to the air in the room.

A simple test: air and heat the room properly for a few days. If the smell lessens, the cause is the room. If it stays and patches are visible on the fabric, send a close-up photo. Mould in the filling usually means replacement, because a porous material may be impossible to clean fully. Read also why a sofa smells and what to do.

A room with a stove and a smell of smoke

If someone in the house smokes, tobacco smoke residue settles in the fabric. In a study it was still present in cotton and polyester after 19 months, so the promise “the smell will disappear” would not be honest. It can usually be reduced. We have no such study on stove smoke, so we do not promise a result there either. Before you order, read cigarette smoke smell in a sofa.

Frequently asked questions

My sofa is 20 years old. Does anyone clean those at all?

Age is not the main thing. What matters is whether the fabric and foam are sound. Send a photo of the seat and back, and the specialist will tell you whether it is worth it.

There is only one mattress in the house. Is it worth ordering?

A single mattress from €30 is below the €45 minimum, and travel comes on top. If there is another piece of furniture, sort it out in the same visit.

The fabric on the seat is torn, but the rest is clean. What should I do?

Cleaning does not repair a tear, and moisture does it harm. If the sofa is going to be reupholstered, there is no point in cleaning it. If not, send a photo.

Will cleaning remove a stove smoke smell?

We have no verified data on stove smoke. The smell can usually be reduced, but it may stay in the filling, so we make no promises.
