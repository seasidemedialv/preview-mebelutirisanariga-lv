How to put together an order for a house

Before you book the specialist, it is worth making a list. In a house, everything soft counts: sofa, armchair, pouffes, headboard, mattresses, chairs around the table. The minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45. Jobs ordered for one visit are added together.

Children, dogs and the terrace

In a family home, a sofa wears differently from one in a showroom. The armrests take on what is on people's hands, and the seats what falls off the plate. An animal's favourite corner with its hair is a separate matter, and children's sofa cleaning in Ķekava has its own page.

A fresh stain has simple rules: blot gently with a clean cloth without rubbing, and do not use hot water or chlorine bleach. Then send a photo. A fresh stain usually comes out better than one that is years old.

Terrace cushions can be added to the same order. In summer they take in dust and rain, in winter they lie in the shed, and in spring they may have a musty smell. They have their own page: outdoor furniture cushion cleaning.

A mattress: one side or both

In a private house there may be more than one bed: a double in the bedroom, singles for the children, and a guest room mattress that is rarely used. The price is for one side: from €30 for a single, from €45 for a double, and the second side costs +50%. The second side is worth cleaning if you really do turn the mattress. If a mattress has only one sleeping side, there is usually no point in cleaning the other side.

In a spring mattress moisture may cause rust, and in a foam mattress it stays in the core, so a mattress must not be soaked. A coconut fibre mattress is not designed for moisture either. Before cleaning, check the manufacturer's instructions: for some materials, such as TEMPUR, the manufacturer rules out washing. A removable cover may be washed only if the label allows it. More: mattress cleaning at home in Ķekava and mattress cover cleaning.

Multi-storey blocks in the poultry factory settlement

Ķekava is not only private houses. After the poultry factory was built, a workers' settlement with multi-storey housing grew up, and in the flats an order is different: usually one sofa and one mattress in a smaller room.

In a flat, all the work is done on site, so stairs or a lift are no problem. If the flat tends to be damp, say so before cleaning: drying will be slower, and the window has to be kept open. One two-seater sofa from €35 still needs something added to reach €45. More: furniture cleaning in a flat in Ķekava.

The car by the house

In the suburbs, a child car seat and the back seat may be just as dirty as the sofa. If the car is parked in the yard, its seats can be added to the order too: car seat cleaning in Ķekava from €12 per seat, child car seat from €20. Child car seat straps and buckles are looked after only as the instructions say. If you want the whole interior cleaned, that is not our work: we clean seats.

Where Ķekava ends

This page is about the town of Ķekava, where travel is included: it is about 10 km from the Riga boundary. Ķekava municipality also includes Baloži, where travel is likewise included, and Baldone, where it is from €10. In the rural parts of the municipality, write the nearest village, and we tell you the travel charge before the work.

On the form, say when you are at home and how to get there, if the house is in a new development with unmarked streets.

What may stay

Old stains, ink, grease that has soaked in, and urine smell if the liquid has reached the foam may stay. Cleaning does not remove wear or faded fabric. A photo usually lets us say this in advance, and we agree the price before the work. See the full price list on the prices page.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to order everything at once?

No, but it is more convenient: the minimum order is €45 per visit, so several small jobs together work out better value than separately.

I live in the municipality, outside the town of Ķekava. Is travel included?

If the address is within the limit of about 15 km outside Riga, it is included; further out it is from €10. Write the address on the form, and we will tell you before the work.

We have a U-shaped sofa in a light fabric. How much does that cost?

A U-shaped sofa starts from €65, and with light fabric the price depends on how dirty it is. Send photos of all the parts.

Can cleaning be done while the children are at home?

It can be, but a damp sofa should not be used for a while, so plan a time when the room is free.
