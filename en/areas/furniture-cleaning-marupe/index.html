Mārupe borders Riga, so travel is included, and the only question is what to sort out and when.

A terraced house: three floors, narrow stairs

Mārupe is known for dense private housing and new villages. In a terraced house, the rooms may be on several floors, and the stairs are narrow. This is exactly why cleaning at your home is convenient: a corner sofa does not have to be hauled down the stairs, and a mattress on the third floor stays on the third floor.

There is no need to mention the floor in your request, but it helps to know what stands where. Photograph each piece, and if the sofa's sections come apart, show those too. In the dining room, chairs with an upholstered seat start from €7 each, and six chairs with a pouffe exceed the €45 minimum.

Important: furniture needs a dry, ventilated room. In a terraced house with no through draught, open windows on two sides if the weather allows, and do not put a heater by leather furniture.

The children's room and animals

In the new developments, homes may have children's rooms where the sofa and mattress go through a lot. A child's urine on a mattress is a common case, and what to do is simple: blot gently with a clean cloth without rubbing, do not soak it, and send a photo. If the liquid has gone down to the foam, the smell may stay. Read child urine on a mattress and children's mattress cleaning.

We make no promises about children's health. After cleaning, furniture has to be aired and left to dry, and the care label and the specialist will tell you what can be expected from a particular fabric.

If allergies are the concern, remember: cleaning is not treatment, and dust mites come back if the air in the house is damp. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) links this to room humidity, and the solution is a set of several measures.

A leather sofa, faux leather and velour in a new interior

Each material needs a different approach. A leather sofa usually suffers from direct water and heat, and leather absorbs stains readily. Rubbing it with an unknown cream can do more harm than good, so wait until the specialist has seen the photo. A two-seater leather sofa starts from €40, a leather corner sofa from €50. More: leather sofa cleaning in Mārupe.

Faux leather looks similar but is a different material: its coating may peel over time, and cleaning does not repair that. Velour, for its part, is sensitive to water and pressure, and pressure marks are a property of the fabric. There are no separate prices for faux leather and velour; the fabric sofa prices apply. Photos are best taken with daylight from the side, so that marks and stains show, not just the colour.

Tīraine and apartment buildings

There are apartment buildings on the boundary with Tīraine too. In newer developments with small rooms, one piece of furniture may work in two ways: a sofa bed costs from €40, and chairs can be added to reach the minimum order. If the flat is let, see rental flat furniture cleaning.

Offices, warehouses and the nearby airport

Mārupe has offices, warehouses and logistics sites, and the airport creates its own working rhythm. The seats and armrests of office chairs collect dirt for years, and replacing a whole set may cost more than sorting it out.

  • Office chair cleaning in Mārupe: from €10 each.
  • Office sofa cleaning: the same sofa prices.
  • Hotel furniture: for larger volumes we agree the price separately.
  • Office furniture cleaning: for the whole office together.

Warehouse sites and offices near the airport may have their own access rules. On the form, write whether a pass is needed, who will meet the specialist, and when the premises are empty.

Access and addresses in the municipality

In Mārupe municipality, dense housing blocks give way to villages and rural land, so an address in the same municipality does not always mean the same access conditions. Streets in the new areas may be narrower and have different surfaces. On the form, say whether a car can stop by the house.

Frequently asked questions

My sofa is modular, and the sections come apart. Is the price different?

The price depends on the overall size and shape: corner sofa from €50, large corner from €60, U-shaped from €65. A photo from above helps.

I live in a terraced house with a shared driveway. Where should the car go?

Write that on the form: whether the driveway can be used for a while, or whether it is better to park on the street.

The office has 40 chairs. Is there a discount?

For larger volumes we agree the price according to the number and condition. We do not name a discount percentage in advance. Send photos and the number, and the specialist will tell you the price.

The sofa is new, but a child has spilled juice on it. What should I do now?

Blot gently with a clean cloth without rubbing, and do not use hot water. Then send a photo and say when it happened.
