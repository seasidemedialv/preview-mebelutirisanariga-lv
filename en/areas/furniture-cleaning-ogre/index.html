An estate flat and a house with a garden are two different jobs, and that decides what is worth sorting out, and how much, in one visit. Ogre is about 21 km from the Riga boundary, so travel costs from €10.

Jaunogre: a flat, a sofa, one room

About two thirds of Ogre's residents live in the Jaunogre housing estate, so furniture in flats is the main question here. In a room that serves both day and night, the sofa bed is also the bed. There is little space around it, so before the specialist arrives take the blankets off, pull it out if that is easy, and leave free access.

Stairs and the size of the lift change nothing. The furniture stays in the flat, and the work is done at your home. A sofa bed costs from €40, which is below the €45 minimum order. The cheapest way to reach it is to add a pouffe, a chair or a few removable cushions. More: sofa bed cleaning in Ogre.

If the flat is let, it is better to sort out the furniture between tenants, when the room is empty. Then it can dry without hurry, and the bed does not have to be moved to another room. See rental flat furniture cleaning.

The summer house legacy and a house along the river

In 1875 intensive summer house building began in Ogre, and by the First World War there were about 300 of them. Today the town has a historic centre, areas of private houses and wooded terrain. In an old wooden house with stove heating, furniture lives in different conditions from those in a flat with central heating.

In a room that is heated only now and then, air humidity may rise. If it stays above 80% for a long time, the Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) calls this a high mould risk, and then the musty smell in the sofa comes from the air, not from the sofa itself. Before cleaning, sort out the ventilation and heating, or the smell may return.

After cleaning, do not push the sofa right up to the stove. Fabric needs air, not heat, and heat directly harms leather.

A light sofa and a pet in a flat

In a Jaunogre flat, the sofa is also where the cat sleeps. Hair is usually the least of the worries. If the cat has urinated on the sofa, the area of smell may be larger than the stain, and if the liquid has reached the foam, the smell may return. In repeated cases, talk to a vet first. More: urine sofa cleaning in Ogre and cat urine smell in furniture.

Ogre as a service centre

Ogre is the municipality's service and trade centre, and it also has offices, salons and cafés. If a business needs its chairs sorted out, the unit prices are the same as for a family, and for larger volumes we agree the price separately. Start with the pages on café chair cleaning or beauty salon furniture. On the form, write when the premises are free, because furniture needs time to dry before visitors sit on it.

A town with a river, a railway and crossings

The Daugava, the Ogre river, the railway and the A6 set the practical routes. On the form, write the street and, if you know it, which side of the railway you are on. If the house is in the wooded part, mention the way in and where to park.

Ikšķile and Ogre are in the same municipality, but the rules differ: in Ikšķile travel is included, in Ogre it starts from €10, and in Ķegums likewise from €10.

What sofa cleaning in Ogre does not solve

Usually comes out

  • Dirt on seats and armrests often becomes less visible.
  • Fresher stains usually come out better than old ones.
  • A slight household smell can be reduced.

May stay

  • Old urine smell in the foam may stay or return.
  • Smoke smell may come back from the filling.
  • Mould inside a sofa is not a cleaning job.

If a stain is fresh, blot it gently with a clean cloth without rubbing, and do not use hot water. More on what can and cannot be expected: what may stay after cleaning.

Frequently asked questions

I live in Jaunogre on the fifth floor with no lift. Does the sofa have to be carried down?

No. The sofa stays in the room, and the work is done there.

The house has stove heating. When is it better to clean: autumn or spring?

It is better when the room can be both aired and heated. Drying depends on air humidity and temperature, so in a damp, cold room it drags on.

Is the €10 for travel a fixed amount?

It starts from €10. Based on the address, the specialist will tell you the exact amount before the work.

Will cleaning help against allergies?

Cleaning is not treatment, and we do not promise that. For dust mites, several measures together help, including lowering the room's humidity.
