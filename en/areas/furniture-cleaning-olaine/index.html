Olaine is a planned industrial and residential town with a large stock of apartment buildings, so the main focus of this page is one room and one sofa. Travel to Olaine is included.

An apartment building from the sixties

From 1962 to 1965, industrial sites and residential buildings went up fast in Olaine. This says nothing about your particular building, but a sofa in a flat like this has usually been chosen for whether it fits in the room.

An older sofa in a small room is no problem for cleaning. The only thing that helps is space around it: take off the blankets and cushions and, if it is easy, move it away from the wall. The furniture does not have to be carried down the stairs, and no lift is needed. If one room is also the bedroom, the sofa bed is in use every day, and dirt from both day and night builds up on it. See sofa bed cleaning in Olaine and furniture cleaning in a flat.

If the flat tends to be damp, that changes one thing: furniture dries more slowly. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) recommends airing for at least 15 minutes in the mornings and, if possible, drying laundry outdoors. After cleaning, keep a window open as far as the weather allows, and do not leave the sofa pressed against an outer wall.

The tenant moves out, the landlord waits

In Olaine some flats may be let to working people, and between tenants the landlord needs to know whether the sofa can be sorted out or a new one has to be bought. Send a photo of the stains, and the specialist will tell you what you can expect. More: furniture cleaning in a rental flat after tenants in Olaine.

If someone has smoked in the flat, bear in mind that cleaning may reduce the smell but not always remove it, because it lingers in the filling. If bedbugs have been noticed, that is a job for pest control, and furniture cleaning will not help here.

Workplaces: chairs, break rooms, the office

Olaine has production, warehouse and railway sites, and they have offices, break rooms and meeting rooms. We clean only upholstered furniture, such as chairs and sofas, not production premises.

  • Office chair cleaning in Olaine: from €10.
  • Office sofa cleaning: the same sofa prices.
  • Waiting room chairs: price by volume.

An industrial site may have its own access rules, which differ from those of residential yards. On the form, write whether a pass is needed, the entrance and a contact person, and for larger volumes we will agree the price separately.

A sofa from a second-hand advert

A sofa for a flat may be bought second-hand. Before it comes into the room, at least assess it: look under the cushions, smell the filling, check the seams. If you suspect bedbugs, that is not a cleaning job, and it is better not to take such a sofa. If it is simply dirty, it is an ordinary job: second-hand sofa cleaning in Olaine.

It is best done before the sofa is placed in the room: free space around the furniture makes the work easier.

A mattress in Olaine

A mattress is cleaned on site, and it must not be soaked, because foam holds moisture for a long time. One side starts from €30 for a single and from €45 for a double, and the second side is +50%. If a mattress is damp inside or smells of mould, it is usually better to replace it. See mattress cleaning at home.

Travel and what may stay

In Olaine, travel is included. The town centre is about 14 km from the Riga boundary, close to the 15 km limit, so for addresses further out in the municipality, towards the forests and bogs, travel may be from €10. We tell you this before the work. The way from Riga runs along the A8, and Mārupe and Jelgava are nearby.

Old stains, smell in the filling and worn fabric may stay. All prices are on the prices page.

Frequently asked questions

Can I order just the seat cushions, without the sofa?

You can; a removable cushion costs from €2. The minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45. It is usually better value to add the whole sofa or another piece of furniture.

Does the sofa have to be moved away from the wall before the work?

If that can be done easily, it helps. If the sofa is heavy, leave it where it is and say so on the form.

The house has a damp cellar, and the sofa smells. Is it from the cellar?

That is impossible to tell from a distance. Send photos of the back and underside of the sofa. If the cause is damp in the room, cleaning will not solve it, and the smell may return.

Can you come to a factory site?

Write the company's name and rules on the form, and the specialist will tell you whether it is possible.
