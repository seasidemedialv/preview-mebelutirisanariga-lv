In this neighbourhood, wooden houses, pre-war masonry buildings and a post-war housing estate stand side by side, and the work looks slightly different in each. Send a photo, and the specialist will tell you the price and what you can expect from that particular stain.

Two very different Āgenskalns homes

The smaller streets of Āgenskalns are dominated by low-rise apartment buildings with fairly large green gardens. In the northern part, Riga's first housing estate, Āgenskalna priedes, was built in the 1950s and 1960s. The neighbourhood also has pre-war masonry buildings, public institutions and a market. This is not just architecture: the house decides how much space there is around the sofa, how dry the air in the room is, and how long the furniture will need airing.

In a wooden house, upholstered furniture may stand by an outer wall or by a stove, if the flat still has one. In the Priedes, the small room is often the bedroom too, so the sofa bed is in use every day. In both cases the floor and the lift do not matter, because the furniture stays where it is.

A high room, a cool stairwell and drying

Fabric and foam stay damp for some time after cleaning. How long depends on air humidity, ventilation, room temperature, fabric and filling, so we do not promise a number of hours. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) considers 40–60% relative air humidity comfortable, and in a wooden house in autumn it may be higher.

What you can do: after the work, open a window if the weather allows, and keep the door to the next room open so that the air moves. If you heat the room with a stove, do not push the furniture up against it. Direct heat dries out and deforms leather, and it does not suit a latex mattress either.

Older furniture: start with the label

The care label is often hidden under the seat cushions or by the base of the furniture. It may carry a letter code, which sets what the fabric may be cleaned with at all:

  • W: water-based cleaning, sparingly, without soaking the fabric;
  • S: only a water-free method in a well-ventilated room;
  • WS: both methods are suitable;
  • X: no liquid at all, only vacuuming and light brushing; any other cleaning has to be assessed separately.

An older armchair or a sofa inherited in a pre-war flat may have no label at all, because these codes are voluntary. In that case, take a close-up photo of the fabric and one of the whole piece, and the specialist will tell you what you can expect. Not every old fabric is worth wetting: the colour may run, and the fabric may shrink.

What may stay

We do not promise that everything will disappear. An old grease stain on the backrest, a water ring in the fabric and old urine in the filling may stay or only get paler. Pressure marks on velour are a property of the fabric, not a stain. If furniture has stood for a long time in a damp corner and the smell comes from the foam, cleaning may not change that, and the cause of the damp has to be dealt with first.

Frequently asked questions

My sofa stands next to the stove. Can I light it after cleaning?

You can, just do not dry the furniture with the stove. Leave a gap and air the room. The specialist will tell you what to bear in mind with your fabric.

Can I take off the covers and wash them to make it cheaper?

Usually this should not be done. A cover taken off and washed separately may shrink or change colour, and then it will no longer match the rest of the sofa. First look at the care label.

The chair is inherited, there is no label, and the fabric is thin. Can it be cleaned?

Possibly, but without a label it has to be assessed. On thin or old fabric, water may leave a ring or change the colour. Send a close-up photo and one of the side of the furniture, and the specialist will tell you whether it makes sense to take it on in this case.

The sofa has stood by an outer wall for years, and the back smells of damp. What should I do?

First move it away from the wall and see whether there is mould on the back. If there is, send a photo. The fabric can be cleaned, but if there is mould in the foam, the furniture usually has to be replaced, and the cause of the damp in the wall has to be dealt with too.
