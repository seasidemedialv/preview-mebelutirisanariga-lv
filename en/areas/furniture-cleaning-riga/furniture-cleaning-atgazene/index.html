On one side are private houses and low-rise buildings with gardens, on the other, along Vienības gatve, offices and shopping centres. For a home the sofa matters; for a business it is the number of chairs and when the staff are out of the room.

An office or shop by Vienības gatve

Along Vienības gatve and in the central and southern parts of the neighbourhood there are shopping centres, offices and housing, and car repair shops nearby. In an office, furniture wears differently from at home: a chair's seat and armrests go dark from clothes and hands, not from any single stain. In a break room, the sofa usually has coffee marks.

For a business, the price is built from the same unit prices, but the price for larger volumes is by agreement. Write how many chairs there are, and add a photo of one typical chair and the worst one. Office chair cleaning and office sofa cleaning are described separately, as are waiting room chairs for a car repair shop or clinic.

Atgāzene houses and gardens

In the private and low-rise housing there is usually one piece of furniture for each need: the big sofa in the living room, chairs at the table, a mattress upstairs. Dog owners know that from time to time a sofa needs a more serious clean to get the hair out than a vacuum cleaner can give.

The work is done right there in the house. It helps to have free access to the furniture and a room that can be aired afterwards. If the house is by the railway and you do not keep the windows open, shorter but more frequent opening is enough for airing. Moisture stays in furniture longer if the air does not move, so leave the door open.

How to prepare a request for a business

For a larger order a couple of facts are enough, and a rough price can be given from a photo. Write them in one message:

  • how many chairs, sofas or pouffes, and what their finish is: fabric, faux leather or leather;
  • photos of a typical piece and the worst one;
  • which floor the premises are on and whether people work in them;
  • when the premises are empty and how long they can be aired;
  • whether the work is needed once or repeatedly.

The unit prices stay the same, but for larger quantities we agree the amount separately. We do not name discount figures in advance, because they depend on finish and dirt. For retail premises, office furniture cleaning also applies, and for food businesses there is a separate page on restaurant furniture.

An honest word on what stays

The dark patch on an office chair seat may get lighter, but that will not make the chair new. A faux leather coating may peel over time, and cleaning cannot repair that. An ink stain on light fabric may stay. The specialist will tell you this before the work, after seeing the photo.

A fresh stain at home or in the office: the first minutes

Coffee on a break room sofa or wine on a living room chair is a stain that can be reduced if you act straight away. Blot gently with a clean cloth dampened in cold or lukewarm water, without rubbing. Do not use hot water, as it may set the stain. Chlorine bleach may change the colour.

If a stain is small and fresh, that is often enough. If it is old or large, or the liquid has reached the upholstery, send a photo. Do not soak the fabric: liquid that gets into the filling stays there and may cause a smell later. More in the article stains on a sofa.

Apartment buildings in the neighbourhood

Atgāzene also has a few apartment buildings. The work there looks different from an office or private house: one or two pieces, a small room and a sofa standing by the wall. The floor and the lift make no difference, because the furniture stays in the flat. It is enough to clear access and write in the form how many seats the sofa has. If it is pulled out at night, mention that too. More: furniture cleaning in a flat.

Frequently asked questions

Can office chairs be cleaned outside working hours?

Write in the form when the premises are free, and the specialist will tell you whether it is possible. We need to see the volume first.

The chairs in a car repair shop waiting room are oily. Can they be cleaned?

Stains can usually be reduced, but oil and grease in fabric are a hard case, and they may stay. Send a close-up photo so that the specialist can assess it.

We have just one sofa and a pouffe. Will that reach the minimum?

A two-seater sofa from €35 plus a pouffe from €10 makes exactly €45, if the dirt does not push up the cost of the work. The specialist will tell you the exact price from a photo.

A stain on a faux leather chair will not come off. Can it be removed?

A lot depends on what it is and how old it is. If the coating has started to flake, cleaning will not restore it. A close-up photo will tell us more.
