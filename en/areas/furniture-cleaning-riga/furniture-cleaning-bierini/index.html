We clean on the spot: the sofa stays in the living room, the mattress on the bed, the car in the yard. A photo in the form lets the specialist tell you the price before the trip.

A private house with a garden, and furniture shared with an animal

The character of Bieriņi is private houses and gardens, and planning documents aim to keep it that way. In a house with a garden, the cat and dog go out and come in, so the sofa gets what the animal brings in. Hair and sand can usually be removed. Urine is harder.

The area of smell may be up to three times larger than the visible stain. International Cat Care states that heavily soaked material stays attractive to a cat even after cleaning. So we will be honest: if urine has reached the foam, the smell may stay or return. It depends on how deep the liquid has gone. Smell treatment costs extra, from €15.

If a cat marks furniture repeatedly, see a vet first. Veterinary sources treat it as a matter of behaviour or health. More on this work: cleaning urine from a sofa and cat urine smell in furniture.

The car is in the yard, so the seats can be done too

At a private house the car is usually parked on your own land, and that makes it possible to combine seats with furniture. Car seat cleaning costs from €12 per seat. We clean seats, not the whole interior. Four seats start from €48, and that reaches the minimum order at the same time.

If the seats are leather, see the page leather car seat cleaning. Liquid and heat do leather no good, so it is handled differently from fabric.

The bedroom mattress and headboard

In a private house the bedroom is often upstairs, and nobody wants to carry a double mattress down the stairs. Double mattress cleaning takes place right there, and one side costs from €45. If you need both sides, the second costs +50%. A headboard costs from €35, and it is worth looking at in the same visit.

Before sending a request, look at the mattress label. TEMPUR states that its material cannot be washed, sponged or dry cleaned, so for such a mattress the manufacturer's care instructions have to be read first. Direct heat and sun do not suit a latex mattress. If you are not sure what is inside, send a photo of the label.

Terrace furniture after the season

A house with a garden may have a terrace. The cushions there get rain, dust and sometimes the cat's attention too. Outdoor furniture cushion cleaning is simpler if you gather the cushions in one place indoors and they are no longer wet. A removable cushion costs from €2.

Before winter it is better to dry the cushions and store them in a dry place. Do not put a damp cushion in a closed box, because porous material that stays damp for a long time is a good environment for mould. If mould is already visible in the filling, the cushion usually has to be replaced, not cleaned.

A child's place in the living room

In a family home, the sofa is also for playing on, and juice and paint end up on it. The same goes for the dining chairs where a child sits. These cases have a separate page: children's sofa cleaning.

What to check before sending a request

A few things are worth looking at before you send the form, because they change the answer:

  • Furniture in a cellar or garage. If it has stood there a long time, first bring it into a dry room and see whether there is mould.
  • A light sofa by the big window. What you take for a stain may be pressure marks in velour, and those are a property of the fabric.
  • A sofa from your previous home. If it was bought second-hand, first check it for bedbugs. That is a job for pest control, not cleaning.
  • A small stain. If it is small and fresh, first aid given straight away is often enough, and no order is needed.

What may stay

We do not promise to clean everything. An old grease stain on the backrest and urine in the filling may stay. Velour may look different where it has been sat on for a long time. The more photos you send, the more precisely the specialist will tell you what to expect.

Frequently asked questions

The cat has urinated on the sofa. What should we do while we wait?

Blot gently with a clean cloth without rubbing, and do not use hot water. Do not use cleaning products containing ammonia, because to a cat they smell of urine. Then send a photo.

At the end of the season, can you clean the garden swing too?

The upholstered cushions, yes, if they are removable. Send a photo, and the specialist will tell you.

The sofa smells like a cellar. Why?

The cause may be damp in the room, not the sofa. First see what the air humidity in the room is and whether there is mould on the back of the sofa. The smell in the fabric can be reduced, but the cause has to be dealt with separately.

Someone in the house has an allergy. Will cleaning help?

Cleaning does not treat allergies, and we cannot promise that. Talk to a doctor before sending a request. For mites, the room's air humidity matters more: keeping it below 50% is recommended.
