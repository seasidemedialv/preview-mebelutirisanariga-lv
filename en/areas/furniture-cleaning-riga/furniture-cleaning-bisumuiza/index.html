Furniture cleaning in Bišumuiža - for homes, allotments and workplaces

Furniture cleaning in Bišumuiža can mean very different jobs, because on the left bank of the Daugava private houses, a few apartment buildings, warehouses and family allotments sit side by side.

A sofa in a flat, chairs in a warehouse office and a mattress in a garden house are three different tasks. Send a photo of what needs cleaning, and the specialist will tell you the price.

The allotment garden house after winter

The land around the Bišumuiža ditch is mostly used as family allotments. A garden house usually stands unheated in winter, and a sofa or mattress left there may smell of damp in spring. That does not yet mean the furniture is finished: first look at it in daylight and smell the fabric, not just the room.

If the smell is on the surface, cleaning usually reduces it. If you can see marks on the fabric or foam, or the smell comes from deep inside, that is a different matter. Porous material with mould may not clean out fully, and then the furniture has to be replaced. Read mould in a sofa and mould in a mattress first.

In a garden house like this, it is worth planning the cleaning for when you can already air and heat the place. If the room is cold and closed up, the furniture will not dry after the work, and then there is no point in cleaning it.

Chairs and sofas in workplaces

Bišumuiža also has manufacturing, warehouses, logistics and other services, served by Bauskas iela and the nearby Dienvidu tilts. In places like these, the staff room sofa and the office chair work hard. An office chair costs from €10, a dining-type chair from €7, and for larger numbers we agree the price by volume.

Write how many chairs there are and whether they are finished in fabric or faux leather, and add a photo. More on office chair cleaning and furniture cleaning for property managers. Oil and machine grease that has reached the fabric from work clothes is a difficult case, and it may not be possible to remove it.

Flat or house: how to prepare

At home the job is simpler. Clear access to the sofa and take off whatever is on it. Nothing has to be carried out, because the cleaning is done on site. If the furniture stands in a far corner and cannot be reached, write that in the form.

Look for the care label under the seat cushions or near the base. If you see the letter X, the fabric is not meant for liquids, and any other cleaning has to be assessed separately. If you see W, S or WS, photograph it and add it to your request. That means fewer questions before the work.

Apartment building versus private house

The housing in Bišumuiža is not uniform. One address is a stand-alone apartment building, another a private house with a garden, and their needs differ. A flat usually has one or two pieces of upholstered furniture, and the €45 minimum may be reached only with a larger sofa or extra chairs. In a private house, the list usually reaches the minimum anyway.

Before you send a request, think about what else in the flat needs freshening up, such as an armchair, a pouffe from €10 or an upholstered headboard from €35. That way more gets cleaned on one trip, and the total reaches the minimum naturally, not through a surcharge.

Smoke in the staff room and used furniture

If people used to smoke in a company's staff room, the sofa remembers. In studies, smoke residue could still be found in cotton and polyester after 19 months, and in flats wiping and repainting gave only a short-lived result. The smell can be reduced, but we do not promise it will not come back from the filling. Smell treatment costs from €15 extra.

Much the same goes for a used sofa you bring to a garden house or warehouse office. Before you pay for cleaning, compare the cost with what the furniture is worth. If you see mould or traces of bedbugs, cleaning is not the right route. For everything else there is used sofa cleaning.

An honest word on the result

Grease, old smells from the filling, cigarette smoke smell and urine that has reached the foam may stay. We can usually reduce them, but we do not promise a complete result. Cleaning does not help foam filling damaged by damp.

Frequently asked questions

The garden house has no electricity or water. Is cleaning possible?

Write that in the form with a photo, and the specialist will tell you what can be done in these conditions.

How many chairs do I need for a price by volume?

The furniture is on a company's second floor with no lift. Will it have to be carried down?

The furniture is not carried anywhere: the work is done on site. Give the floor in the form so that the specialist knows where to go, and we agree the price before the work.

The sofa in the warehouse staff room is grey from work clothes. Is it worth it?
