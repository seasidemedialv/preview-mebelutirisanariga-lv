It may be the owners of a flat in an Art Nouveau building, an office with many chairs, a café or an aparthotel. The work is done on site, and the specialist will give the price from photos and the volume.

An Art Nouveau building and a flat in it

The buildings of Centrs stand out for the tenement houses and villas built by Baltic German and Latvian architects, and a particular feature is the density of Art Nouveau buildings. A flat in such a building may hold valuable upholstered furniture or inherited chairs. Before cleaning, it is important to know what the fabric is and whether it will hold up.

If the furniture is expensive or sentimentally important to you, write that in the form before the work. Such furniture usually has no care label, and then it has to be assessed carefully: on old fabric the colour may run, and the fabric may shrink. Cleaning is not restoration.

If the flat is in a courtyard wing or on an upper floor with no lift, the furniture stays where it is, so that changes nothing.

Parking in Centrs

Paid parking zones A, B and C operate in Centrs, so please say in the form where a car can be left near the building. Vehicle access here is often restricted and courtyard gates tend to be locked, so also write whether the entrance is from the street or the courtyard and whether a code is needed.

Offices: chairs, meeting rooms, the break corner

Centrs is a place of offices, hospitality and professional services, so the work here is often measured in chairs. Chairs and sofas need not be taken anywhere: the work is done in the room. An office chair costs from €10, and for a larger volume we agree the price. More: office chair cleaning, office sofa cleaning and conference room chairs.

To get a price, send: the number of chairs, the finish, a photo of one typical chair and of the worst one, and a time when the room is empty. After the work the room needs airing, so plan a time when nobody is working in it.

Hotels and apartments

In a hotel the most important question is room availability: a room where cleaning has taken place cannot be let to the next guest straight away. So write how many rooms there are, what furniture, and when the rooms are free. More: hotel furniture cleaning and hotel and hostel mattress cleaning.

In a short-let flat the concerns are similar, just on a smaller scale. A sofa and mattress may need cleaning after guests, and this work has its own page: cleaning furniture in a rental flat.

Bedbugs are not a cleaning job. If they are found in a room, that is a task for pest control, and cleaning does not replace it. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) has a register of service providers.

Cafés and restaurants

Upholstered chairs and benches in a café get food, coffee and greasy hands. A dining-type chair costs from €7, and for larger numbers the price is by volume. A grease stain is usually the hardest case: it can be reduced, but it may stay. Plan the cleaning for when the premises are closed, and leave time for airing. More: café chair cleaning and restaurant furniture cleaning.

A flat before selling or moving in

Flats in Centrs change owners, and then everything that has to be shown needs freshening up at once. If you are selling a flat with its furniture, a clean sofa looks better, but cleaning does not hide wear, and the buyer will see it. For furniture you sell separately, see sofa cleaning before selling. If you are moving into a flat that already has furniture, furniture cleaning before moving in will be useful.

What we cannot promise

We clean fabric, leather and upholstered furniture, not the room. Old smells, cigarette smoke and the smell of a smoker's flat may stay, because they are also in the walls and curtains. Worn fabric will stay worn. An old wine stain on light fabric may stay, even if it fades. The specialist will tell you this before the work, not after it.

Salons and clinics

In a beauty salon or clinic, chairs come into contact with many people, so the state of the surface is worth thinking about. We clean furniture, we do not disinfect it: hygiene requirements remain a matter for your institution's rules. More: beauty salon furniture cleaning.

Frequently asked questions

The office has 30 chairs. How do I get a price?

Write the number and the finish, and add two photos: the typical chair and the worst one. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes, and the specialist will give the price by volume.

Can you serve several apartments in one building?

Yes, if the total volume is clear. Write how many flats and what furniture, and whether they are free at the same time.

Does an Art Nouveau building with marble stairs need special preparation?

No, the furniture stays in the flat, and the stairs do not affect the work. It matters more to write how to get to the door and where a car can be left.

Can a leather chair be cleaned on the same visit as a fabric sofa?

Yes, they are just two different jobs with their own prices. Mention both in the form and add close-up photos.
