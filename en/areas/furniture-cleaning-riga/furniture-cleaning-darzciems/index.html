Upholstered furniture cleaning in Dārzciems - for private houses and multi-storey buildings

After the Second World War a large area of single-family houses was built here, and in the 1970s and 1980s multi-storey buildings joined them. Between Purvciems, Pļavnieki and Šķirotava there are also warehouses and car workshops, so customers vary.

The single-family house

In the post-war houses of Dārzciems, several generations may have left their furniture: a sofa from the parents, chairs from the grandparents, beds upstairs. Private housing takes up a substantial part of the neighbourhood, and a house like this may have more than one piece of furniture to clean.

List what is actually used. Furniture standing in an empty room need not be cleaned just because it is there. Send a photo of each piece, and the specialist will tell you the total price. If one sofa does not reach the minimum, add chairs or a mattress.

The work is done right there, so nothing has to be carried to the garage. More: furniture cleaning in a private house.

The multi-storey building

In flats built in the 1970s and 1980s, the sofa is usually the centre of the living room, and it often serves as a bed too. The lift and the floor do not affect the work, because the furniture stays in the flat. All that is needed is clear access to the sofa. More: furniture cleaning in a flat.

In a flat where furniture has been used for years, stains and a seat worn flat are typical. Cleaning freshens up the fabric, but it does not restore the shape of the filling. If a sofa is sagging from being slept on, it will stay that way, only clean. If it smells of cigarettes, the smell can be reduced, but we cannot promise to remove it.

Dining chairs and the family table

Everything that falls off the table lands on chairs with upholstered seats. A dining chair costs from €7, and six chairs start from €42, which is still short of the minimum. Seven chairs start from €49, or add removable cushions to the six. More: dining chair cleaning.

A mattress in a two-room flat and in a house

A mattress is heavy to carry, so cleaning on site makes sense in both a house and a flat. Mattress cleaning is done at your place. A single mattress costs from €30, a double from €45 per side. If you need both sides, the second is +50%.

A mattress is not a sofa: it cannot be dried properly if it is soaked through. Foam holds moisture, and in a spring mattress excess liquid may cause rust. So a mattress must not be soaked, and a deep stain may stay. If a mattress smells and cleaning does not solve the smell, it has to be replaced. More: why a mattress smells and what to do and how often to clean a mattress.

Warehouses and car workshops

Dārzciems also has retail, logistics and car workshops. Staff rooms, waiting rooms and offices have chairs and sofas that are used every day. Write how many there are and add a photo. An office chair costs from €10, and for a larger volume we agree the price. On customer waiting rooms: waiting room chairs.

If the car is at the door, the seats can be cleaned on the same visit: they cost from €12 each. We do not clean the whole car interior, only the seats. Car seat cleaning is done at your place.

Cats and dogs in a small space

In homes with pets, the sofa is where they spend much of their time. Hair can usually be removed, but urine is another matter. If a cat marks furniture repeatedly, see the vet first, or it may happen again. If urine has reached the foam, the smell may remain.

Before cleaning, say whether the stain is from a pet. That changes what you can expect. Smell treatment costs from €15 extra. More: dog urine smell in furniture.

A children's sofa and a teenager's room

A sofa in a children's room wears from games, school bags and juice stains. This is ordinary work, and the price of a small sofa starts from the two-seater price. For the fabric, what matters most is how long ago the stain appeared. More: children's sofa cleaning.

Prices, minimum and travel

A three-seater sofa from €45, a corner sofa from €50, a sofa bed from €40, a dining chair from €7, an armchair from €20. The minimum order is €45, and travel to Dārzciems is included. A sofa bed with two cushions does not yet reach the minimum, but with a third it does.

The price depends on size, fabric, dirt, smell and distance, and we agree it before the work. Full list: prices.

What may stay

Not every stain disappears. An old grease stain on the backrest, worn fabric, urine in the filling and cigarette smell may stay. Peeling on a faux leather sofa is not dirt, and cleaning will not repair it. If furniture has served its time, cleaning will not restore it.

Frequently asked questions

We live in a single-family house with an older sofa. Is it worth cleaning?

If the fabric is whole and the foam holds its shape, it is usually worth it. If the sofa is sagging or torn, cleaning will not restore it. Send a photo, and the specialist will tell you frankly.

The car workshop waiting room has leather chairs. Is the price different?

A leather armchair costs from €20, and for a larger volume we agree the price. Send a photo, and the specialist will tell you the total price.

Can we share an order with a neighbour?

Each address is a separate order with its own €45 minimum. If you would like both at the same time, write that in the form.

Can you drive up to a multi-storey building in Dārzciems?

Usually yes, but write which entrance it is and which street is the better approach, especially if the yard is closed off.
