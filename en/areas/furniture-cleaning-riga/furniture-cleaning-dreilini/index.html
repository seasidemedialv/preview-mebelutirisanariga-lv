Dreiliņi is a mixed neighbourhood: 602 series buildings, new developments, large shops and undeveloped land. So the first thing to establish is what furniture and what conditions you have. Send a photo, and the specialist will tell you the price.

Three different jobs in Dreiliņi

Construction of 602 series buildings began here around 1990 and stopped in 1993, leaving an unfinished complex of nine-storey buildings and undeveloped fields. New apartment developments came later. So the furniture cleaning task depends on the building, not on the name of the neighbourhood.

After building work and before moving in

Dreiliņi has development sites and busy roads, but in a new flat the source of dust may also be your own renovation. If you are moving, a sofa or mattress is easier to clean while the room is still empty and the furniture can be reached from all sides.

Write in the form that the furniture stood there during renovation and how long ago it ended, and add a photo. The specialist will tell you what to expect. More: furniture cleaning after renovation and furniture cleaning before moving in.

If the furniture was bought used, say so in the form, because an unknown history means a smell or stain may be older than it looks. We write about this on the page used sofa cleaning.

Mattress cleaning in Dreiliņi: what to say in the form

What matters most for a mattress is how it is built. Foam is sensitive to moisture, and in a spring mattress moisture may cause rust, so a mattress must not be soaked. Write the type of mattress in the form, if you know it, and the size. Also check whether the cover is removable and what the label says about it. If you have a TEMPUR mattress, first read the manufacturer's care instructions, because its material must not be washed.

Double mattress cleaning costs from €45 per side, with the second side at +50%. A single mattress is from €30, so on its own it does not reach the €45 minimum order. Add sofa cleaning in Dreiliņi or another mattress. More: mattress cleaning at home.

In Dreiliņi's private houses: more furniture, but one trip

Dreiliņi also has private houses, and there the task is different. A living room corner sofa from €50, two armchairs from €20 and a bedroom headboard from €35 in one request is simpler than three separate jobs, and it goes over the minimum order. Walk round the house and put everything in one form.

If the family has small children, you can also add a child car seat from €20 and a pram from €20. For the car seat's straps and buckle, follow the manufacturer's instructions.

Light fabric and a new sofa call for a different approach from an old one: a small stain is more visible, and too much water may leave a water ring. The page light and white sofa cleaning describes what to expect. If the sofa has removable covers, do not rush to wash them: a cover washed separately may shrink or change colour.

Frequently asked questions

In the new flat there is building dust on the sofa. What should I say in the form?

Write that it is building dust and add a close-up of the fabric. The specialist will tell you from a photo or on site what to expect.

Does a new sofa need cleaning at all?

If there are no stains or smell, usually not. If the sofa stood in the room during renovation or something was spilled on it, send a photo, and the specialist will tell you whether it is worthwhile.

Can I request cleaning for just a mattress?

Yes. The minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45. A single mattress is from €30, so you can add a second mattress, chairs or a sofa to the order. A double mattress, one side, already starts from €45.
