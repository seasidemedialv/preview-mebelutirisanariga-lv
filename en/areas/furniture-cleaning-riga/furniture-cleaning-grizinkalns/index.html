Old building, old sofa: what to bear in mind

Rapid development began here after the Mīlgrāvis railway opened in 1892, and Grīziņkalns is characterised by pre-war masonry and wooden tenement houses, yard buildings and renovated blocks. Rooms in such buildings may be high and the stairwells cold. So it is worth thinking here about air humidity and airing.

Older upholstered furniture may be inherited, bought used or reupholstered. Fabric wetted too much may shrink, lose colour or get water rings, and old furniture does not always still have a care label. The codes are voluntary anyway. Describe in the form what you know about the furniture and send a photo.

Stove, damp room and drying

If the flat is still heated by a stove, do not put the sofa next to it to dry faster. Heat dries out leather and makes it brittle, and it may set a fresh stain. It is better to leave the furniture at normal room temperature and make sure the air moves in the room.

In a room where the air is damper and airing is harder, furniture takes longer to dry. So think ahead about which day you can open the windows. We do not promise an exact time, because it also depends on the fabric and filling.

If the smell comes back after cleaning, the fault may lie not only in the sofa but also in the room. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) gives 40–60% relative humidity as comfortable. If there are mould marks on the wall, the cause has to be dealt with first.

Parking and what to write in the form

Paid parking zone C operates in Grīziņkalns, with zone D on some streets, and since 20.04.2026 there are more paid streets. So write in the form where a car can stop near your building.

  • Entrance from the street or through the yard? If it is a yard building, say how to reach it.
  • Which floor is the furniture on, and are the stairs accessible?
  • The code or bell at the gate, if there is one.
  • A photo of the whole sofa and a close-up of the problem spot.

Leather chairs, velour and a smoker's legacy

Older furniture may also be leather or velour. Heat harms leather: it becomes dry and brittle, so do not put it by the stove. On velour and chenille, pressure marks and differences in shade arise with use, and that is a property of the fabric, not damage. Cleaning usually does not remove them.

If furniture has stood for years in a flat where people smoked, smoke residue stays in the fabric and filling. Cotton holds it for an especially long time. The smell can be reduced, but it may come back from the filling. Say in the form how long people have smoked in the room, and the specialist will tell you what you can realistically expect. More: cigarette smoke smell in a sofa.

Renovated blocks and rental flats

Next to the old buildings, Grīziņkalns also has renovated blocks where a flat may have new furniture. If you let out a flat, it is important to clean between tenants when the rooms are free, not while someone is living in them. We write about this on the page cleaning furniture in a rental flat. If the sofa is new and light, even a small stain shows, so a photo is useful even if the problem seems small.

Cafés and small businesses

The neighbourhood also has creative businesses, places to eat and local commercial premises, and there upholstered chairs are used differently from those at home: many people, food and drink on the seat. Frequently asked questions

The sofa is very old and the fabric is thin. Can it be cleaned at all?

That depends on the fabric and the label. Send photos from several sides, and the specialist will tell you what can be done and what is worthwhile.

We found bedbugs in an old sofa. Will cleaning help?

No, that is a job for pest control, not furniture cleaning. The register of service providers is kept by the Health Inspectorate.

There is nowhere to leave a car in the yard. Is that a problem?

No, it just has to be known in advance. Write in the form where a car can stop. Grīziņkalns has paid zones, so it is better to agree the spot before the work.

The café is open every day. When can the chairs be cleaned?

Write the number of chairs in the form and when the room is free. The specialist will get in touch, and we will agree the time and price.
