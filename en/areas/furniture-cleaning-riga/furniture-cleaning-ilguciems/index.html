When the sofa is also the bed

If you sit, eat, watch television and sleep in one room, the sofa bed works round the clock. It has two surfaces: the one guests see, and the sleeping surface that is in contact with skin and bedding all night. So write in the form whether someone sleeps on the sofa every night, and photograph it pulled out as well.

Pulled out, it also shows the joins of the mechanism and cushions, where crumbs and dust collect. Sofa bed cleaning starts from €40, but the final price depends on size, fabric and dirt. If the sofa stands against the wall and cannot be pulled out fully, say so in advance.

If you sleep on the sofa, plan the cleaning for a day after which you can sleep elsewhere. We do not promise a drying time, because it depends on air humidity, airing, temperature, the fabric and the filling.

Airing a flat in a panel block after cleaning

In a panel block the state of the ventilation and the humidity differ from flat to flat, so general advice does not work here. There are only a few things you can observe. If the windows mist up in winter or dark marks appear in the corner of an outer wall, the air in the flat is damp, and upholstered furniture will dry more slowly.

The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) considers 40–60% relative humidity comfortable and recommends airing a bedroom every morning for at least 15 minutes. If the sofa stands against an outer wall, after cleaning you can move it away so that the air also moves behind it. A fan at home will help, but do not warm the furniture with a heater.

Cleaning does not remove the cause of the damp. If the sofa has gone mouldy on the wall side, the damp has to be dealt with first, and only then can the furniture be assessed. If the mould is in the filling, that is usually a case for replacement.

Mattress and chairs in the same request

If you also have a separate bed, mattress cleaning in Iļģuciems fits well into the same visit. A mattress must not be soaked: foam is sensitive to moisture, springs may rust, and moisture soaked deep into the filling may stay for a long time. Say what kind of mattress it is, and show the label if there is one.

Kitchen chairs with fabric seats cost from €7, an armchair from €20. If the flat is let, see the page on cleaning furniture in a rental flat after tenants. In the Grīva estate and elsewhere in the neighbourhood the rules are the same: the furniture stays in the flat.

The first minutes after a stain

In a small flat coffee, tea or juice easily ends up on the sofa, and a lot depends on the first minutes. Gently blot the liquid with a clean, absorbent cloth, without rubbing. Use only a little cold water, because a wet filling will be a bigger problem than the stain. Hot water may set the stain, and chlorine bleach should be avoided altogether. Then photograph the spot and send it to us. If the stain is small and fresh, that is often enough.

Not all of Iļģuciems is panel blocks

The neighbourhood also has fragments of pre-war buildings, private houses and industrial land. In a private house the task is broader, and the page furniture cleaning in a private house will be useful. In an older building with stove heating, do not put furniture by the stove: heat is no way to speed up drying, and it harms leather.

If you work in a manufacturing or warehouse area and grease or oil stains end up on the sofa at home, say so straight away. A greasy stain usually needs a different approach from coffee, and the right one depends on the fabric and the label.

What may stay

May stay: wear and seats worn down by sitting, which are not dirt, long-dried stains, cigarette smell from the filling, and urine, if it has reached the foam. The specialist will tell you before the work what to expect. More on the page what can be cleaned and what may stay.

Frequently asked questions

I live on the fifth floor with no lift. Will the sofa have to be carried down?

No. The furniture is not carried anywhere, and the work is done in the room. Write the floor in the form so that the specialist knows where to go.

The sofa stands against an outer wall and smells of damp. What should I do?

First move it away and look at the back and the wall. If you see marks, send a photo. Cleaning may reduce the smell in the fabric, but if the cause is a damp wall, it may come back.

Tea has spilled on the sleeping surface. What should I do straight away?

Gently blot the liquid with a clean cloth, without rubbing, and do not pour more water on it. A fresh stain usually responds better than a dried one, but the result depends on the fabric.

Can I request cleaning for just the sleeping part?

Depending on the sofa that may be possible, but the cleaned part may look different from the rest of the fabric. Send a photo of both sides, and the specialist will tell you what is worthwhile.
