An estate flat: a corner sofa in a small room

In a multi-storey building, a corner sofa may take up much of the room and serve as a place for guests, a children's playground and a bed. Crumbs collect in the corner, and the front edge of the seats is the first to darken. Corner sofa cleaning costs from €50, a large corner sofa from €60, a U-shaped sofa from €65.

If the sofa reaches the wall on both sides, note that in the form. Removable cushions make access easier, but do not wash the covers separately before the work: they may shrink or change colour. A removable cushion costs from €2, if only that needs cleaning.

The furniture is not taken away, so the lift and the stairs do not matter. What matters more is how well the room airs afterwards. Choose a time when the windows can easily be kept open.

Private house areas: more than one sofa

In a private house one request can cover the whole house. The total quickly goes over the €45 minimum order: a corner sofa from €50 or a three-seater from €45 is enough, and mattress cleaning in Imanta and an armchair can be added.

If there is a dog or cat in the house, say in the form what the pet has done to the furniture. Hair and sand are one task, urine is another, because the smell area may be larger than the visible stain. At the end of summer you can add outdoor furniture cushions and car seats, priced from €12 per seat.

New developments and furniture before moving in

In the new developments the sofa may be new, but the problem is a different one: light fabric, a first stain, or furniture that stood in the room during renovation. Gently blot a fresh stain with a clean cloth, without rubbing, and do not use hot water or bleach. Then send a photo with the label.

If you are moving into a home where the previous owner's furniture stays, see the page furniture cleaning before moving in. This is easier to do while the rooms are still empty.

How to reach the address

Imanta has wide inner courtyards, and the railway affects routes. Write in the form not just the address, but also which side is easier to drive up from and which entrance to use. That saves needless searching.

  • Exact address, entrance number and floor.
  • Where a car can usually stop near the building.
  • Stairwell code, if one is needed.
  • A photo of the whole piece and a separate one of the stain.

Leather sofa and armchair

Leather needs a different approach from fabric. Direct water may damage it, heat dries it out and makes it brittle, and a stain soaks into the leather itself. So what suits fabric does not suit a leather sofa. A leather two-seater sofa costs from €40, a leather corner sofa from €50, a leather armchair from €20. More: leather sofa cleaning. If the sofa is faux leather, say so in the form, because the fabric sofa prices apply to it.

The price in Imanta and what may stay

Travel to Imanta is included, and the minimum order is €45. A two-seater sofa is from €35, a three-seater from €45, a leather two-seater from €40. The price is affected by size, fabric, dirt, smell and distance, and we agree it before the work. The full list is on the prices page.

May stay: greasy armrest stains soaked in over the years, sun fading, pressure marks on velour and a smell from the filling. The coating on faux leather may peel over time, and cleaning does not repair it.

Frequently asked questions

I live in a new development, and the sofa is only a year old. Is there any point in cleaning it?

If there are no stains or smell, there is usually no need. If there is a stain on light fabric, it is better to act in good time: send a photo, and the specialist will tell you what to expect.

What if I do not know the type of fabric for the form?

That is no obstacle. Lift the seat cushion, find the label and send a photo of it. If there is no label, write that and add a close-up of the fabric.

How quickly will you get in touch?

After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes. We agree the time of the work once the price is known.

Do you also clean car seats at the house?

Yes, one car seat costs from €12. The €45 minimum order applies, so it is easy to combine them with a sofa or several seats.
