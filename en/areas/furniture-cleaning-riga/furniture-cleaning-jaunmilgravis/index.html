Jaunmīlgrāvis is part of the Mīlgrāvis neighbourhood in the Northern District, with apartment buildings, historic low-rise buildings and port sites. Send a photo, and you will get a price. Travel is included.

Start with a photo, not assumptions

We cannot claim anything here about each particular building, and there is no need to. The furniture itself says more than the address. For the specialist to name a price, a few photos and a short description are enough.

  1. The whole piece One photo from the front in daylight, so that the size can be seen.
  2. The problem spot A close-up of the stain or wear.
  3. Label Lift the seat cushion or look under the furniture.
  4. Short description What was spilled, how long ago, whether there is a smell, whether pets live in the home.

The page how to photograph a sofa to get a price has examples. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes, and we agree the price before the work.

A flat in an apartment building

Sofa cleaning in Jaunmīlgrāvis is done in the room, so the lift or stairs are not an issue. It helps to clear space around the furniture, take off blankets and pick small things out of the gaps. If the sofa pulls out and someone sleeps on it, plan a day when the sofa is not needed straight away.

A two-seater sofa costs from €35, a three-seater from €45, a corner sofa from €50. A two-seater sofa or a single chair falls short of the €45 minimum order, so add something else you have at home anyway: an armchair from €20, a pouffe from €10 or dining chairs from €7.

Mattress cleaning: one side or two

Mattress cleaning in Jaunmīlgrāvis starts with the question of what exactly is needed. A single mattress is from €30, a double from €45 per side, with the second side at +50%. If the stain is on one side only, the other can be left alone.

A mattress must not be soaked. Foam is sensitive to moisture, moisture may cause rust in springs, and direct sun and heat harm latex. So mattress cleaning is assessed differently from sofa cleaning. If the mattress is TEMPUR, check the manufacturer's care instructions, because this material cannot be washed.

With a urine stain, the outcome depends on how deep the liquid has gone. More: urine smell in a mattress. If there is mould in the filling or the smell does not go away, the mattress may have to be replaced.

Air movement and drying

After cleaning, furniture needs moving air. If it is a dry day outside, open the windows.

Drying is affected by air humidity, airing, temperature, the fabric and the filling. So we do not promise a set number of hours. A fan at home will help. Do not put furniture by a heater, especially leather.

Stains from the port and industrial area

If you work at the port or in manufacturing and your work clothes end up on the sofa or a car seat, the stain may be greasy or oily. A stain like that has to be handled differently from coffee. Solvent may damage foam upholstery, so do not try to clean it with solvents. Blot what you can and send a photo.

Car seat cleaning costs from €12 per seat. If a company has a minibus or several vehicles, see the page bus and taxi seat cleaning. For a larger volume we agree the price separately.

A rental flat and used furniture

If you are moving into a rental flat or have bought a used sofa, only the present signs are known, not the history. Say whether you know what has happened to the furniture. If you do not, that is no obstacle: a photo shows the present condition. We write about this on the pages used sofa cleaning and furniture cleaning before moving in. If you see insects in the furniture, that is a job for pest control, and cleaning does not solve it.

Travel, minimum and an honest answer

Travel to Jaunmīlgrāvis is included, and the minimum order is €45. The price is affected by size, fabric, dirt, smell and distance. The full list is on the prices page.

May stay: a greasy stain soaked into the fabric over the years, a smell from the filling, wear and fading. Smoke smell can be reduced, but not always removed entirely. The specialist will tell you before the work what to expect.

Frequently asked questions

Is the price different for a leather sofa?

Yes, a leather two-seater sofa is from €40, a leather corner sofa from €50. Faux leather and velour have no separate prices: the fabric sofa prices apply.

How do I know if a mattress needs both sides done?

If the stain or smell is on one side and the mattress is not soaked through, one is usually enough. If the liquid has gone through, both have to be looked at. Send a photo of both sides.

There is an oil stain on the sofa from work clothes. Can it be cleaned?

Sometimes it can be reduced, but that depends on the fabric and on how old the stain is. An old grease stain may stay. Blot what you can and do not use solvents.

Can you clean car seats in the yard at home?

Yes, if a car can stop near the house. Write the number of seats and the type of vehicle in the form.
