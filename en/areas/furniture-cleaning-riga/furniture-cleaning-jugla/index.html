Terraced house: furniture on several floors

In a terraced house, upholstered furniture may be on several floors: a sofa in the living room, beds and a children's room upstairs. The specialist works right there, so the furniture need not be carried on the stairs, but it helps to say in the form which piece is on which floor.

One request with several pieces is easier than several small ones. An example: corner sofa cleaning from €50 and a double mattress from €45 per side. We agree the total before the work, and the final price depends on size, fabric, dirt and smell.

If there is a terrace in the yard, outdoor furniture cushion cleaning is done on site, and a removable cushion costs from €2. They are easy to add to the same request.

A family with children: what we can and cannot promise

A children's room usually has other problems: paint, juice, chocolate and urine. Children's sofa cleaning and children's mattress cleaning have separate pages, because what a child uses every day matters there. We do not say that cleaning makes furniture suitable for allergies. It is not treatment, but care of the fabric.

If a child has wet the mattress and the stain is fresh, gently blot it with a clean cloth, without rubbing, and do not use hot water. If liquid has reached the foam, the smell may stay. See the advice on child urine on a mattress.

Pram cleaning and child car seat cleaning cost from €20 each. On a car seat, the straps and buckle are cared for only according to the manufacturer's instructions. We make no claims about a car seat's protective properties: those are set by the manufacturer.

An estate flat in Jugla

In an apartment building the work is done in the room, and the lift or stairs are not an issue. If one room is also the bedroom, sofa bed cleaning from €40 will be the main job. In a small room it is important to air the room after the work, so choose a day when the windows can be open, and do not lay a blanket on the sofa until it has dried.

Lake Jugla and forests are close, but that does not say how damp your flat is. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) recommends airing a bedroom every morning for at least 15 minutes and, if possible, drying laundry outside the flat. That helps furniture too.

Schools, kindergartens and small offices

Jugla has educational institutions and retail sites. If you run a school, kindergarten or small office, see the pages school chair cleaning and kindergarten furniture cleaning. The published unit prices apply, but for a larger volume we agree the price by number. Write the type and number of chairs in the form.

The fabric decides what to expect

Different fabrics need different approaches. Velour and chenille may show pressure marks, and that is a property of the fabric. Leather absorbs moisture well, and a stain may soak into its structure, so it cannot be cleaned like fabric. A faux leather sofa has no separate price: the fabric sofa prices apply. A leather two-seater is from €40.

Before the work, look at the label under the seat cushion. The letter X means no liquids may be used. If no code can be found, write that in the form, because the codes are voluntary and some furniture does not have them.

Hair, used furniture and moving

If there is a dog or cat in the home, hair collects in the seams and under the cushions. Regular vacuuming helps, but some hair embeds itself in the fabric. The page pet hair on a sofa describes what is realistic. Do not rush to wash a cushion cover separately: it may shrink.

In a new home, furniture is easier to clean before moving in, while the rooms are empty. If a used sofa has been bought for the children's room, it is not known how it was used before, so say that in the form. Used sofa cleaning usually improves the look and reduces the smell in the fabric.

Frequently asked questions

A child wet the mattress in the night. Should I request cleaning straight away?

First gently blot it with a clean cloth, without rubbing, so that the liquid does not go deeper. Then send a photo. The less time has passed, the better the chances, but we cannot promise a result.

Will cleaning help a child with an allergy?

We cannot promise that. Cleaning is not an allergy treatment. Against dust mites, low air humidity, washing bedding at 60 °C and regular vacuuming are useful.

Can you clean a pram along with a sofa?

Yes, a pram costs from €20. It is easy to add to other furniture, because the minimum order is €45.

The terraced house has no lift, and the sofa is on the second floor. Is that difficult?

No. The furniture stays where it is, and the cleaning is done right there. Just say which floor it is on.
