The allotment garden house and seasonal rooms

If a summer house stands closed in winter, the furniture in it stays for a long time in damp, unaired air. In spring the first sign tends to be a smell, not a visible stain.

Before you send a request, air the room and see whether the smell fades. If it stays and you can see marks underneath, photograph them. Mould in porous filling cannot always be removed, and in many cases it means replacement, not cleaning. We write about this in the advice article why a sofa smells and what to do.

The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) points out that in a room with relative humidity above 80% the risk of mould is high. In a room that stands empty, it is worth checking the humidity with a hygrometer.

Work at the house: indoors or outdoors

We work on site, so large furniture need not be carried. For sofa cleaning it is best to choose a room that can be aired well. Terrace cushions and removable seats may stay wherever is easier for you, but afterwards they need a dry place and air.

Write in the form how to drive up to the house and whether the gate has to be opened. It also helps to know about stairs to the second floor, if the furniture is up there. If a piece cannot be reached from all sides, mention that in the form.

Pets, camper and car

In a house with a dog or cat, hair is an everyday thing, and the amount in a sofa can usually be reduced well. Repeated marking is a harder case. If a cat marks one and the same spot, talk to a vet first, because heavily soaked fabric may stay attractive to the animal even after cleaning.

If there is a camper at the house, its seats and mattress can be added to the same request: camper furniture cleaning. For a camper that has stood through the winter, the same questions matter as for a closed house: smell and damp. Car seat cleaning is from €12 per seat, and for leather seats read the page leather car seat cleaning.

Headboard, fireplace and leather chair

An upholstered headboard from €35 is one of the things often forgotten on the list, even though it is exactly what you rest your head against. If you live in a private house with a fireplace, place an armchair or leather sofa further from the heat: it dries out leather and makes it brittle. A leather armchair costs from €20, a leather two-seater sofa from €40.

Before the work, lift the seat cushions and see whether the furniture has a care label. It is often hidden. If the house has several chairs or sofas from one set, one photo of the label is enough.

Frequently asked questions

The house is on the edge of the neighbourhood by the Daugava. Is travel charged?

No, Katlakalns is a Riga neighbourhood, so travel is included throughout the neighbourhood.

Can camper seats be cleaned at the house?

Yes, if the camper can be reached and aired. Send a photo of the interior and say what has happened on the seats.

Is it worth cleaning a garden house sofa that has stood through the winter?

It depends on whether the smell stays after airing and whether mould is visible. If the furniture is cheap and the filling is damaged, we will tell you honestly that cleaning may not pay off.

Can I request cleaning for just car seats?

Yes. One seat is from €12, so four seats go over the €45 minimum. With fewer, you can add another piece of furniture.
