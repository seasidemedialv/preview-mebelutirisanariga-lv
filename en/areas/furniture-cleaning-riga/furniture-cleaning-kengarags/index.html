Ķengarags is a predominantly high-rise residential neighbourhood, and the residential area consists of 5 housing estates. The work varies not by estate but by sofa: for one the problem is a stain, for another a smell. Send a photo and the specialist will tell you the price.

A sofa you have lived with for years

High-rise building in Ķengarags began after a detailed plan of 1962, while in the 1950s two-storey standard houses started going up here for railway workers. That says nothing about the age of the furniture, but in a flat where one family has lived for a long time, a sofa may have spent many years in one spot.

Such a sofa does not always need rescuing from a stain. Sometimes the question is simpler: is it worth cleaning at all? Worn fabric will not become new after cleaning. But the dark bands on armrests and seats are often dirt, and that can be reduced. Send a photo of the seat and armrest, and you will get an answer before the work.

Three smells and three different outcomes

In a small flat, a smell can be noticed throughout the room. Three different sources need three different assessments.

Smell What usually happens What may stay
Cigarette smoke The residue sits in the fabric and filling, and cotton holds it for a long time The smell may come back from the filling
Cat urine The smell area may be up to three times larger than the stain If the liquid has reached the foam, the smell may stay
Damp If the source is the room, the smell may come back Cleaning does not remove the cause

Additional smell treatment costs from €15. Tell us what the source of the smell is, and the specialist will tell you whether it is needed. If the cat does this repeatedly, consult a vet first. More detail: cleaning urine from a sofa, cigarette smoke smell in a sofa.

Selling the sofa or letting the flat

If the sofa is being sold or the flat let, clean furniture is easier to show. The page sofa cleaning before selling describes what to do if the furniture will go on to a new owner. If the flat is being prepared for a new tenant, see furniture cleaning for rental flats.

If you choose only one thing, let it be the item the next user will notice first. When the flat is empty, the air moves more freely and the furniture can dry undisturbed.

The floor is no obstacle

The sofa does not have to be carried anywhere: it is cleaned right there in the flat, in a low two-storey house or at the top of a high-rise. Enter the address, floor and stairwell code, if there is one, in the form. Maskavas iela, the tram and the railway affect the way to the building, so also say from which side it is easiest to drive up.

  • Clear the space around the sofa so that it can be reached.
  • Remove blankets and decorative cushions.
  • Open a window after the work, if the weather allows.
  • Do not put anything on the sofa until it is dry.

A mattress after illness or long bed rest

If someone at home has been ill or in bed for a long time, the mattress may have stains and a smell. Mattress cleaning after illness can reduce what can be seen and smelt in the fabric, but it is not disinfection and not a medical service. Cleaning does not cure an allergy or settle the question of dust mites: mites breed in damp air, so room humidity and airing matter more.

If a stain has soaked in deep or the mattress is old, sometimes it is more sensible to replace it. Tell us what happened, and the specialist will say before the work what you can expect. A brief description in the form is enough.

Frequently asked questions

When is additional smell treatment needed?

Smell treatment costs extra, from €15. The specialist tells you from the description whether it is needed, because that depends on the source of the smell and how deep it goes.

The sofa smells of cigarettes, but nobody smokes in the flat any more. Will it go away?

The smell can be reduced, but we cannot promise it will disappear. Smoke residue can stay in fabric and filling for a long time.

How do I know whether a sofa is worth cleaning or better replaced?

Send photos of the seats, armrests and underside. If the fabric is worn through or the foam is showing, cleaning will achieve nothing. If the dark bands are dirt, it may help.
