Kleisti is a large, sparsely built-up north-western neighbourhood with forests, meadows and scattered residential areas, and travel here is included. Send a photo and the specialist will tell you the price.

Distance is not a problem, but describe the way

In Kleisti the houses are far apart, and public transport and the street network are sparser. If the house cannot be seen straight away from the street, add one sentence to the form on how to reach it: the turning, the gate, the way in. That saves time for both sides.

Travel is included throughout Riga, so distance from the centre does not affect the price. The minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45. So it makes sense to request all the furniture at once, rather than several times one item at a time.

The room hardly anyone uses

A larger house often has a guest room where the mattress and sofa stand unused. Damp lowlands and forests around the house tell us nothing about your rooms, but a room that is rarely aired can become stuffy. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) recommends airing the bedroom every morning for at least 15 minutes, and that advice also applies to a guest room.

If a mattress smells stuffy, first air the room and look at both sides of the mattress. Photograph any marks separately. Mattress cleaning in Kleisti usually helps with stains and smell in the fabric, but mould in the filling usually means the mattress has to be replaced. Read about this in the advice article mould in a mattress.

A coconut fibre mattress is sensitive to moisture, and if it stands on the floor it can accumulate moisture. A foam mattress, in turn, holds moisture in the filling for a long time. A single mattress costs from €30, a double from €45 per side, second side +50%.

Flats in the former manor

In the former manor house of the Kleisti estate, flats were created in the Soviet period. Rooms in such a building can differ from those in a standard block, and the floor or stairs do not complicate cleaning, because the furniture stays in the room. If the flat is cool or the room is heated by a stove, plan for drying: do not put the furniture by the stove, and let the air move.

Animals and riders: the smell that comes home with you

Kleisti has riding and sports grounds. If you come from a stable or your dog runs across a meadow, smell and dirt also end up in the house and the car. The amount of hair on a sofa can usually be reduced well, but horse and animal smell in fabric may fade without always disappearing.

Car seat cleaning costs from €12 per seat. Several seats together with a sofa easily reach the minimum order. If a dog or cat has urinated on the furniture, say so in the form, because smell treatment costs extra from €15 and the result cannot be promised. We write about pets on the page sofa cleaning after a cat or dog.

Fleas in a sofa are a job for pest control. Cleaning does not get rid of them, and this needs to be understood before you send a request.

Before the request: what to prepare

  1. List Walk round the house and write everything down, including the guest room, headboards and car seats.
  2. Photo For each item, one photo from a distance and one of the problem area.
  3. The way A brief description: the turning, the gate, where a car can stop.
  4. Airing Think about which day the windows can be opened and the room will have warmed up.

If the house has a leather sofa or chair by a stove or fireplace, move it further away. Heat dries leather out. A headboard costs from €35, a pouffe from €10, and both are easy to add to the list.

Terrace and seasonal furniture

In a house with a yard, terrace cushions are easy to add to a larger request. A removable cushion costs from €2. If they have spent the winter in a damp shed and have black marks, photograph them: mould in a porous filling can be hard to remove, and then it is more honest to replace the cushion.

How to work out your request

Sofa cleaning in Kleisti starts from €35 for a two-seater, from €45 for a three-seater and from €50 for a corner sofa. An armchair is from €20. We agree the price before the work, and it depends on size, fabric, dirt, smell and distance. You will find all prices in the price list.

May stay: strong animal smell in the filling, mould marks, wear and a stain that soaked in long ago. We will assess this from the photos.

Frequently asked questions

The house is a long way from the main road. Does travel cost more?

No, Kleisti is a Riga neighbourhood and travel is included. Just say in the form how to reach the house.

The guest mattress has not been used for years, but it smells. Is there any point in cleaning it?

If the smell comes from standing in a closed room, cleaning and airing often help. If there is mould, the mattress may have to be replaced. Send a photo.

Does the house need warming up before the work?

The room must be one where the furniture can dry. In a cool, damp room fabric dries more slowly, so choose a day when the house has warmed up and can be aired.

Can you clean car seats that smell of horses?

We can, but we do not promise the smell will go completely. One seat is from €12, smell treatment extra from €15.
