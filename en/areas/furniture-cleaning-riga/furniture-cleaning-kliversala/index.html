Klīversala is part of the Āgenskalns neighbourhood by the Daugava, with the perimeter blocks typical of central Riga. Send a photo and the specialist will tell you the price. Travel is included.

Flats in perimeter blocks

Perimeter blocks mean inner courtyards and buildings along the street. That changes only a few practical things. Is the entrance from the street or through the courtyard? Which floor is the furniture on? Does the gate have a code? If the flat is in a historic building, the rooms may be high, and airing the room after cleaning matters.

For some houses, the waterfront setting and Āgenskalns Bay nearby mean more damp air. We do not claim that about every building, but fabric dries more slowly in damp air. Plan the cleaning for when the windows can stay open, and do not cover the furniture until it is dry.

Sofa cleaning in Klīversala starts from €35 for a two-seater. If the flat has velour or another pile fabric, bear in mind that pressure marks are a property of the fabric and cleaning does not usually remove them.

Hotels and apartments

Klīversala has the hotel “Daugava”, and hospitality is one of the neighbourhood's functions. For a hospitality business the question is usually the same: furniture and mattresses are in use every day, but a room cannot stand empty for long.

So it matters to know in good time how many rooms can be taken out of use and how ventilation will be arranged. We do not promise a drying time, because it depends on humidity, ventilation, temperature, fabric and filling. Pages: hotel furniture cleaning and hotel and hostel mattress cleaning.

A hotel mattress should not be soaked: foam is sensitive to moisture, and in a spring mattress moisture can cause rust. If a mattress has mould or bedbugs, that is not a cleaning job. Bedbugs are a job for pest control.

Short lets and new flats

In the waterfront development areas and new developments, flats may be let for short stays. What matters there is the time between guests and how soon the furniture can be ready. We do not promise an exact time, so plan the cleaning for a period when the flat is free for longer. See furniture cleaning for rental flats and furniture cleaning for property managers.

Blot a fresh stain lightly with a clean cloth, without rubbing, and do not use hot water. It is worth telling the cleaner that as well.

Restaurants, cultural venues and offices: what to prepare

  • The number of chairs or sofas and one photo of each type.
  • The label, if there is one, and the material: fabric, leather, faux leather.
  • Whether there is a smell or stains that need a separate assessment.
  • When the room can stay empty so that the furniture can dry.

For a restaurant or café the page restaurant furniture cleaning will be useful. Direct water and heat can harm a leather chair, so it is assessed separately. The published unit prices apply, and for larger volumes we agree the price before the work. Choose a time when the room can stay empty and air out after the work.

Frequently asked questions

An office needs 20 chairs cleaned. How do we get a price?

Enter the number of chairs in the form and send a photo of one chair. For larger volumes we agree the price separately, but the price of one chair starts from €10.

Does the office chair price depend on the fabric?

Yes, the price depends on fabric, dirt and size. The starting price is from €10, but leather chairs have to be assessed separately, so send a photo.

The flat is in an inner courtyard. Will the specialist find it?

Describe the entrance in the form and give the gate code. It is not a problem if we know in advance.

Can you clean hotel mattresses between guests?

That can be agreed if the room stays free long enough. How long depends on the mattress and the air, so we do not promise an exact time.
