The Krasta estate is the most recently formed part of the Latgale neighbourhood, where intensive building of retail and office premises began after independence was restored. So one stretch of street may hold very different jobs. Send a photo and the specialist will tell you the price.

Office chairs and sofas: what to write in the request

Krasta rajons has shopping centres, offices, warehouses and apartment buildings, and for a business the cleaning depends less on the fabric than on the time when the rooms can be taken over. So a few concrete details help in the request, and with them it is easier for the specialist to give a price.

  • how many chairs and sofas there are and what kind: office chairs, a customer area sofa, meeting room chairs;
  • one photo of the whole group and one close-up of the most worn seat;
  • the care label, if you can find it, because the code on it decides what may be done to the fabric;
  • which hours of the day the rooms are free and which entrance gives access.

Office chair cleaning in Krasta rajons costs from €10 per item. The unit price is the same for businesses, but we agree the price for larger volumes separately. More detail: office chair cleaning and office sofa cleaning.

Café seating: why a grease stain must not be rubbed

There are food outlets around Krasta iela too, and whatever is on the table ends up on an upholstered seat: sauce, oil and coffee. A fresh grease stain is first carefully blotted with a clean, absorbent cloth, not rubbed. Hot water is not used, because it can set the stain.

Solvents can damage the foam in the upholstery, so staff are better off not using them on the seats. Read the label first, and if there is none, send a photo. An old grease stain may stay as a darker patch, and we will tell you so honestly from the photo. More: café chair cleaning and restaurant furniture cleaning.

Faux leather in offices and customer areas near shops

Office chairs and customer area sofas may have a faux leather finish. Its price is the same as for fabric furniture. The faux leather coating may peel with time, and cleaning cannot repair that, so if the surface is already flaking, send a close-up and we will tell you whether the work is worthwhile.

The area also has car workshops and shops, and people in work clothes use the seats in their customer areas. If the chairs stand in a waiting room, see waiting room chair cleaning. If coffee is spilt on fabric in the middle of the working day, first blot it lightly with a clean cloth dampened in cool water, and do not soak the fabric, so that the liquid does not reach the filling.

A flat near Krasta iela: windows, traffic and drying

In flats in Krasta rajons the biggest difference from quieter neighbourhoods is ventilation. With the heavy traffic and the wind off the Daugava, not everyone is keen to open a window. Yet damp fabric needs moving air after cleaning. Drying time depends on humidity, room temperature, fabric and filling, so we do not promise a number of hours.

If you would rather not keep a window open for long, the air can be moved in other ways. The US Environmental Protection Agency says that fans and a dehumidifier speed up drying. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) gives 40–60% as a comfortable indoor humidity. Place the cushions so that air also reaches between the seat and the backrest, and do not sit on the sofa while the fabric is damp.

Corner sofa cleaning in Krasta rajons costs from €50, and a large corner sofa from €60. If the bed in the flat needs attention too, mattress cleaning at home starts from €30 for a single mattress. For a rental flat being vacated between tenants, see furniture cleaning for rental flats.

Short-let flats and apartments

If you let a flat short term, there is usually only a little time for cleaning between guests. Write down the date of the next booking and leave enough time, because the fabric has to dry, and nobody can honestly state the number of hours in advance. A sofa and a mattress in one request means one trip. For guest rooms, hotel furniture cleaning is useful too.

Frequently asked questions

Our office has six chairs. Is that enough for an order?

Yes. Six office chairs cost from €60, which is above the €45 minimum. If there are only three chairs (from €30), you can add a customer area sofa. With a pouffe from €10 the total is €40, and then you pay €45.

Krasta rajons is not an official neighbourhood. How do I know if my address is in your area?

Travel is included throughout Riga, so the boundary does not affect the price. Enter your address in the form, and that is enough.

Can furniture be cleaned during working hours, while people are in the office?

We discuss that before the work. Write which rooms can be freed up and when, because a damp chair cannot be used for a while. If it is easier, split the chairs into groups and write how many you can manage without at one time.

The surface of our faux leather chairs is flaking. Will cleaning fix that?

No. Cleaning removes dirt but does not repair a peeling coating. Send a close-up and we will tell you whether the chair is worth cleaning.
