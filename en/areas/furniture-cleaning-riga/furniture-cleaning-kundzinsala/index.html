Reaching the island: what to say in the form

Port roads and a bridge lead to Kundziņsala, and port areas may have a security regime. That affects the way to the address, not the work itself, so one sentence in the request helps: the house address, the most convenient side to drive in from, and whether a car can be parked in the yard.

If the house is deep in a yard or the entrance is from a side street, write that too. The furniture does not have to be carried out and taken over the bridge: sofa cleaning in Kundziņsala is done right in the room where the sofa stands.

Wind off the Daugava, room temperature and drying

In Kundziņsala, everyday life is shaped by the Daugava and the wind. An open window on a windy day moves the air well, but a cold room gives up moisture more slowly. Drying depends on humidity, temperature, fabric and filling, so we do not promise a set number of hours.

In practice this means that if the house is only partly heated in winter, plan the cleaning for a room you can keep warm. Do not push a sofa or leather chair right up to a heater or stove, because heat dries leather out and makes it brittle. Move the furniture slightly away from the wall so that air reaches the back as well.

How to read a care label

The label is usually under the seat cushions or on the underside of the furniture. The letter on it says what the fabric can take, and what the specialist can do depends directly on that.

Code What it means
W water-based foam, sparingly, no chemical solvents
S waterless solvent only, in a well-ventilated room
WS or S-W either a mild solvent or a gentle foam
X no liquids, only vacuuming and light brushing; any other cleaning has to be decided separately

The codes are voluntary, so some furniture has none at all. In that case send a close-up of the fabric and the specialist will tell you what to expect. These letters are not the same system as the care symbols on clothing labels, so do not confuse them with the circled W. More: how to photograph a sofa for a price.

If the sofa has a fresh stain, while you wait blot it lightly with a clean cloth, without rubbing, and do not use hot water. Chlorine bleach and strong cleaning products can change the colour of the fabric.

A mattress at home: look at the surface before you send a request

In a house where a mattress has not been moved for years, first turn it over and look at both sides. Foam is sensitive to moisture, and too much water can stay in the core. In a spring mattress, moisture can cause rust. So a mattress must not be soaked.

Mattress cleaning in Kundziņsala costs from €30 for a single and from €45 for a double (one side), second side +50%. If the mattress has dark marks and a smell of mould, say so straight away. Mould inside a mattress often means replacing rather than cleaning it, and the first thing to find out is where the moisture comes from. See mould in a mattress and mattress cleaning.

Pets, car seats and what may stay

If you have a dog or cat, say so in the form, because hair and smell change the amount of work. Smell treatment is extra, from €15. After urine the smell may stay or come back, especially if the liquid has reached the filling, so we do not promise to get rid of it. More: sofa cleaning after a cat or dog.

While the specialist is at the house, the car seats can be done as well. Car seat cleaning costs from €12 per seat, and from €20 for a child car seat. As the car stands next to the house in the yard, this is one request, not two. For the straps and buckle of a child car seat, the seat's own instructions are what counts.

Frequently asked questions

I only need one kitchen chair cleaned. Is that possible?

One chair costs from €7, but the minimum order is €45. So it is better value to add the other chairs or a sofa you have long meant to have cleaned.

Will the specialist get to the house if the road runs along the port?

Write the address and, if you know it, the most convenient way in. If the route has a gate or a pass checkpoint, mention it, and we will discuss it before the work.

Does the sofa need preparing?

Take blankets, toys and small items off the seats and move aside whatever stands right in front. If you know where the care label is, photograph it. It is often under the cushions or on the underside of the sofa.
