Early 20th-century Art Nouveau villas and interwar functionalist houses alternate here, so the rooms and interiors differ too. Send a photo of each item and the specialist will tell you what to expect.

Velour, leather and fabric in one house

There may be a velour sofa in the living room, a leather chair in the study and a pale fabric sofa in the children's room. Each material has its own limits, so send a photo of each item rather than one overall view.

  • Velour: pressure marks and shade changes in the light are a property of the fabric, not a stain. Cleaning does not remove them. More detail: velour sofa cleaning.
  • Leather: direct water and heat damage it, so its care differs from that of fabric. Do not apply creams on your own initiative. More detail: leather sofa cleaning.
  • Pale fabric: an old stain gets lighter but may stay visible. More detail: pale sofa cleaning.
  • Faux leather: the coating may peel with time, and cleaning cannot repair that.

The care label code is what decides. The label is often under the seat cushions or on the underside. The letter X means no liquids, only vacuuming and light brushing, and any other cleaning has to be decided separately.

Fireplace and sun: where the furniture stands

In a house with a fireplace or large windows facing the garden, where the furniture stands matters, because the state of the fabric depends on it. Heat dries leather out, and cleaning does not restore a dried-out leather chair by the fireplace. If fabric is damp after cleaning, do not dry it with a fireplace or heater: direct heat is not the answer, especially for leather and latex.

Mežaparks is a green neighbourhood by Ķīšezers, and windows on the garden side can be opened wide. Fabric dries more slowly on a rainy day, so we do not promise a number of hours. On what affects the time: how long a sofa takes to dry.

If the leather has already cracked, cleaning does not remove the cracks. Leather soaps and oils are not recommended on finished leather, and relative humidity above 65% encourages mould on leather. So do not keep a leather chair on a damp veranda in winter.

Headboard, daybed and dining chairs

In houses with a larger bedroom the bed may have an upholstered headboard. Upholstered bed and headboard cleaning costs from €35. A headboard gets soiled where the head touches it, and after cleaning that spot usually gets lighter, but it may differ from the rest of the surface.

Daybed cleaning and upholstered chair cleaning go well with a sofa in one order. A set of six dining chairs starts from €42, and with a pouffe from €10 the minimum order is reached. If the chairs are antique, send a photo from the side so that the specialist can see how the upholstery is fixed.

Dog walks, terrace and garden furniture

In a detached house with a garden, a pet brings sand, hair and moisture onto the same sofa the family sits on in the evening. Write that there is a dog or cat in the house. We discuss hair and smell separately, and smell treatment costs extra, from €15. The smell may stay if urine has reached the filling.

Terrace chair cushions fit into the same order: outdoor furniture cushion cleaning. A removable cushion costs from €2. The minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45. One visit often covers several items, so this is not usually an obstacle, and travel in Riga is included. On planning work in a larger house, read furniture cleaning in a private house.

Guest houses, restaurants and event days

Alongside homes, Mežaparks also has hospitality, sport and culture. The same unit price applies to a guest house or restaurant, and we agree larger volumes separately. More detail: hotel furniture cleaning and restaurant furniture cleaning.

During big events the traffic in the neighbourhood changes considerably. If you are choosing the time yourself, write that in the request and we will talk through a suitable day.

Frequently asked questions

Our old sofa has expensive, inherited upholstery. Can it be cleaned?

That depends on the condition of the fabric and the label, if there is one. Send close-ups of the seams and worn spots. If the fabric is fragile, we will tell you that cleaning may not be the right step, and then restoration is a matter for a restorer.

The velour sofa has lighter patches on the seat. Is that a stain?

Often those are pressure marks, typical of velour and chenille, not a stain. Cleaning does not remove them. Send photos from several angles and we will tell you exactly what we see.

Can one request cover both a sofa and terrace cushions?

Yes, enter everything in one form and add photos. We will add up the prices and agree the total before the work.

The sofa smells of dog. How much will it cost?

A three-seater sofa costs from €45, smell treatment extra from €15, together from €60. The smell usually gets weaker, but it may stay if it is in the filling.
