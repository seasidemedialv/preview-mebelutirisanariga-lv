It is often a flat with small rooms, where the sofa takes up much of the floor. Send a photo and the specialist will tell you the price.

A corner sofa in a small room

In a room where a corner sofa takes up a whole wall, there is no need to find space for cleaning: we do it where the sofa stands. It matters more to say how many seats it has and whether it has removable cushions. Corner sofa cleaning in Purvciems costs from €50, a large corner sofa from €60, a U-shaped one from €65.

In a small room, moisture does not disperse after cleaning as easily as in a spacious one. So after cleaning, open a window and the door to the hallway and let the air flow through the whole flat while it dries. How long that takes depends on humidity, fabric and filling.

If the sofa stands against an outside wall, move it a little further from the wall. That helps the air reach it and shows whether everything is in order behind it. Dark marks at the back may be a sign of mould, and then the first thing to find out is where the moisture comes from.

In a one-room flat where the sofa is also a bed, add a photo with the sleeping area pulled out: sofa bed cleaning costs from €40. A lift and stairs do not affect the work, because the furniture stays in the flat.

For landlords: a flat between tenants

Between two tenants you can repaint the walls, but you will not renew a sofa that way. Three things help a landlord in the request:

  • by what date the flat must be ready;
  • a photo of each item, with close-ups of stains;
  • whether the smell is from smoke, pets or a closed-up flat.

More detail on this work: furniture cleaning for rental flats. If you are selling the flat or moving in: furniture cleaning before moving in.

Smoke smell in furniture: honest about the limit

If people have smoked in a flat for years, smoke residue stays in the fabric and filling. In studies, cotton and polyester still held the chemicals 19 months after exposure. Research summaries state that usual methods such as carpet cleaning, repainting walls and wiping surfaces give only a short-lived result, so the smell can be reduced, but we cannot promise it will disappear. It may come back from the filling.

Smell treatment costs extra, from €15. Before ordering, consider whether that is worth it for an old sofa. If the room itself is soaked with smoke, the furniture may take the smell up again. On this: cigarette smoke smell in a sofa.

South-eastern detached houses and offices

Detached houses are typical of the south-eastern part of Purvciems, and one visit there may cover several items rather than one sofa. Read about such work here: furniture cleaning in a private house. For retail and office buildings see office furniture cleaning: the price is per unit, with larger volumes by agreement.

New developments: pale sofas and dust after renovation

Purvciems also has new developments and renovated flats, where a sofa may be pale. First vacuum up the building dust, and request cleaning once the renovation has really finished, or the fabric will get dirty again. More: furniture cleaning after renovation.

On a faux leather sofa that has stood by a window for years, the coating may peel, and cleaning does not repair that. A faux leather sofa costs the same as a fabric sofa: faux leather sofa cleaning.

Is an old sofa worth cleaning at all

An honest question before ordering: what is the sofa worth now? Cleaning a corner sofa, from €50, can be a good idea if the frame is sound and the fabric is not torn. If the foam has collapsed and the fabric is worn, cleaning will not restore it, and the money is better put towards a new sofa.

In a rental flat where a sofa has to last a few more seasons, cleaning can be a sensible stopgap. Send a photo and the specialist will tell you whether the result will show.

Frequently asked questions

Tenants smoked in the flat. Will cleaning the sofa be enough?

Sofa cleaning can reduce the smell, but we cannot promise it will disappear. If the smell is in the walls and curtains, the sofa may take it up again. Send a photo and describe how long people smoked there.

Is it worth cleaning an old sofa that has stood there since the 1990s?

That depends on the filling and fabric. If the foam has collapsed, cleaning will not change that. If the sofa is sound but the fabric is dirty, cleaning usually helps. Send a photo and we will tell you frankly.

The flat is empty and I am in another country. Can I request cleaning remotely?

You can. The request with photos can be filled in from anywhere. Write who will let the specialist into the flat, and we will agree the price before the work.

I have a two-seater sofa and two armchairs. Does that reach the minimum?

Yes, a sofa from €35 and two armchairs from €40 make from €75 in total, which is above the €45 minimum. We agree the final amount from the photos.
