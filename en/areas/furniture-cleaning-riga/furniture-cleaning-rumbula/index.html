Among the allotments there are a few farmsteads and private houses built in the 20th and 21st centuries, while the rest of the neighbourhood is made up of large, sparsely built-up areas and industry. Send a photo and the specialist will tell you the price.

Private house: everything in one request

If a house in Rumbula has room for a car, one visit can cover both the furniture indoors and the seats in the yard. So list everything you need in the request, not just the sofa. The €45 minimum then stops mattering, and there is only one trip.

  • Living room: sofa from €35, corner sofa from €50.
  • Terrace: cushions from €2 each, armchair from €20.
  • Bedroom: mattress from €30, headboard from €35.
  • Yard: car seat from €12, child car seat from €20.

A larger house with several rooms needs a clearer plan. More detail: furniture cleaning in a private house and upholstered furniture cleaning.

Car seats in the yard, not at a garage

There are car workshops in the neighbourhood, but the car does not have to go anywhere for seat cleaning if there is room in the yard. Car seat cleaning in Rumbula costs from €12 per seat, and four seats reach the minimum order. Write whether the seats are fabric or leather, and send photos of where the stains are.

Leather seats are a different matter, because leather takes water and heat badly: leather car seat cleaning. On a hot day, park the car in the shade, because heat harms leather. A coffee or juice stain in a seat seam may stay.

Vans and minibuses working in the warehouse district have more seats, and the price is by agreement. On this: bus and taxi seat cleaning.

Office chairs by the warehouses

In warehouse and logistics offices a chair is used differently from one in a private office: staff sit down in work clothes, which may be dusty. An office chair costs from €10, so five chairs already reach the minimum. We agree larger volumes separately.

For a staff room with a sofa and kitchen chairs, note in the request how many people use it and what the shift pattern is, so that a time can be chosen when the room is free. More detail: office chair cleaning.

Child car seat: what is allowed

If the household also has a child car seat, it helps to know the limit. Manufacturers write that straps must not be soaked or washed with strong soap, and that the buckle must not be cleaned with soap or solvents, as this can affect how it works. So for the straps and buckle, the seat's instructions are decisive.

Before making a request, find the instructions or the seat's model. We make no claims about the seat's safety after cleaning. If a strap is worn or very dirty, manufacturers recommend replacing it.

Open ground, wind and drying

Rumbula has large open areas, and on a windy day a through draught changes the air quickly. Drying time, though, depends on humidity, fabric and filling, not on the wind alone. If the house has heating you can adjust, plan the work for a day when the room is warm. Fabric dries more slowly on a damp autumn day.

In allotment garden houses that stand unheated in winter, air the furniture first. If there are signs of mould, send a photo and we will tell you whether cleaning makes sense.

Pets, garden and smell

In a house with a garden a dog brings in soil and moisture, while a cat may mark the same spot again and again. Repeated marking is a matter of behaviour or health, so a vet should be consulted first, or the spot may attract the cat again after cleaning. International cat care sources say that heavily soaked fabric stays attractive for marking even after cleaning.

The smell area may be up to three times larger than the visible stain, so photos from several sides help. Smell treatment costs extra, from €15, and the smell may stay or come back if the liquid has reached the filling. More: cleaning urine from a sofa.

The price and the honest limit

The minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45. Travel is included in Riga. The price depends on size, fabric, soiling and smell, and we agree it before the work. Full list: prices.

Oil from work clothes that has soaked into the fabric, and old stains, may stay. We will tell you so from the photo or on site, not after the work.

Frequently asked questions

The house is by the allotments with no clear address. What should I write?

Write the nearest street and a landmark, and add a map pin if you can. That is enough to find the way.

Can you clean car seats by the warehouse while I work?

That can be agreed if the car stands where it can be reached. Write the address, where the car is and the number of seats.

We have five office chairs and a sofa in the staff room. How much does that cost?

Five office chairs cost from €50, a two-seater sofa from €35. Together that is from €85, and we agree the final amount from the photos, before the work.

We have a leather sofa. Does it cost more?

A leather two-seater sofa costs from €40, a leather corner sofa from €50. Faux leather and velour cost the same as fabric sofas. If the leather has cracked, send a close-up.
