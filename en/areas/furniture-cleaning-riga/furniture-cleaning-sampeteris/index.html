Offices and meeting rooms

A business first needs to know how many items and when. An office chair in Šampēteris costs from €10, so five chairs reach the minimum order, and for ten chairs the price starts from €100. We agree larger volumes separately.

Guest rooms near the airport

Alongside offices, Šampēteris also has hospitality premises, and there a mattress and sofa get different use from those in a home: guests change, and stains and smells change with them. Before making a request, choose which rooms need cleaning and add photos showing the stains.

Hotel and hostel mattress cleaning rests on the same principle as at home: a mattress must not be soaked, because moisture may stay in foam for a long time. The room has to be aired after the work, and bookings need planning with time to spare: the next guest must not find a damp bed. More: hotel furniture cleaning.

If bed bugs are suspected in a room, that is a job for pest control, not cleaning.

Detached houses in the residential clusters

The residential clusters lie among the warehouses, but the houses in them are ordinary houses with ordinary furniture. Sofa cleaning in Šampēteris costs from €35, and if the house also has a mattress and chairs, they can go in one request. Upholstered chair cleaning and dining chair cleaning are useful too, because dining chairs (from €7 each) reach the €45 minimum on their own only from seven upwards.

At some addresses, being close to the airport and main roads means noise, so you may not want to keep a window open. Then a fan provides the moving air instead of an open window. The sofa still has to be aired until the filling is completely dry, because damp upholstery in a closed room can go stale.

Leather chairs, meeting rooms and shops

A leather meeting room chair needs different care from a fabric chair. Leather takes direct water badly, and a chair is better not kept right next to a heater. Leather furniture cleaning does not mean a new look: cleaning does not restore colour worn off the armrests. On faux leather the coating may peel, and cleaning does not fix that.

The neighbourhood also has retail and services. For a salon see beauty salon furniture cleaning. For a coffee stain in the office: blot lightly with a clean cloth, without rubbing, and do not pour on hot water. Write in the request how long ago the stain happened, because what you can expect depends on that.

Hostel mattresses: both sides or one

The mattress price is per side, and the second side costs +50%. In a guest house where mattresses get turned, this is a practical question: do both sides need cleaning or just one? Send photos of both. If the mattress has a removable cover, read the label, because some covers can be washed.

For a blood stain on a guest bed, cold water and blotting help straight away, while heat sets it. An old stain may stay, and we will tell you so before the work. More detail: blood stain on a mattress.

Frequently asked questions

We have a guest house with six rooms. Can we arrange regular cleaning?

Regular cleaning can be agreed, and the price for larger volumes is by agreement. Write how many beds and sofas there are and when the rooms are most often free.

When can guests go back in the room after mattress cleaning?

We do not promise a set hour, because it depends on the mattress, humidity and ventilation. Allow time to spare and do not put a guest on a mattress that has not dried.

Šampēteris is mostly businesses. Do you also clean in homes?

Yes. We clean furniture both for businesses and in homes. Write the address and add a photo.

Can company car seats be included too?

Yes. Car seat cleaning costs from €12 per seat. Write the number of cars, the seat material and where the cars will be parked.
