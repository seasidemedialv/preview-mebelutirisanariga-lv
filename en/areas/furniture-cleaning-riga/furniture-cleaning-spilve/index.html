Furniture that has been in a warehouse

Spilve has warehouses, and a sofa or mattress may be in a warehouse or shed waiting for a move. After such a time, the question is not looks but whether the furniture has taken up moisture and smell. Before you take it back home, put it somewhere dry, open the packaging and let it air.

Then check three signs: whether there are dark marks, whether the smell weakens after airing and whether the filling is dry. If moisture has reached the filling or mould can be seen, cleaning does not solve it, and the furniture often has to be replaced. If the fabric is merely dusty, cleaning usually helps.

More: smelly sofa and smelly mattress. If several items need cleaning before you move in: furniture cleaning before moving in.

A garden house in an allotment colony

A garden house is not usually heated in winter, and the room stands closed. On the first spring day, open the door and windows, take the cushions outside and let the furniture air all day. If the smell weakens, you can request cleaning. Plan it for dry weather, because fabric dries more slowly in an unheated room.

A garden house may also have a single mattress, an armchair and terrace cushions. All of these can go in one request, which makes the €45 minimum easy to reach: mattress from €30, armchair from €20. On cushions: outdoor furniture cushion cleaning. On sofas: sofa cleaning.

A mattress after storage

A mattress that has spent a year in an unheated room is a separate question. If it has stood on the floor or in plastic film, moisture may have built up. A coconut fibre mattress is especially sensitive: the manufacturer Kurlon points out that moisture can build up in it on the floor. In a spring mattress moisture can cause rust, while in foam it stays in the core.

If a mattress smells of mould or has marks on it, there is no point in cleaning it: mould inside often means replacement. If the smell is only dusty, mattress cleaning at home starts from €30 for a single mattress. Photos of both sides help to tell the difference. More: mould in a mattress.
