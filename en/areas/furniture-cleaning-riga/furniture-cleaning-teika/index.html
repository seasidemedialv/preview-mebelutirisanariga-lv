Pre-war houses and old furniture

Buyers of plots in Teika once had to build free-standing houses for one or two families. In such houses and in the older low-rise buildings you often find furniture that has been inherited or restored. With a chair or sofa like that, the first thing is to understand what it is: fabric, filling, age.

Old upholstery has often lost its care label. The codes on labels are voluntary, so even newer furniture may not have them. Too much water on an unknown fabric can leave a water ring, or cause the colour to bleed or the fabric to shrink, so a piece like this must not be soaked.

Send a close-up of the fabric and a photo from underneath, if the filling can be seen there. If the piece is valuable or historic and the specialist thinks the risk is too great, you will be told before the work.

Leather and faux leather in the study and living room

A leather chair or sofa needs different care from a fabric piece. Most leather is damaged by direct water, it readily absorbs stains, and methods that suit textiles can damage its structure. That is why leather sofa cleaning is a different job, and on old leather not every stain can be removed.

With faux leather the main question is the coating. Over time it may peel, and cleaning cannot repair that. If cracks are already visible on a faux leather sofa, send a close-up so you know what to expect. The price for faux leather is the same as for a fabric sofa, while a leather two-seater sofa is from €40, a leather corner sofa from €50 and a leather armchair from €20.

Do not dry leather by a radiator. Heat dries it out and deforms it, so a leather sofa is better kept away from the radiator.

After cleaning: airing a flat on a main road

After cleaning, furniture has to be aired, and how long it takes to dry depends on the humidity, air movement, temperature, fabric and filling. We do not promise exact hours. If the window faces Brīvības gatve or Gustava Zemgala gatve and you would rather not keep it open, open a window on the courtyard side or air the room briefly but regularly.

For the bedroom, the Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) recommends opening a window for at least 15 minutes every morning. This habit is also useful if part of the order is mattress cleaning in Teika.

Office chairs and break areas

Teika has office complexes and technology companies whose chairs are used every working day. There is more on this work on the pages office chair cleaning and office sofa cleaning. The unit prices are the same, and for larger volumes we set the price by agreement.

In the form, give the number of chairs and add a few photos of the most worn ones. A fresh coffee stain usually comes out more easily than an old one, but that depends on the fabric. Shiny, worn armrests may stay as they are, because wear is not dirt.

Where to park and what else to mention

In Teika's dense blocks and by the main roads, parking may be harder, so write briefly in the form where it is possible to stop nearby. Also mention the floor and, if the building has a code or an intercom, how to get into the stairwell.

If the headboard (from €35) is upholstered, then together with the mattress it already goes over the minimum. More: upholstered bed and headboard cleaning.

Frequently asked questions

Our office in Teika is open until the evening. Can the chairs be cleaned when nobody is there?

Write in the form when the room is free, and the specialist will agree it with you. Bear in mind that the chairs need time to dry after cleaning, so it is better not to do this just before a meeting day.

The chair was inherited from my grandparents and the fabric is delicate. Can it be cleaned?

That can only be decided from a photo or on site. Send a close-up and, if you know what is in the filling, write that too. Some old fabrics are better not cleaned with liquid, and then we will tell you so honestly.

Does the whole sofa have to be cleaned if the stain is only on one seat?

Cleaning a single stain on fabric can leave a water ring, so it is often more sensible to clean the whole surface. Send a photo, and the specialist will tell you what is better in your case.

The coating on a faux leather office chair is peeling. Will cleaning help?

No. Cleaning cannot repair peeling. The surface can be cleaned, but the places where the coating has come off will stay visible.
