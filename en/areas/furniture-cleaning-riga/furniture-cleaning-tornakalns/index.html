Low-rise family homes, workers' tenements and, here and there, splendid villas grew up here, and next to them are university complexes and new developments. An old chair in a wooden house and a new fabric sofa with a removable cover call for different conversations. So we start with a photo.

Three kinds of home, three kinds of question

  1. Historic wooden and brick buildings Rooms may be high and cool, stairwells cold. It matters that the room is heated and can be aired after cleaning, otherwise the filling stays damp for longer.
  2. Villas and family homes Several items from different rooms can go into one request, so the €45 minimum is usually no obstacle.
  3. New developments Furniture is often newer, and the care label can still be found. A stain on a light fabric sofa shows at once, so blot a fresh stain rather than rubbing it.

This is a way of thinking about the job, not a statement about your house. Even on one street in Torņakalns the buildings may be very different, so a photo says more than an address.

Stove, damp and airing in an older building

If a stove heats the room, do not push the furniture up against it after cleaning. Direct heat makes leather dry out and deform, and latex also has to be protected from direct heat. It is better if the air moves: an open window, if the weather allows, and a door open to the next room.

The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) states that relative humidity above 80% in a room means a high risk of mould. If a corner in an old building is damp and the sofa stands right against that wall, cleaning will not solve the cause. Leave a small gap from the wall, deal with the cause of the damp, and only then clean.

Cleaning may reduce a smell that comes from the filling, but will not always remove it. More on this: smelly sofa: why, and what to do.

Velour on a new sofa: stain or pressure mark

Velour and chenille are pile fabrics. Pressure marks and differences in tone may appear on them in everyday use, and manufacturers state that this is a property of the fabric, not damage. So before velour sofa cleaning, look at the sofa from several sides: if a patch changes tone with the viewing angle, it may not be dirt.

The pile can be damaged by water, rubbing and pressure. On velour, gently blot liquid from the edges towards the centre without rubbing and, once the fabric is completely dry, lightly brush it in the direction of the pile. Send a photo taken in daylight.

Rental flats and rooms for students

The university complexes are right here, and if you let a flat or room in Torņakalns, a sofa and a mattress stay behind after the tenants. There is more on this work on the page furniture cleaning for rental flats.

A practical set is a sofa bed (from €40) together with mattress cleaning (single from €30, double from €45, one side; second side +50%). Write in the form when the flat is empty and who will be able to air it afterwards. If you see bed bugs on the mattress, that is a job for pest control, not cleaning.

If there is construction near the house and dust is building up on the sofa, it is better to plan cleaning after the main works, otherwise the dust will come back. More: furniture cleaning after renovation.

What we cannot promise

On old fabric that has lost its colour, cleaning will not bring the colour back. Water rings from earlier cleaning, grease marks on armrests and smells deep in the filling may stay. It is better to know this before ordering, so do ask.

Frequently asked questions

I let a flat near the university but live elsewhere. How do I arrange this?

Fill in the form, add photos of the state the previous tenant left the flat in, and write who will open the door. We agree the price before the work, but the question of keys is yours to decide.

We have a stove in the room. Should the sofa be dried next to it after cleaning?

No, do not put it close to the stove. Heat is not good for leather or latex. An evenly heated room and airing are better.

The sofa in our new flat has a removable cover. Should it be washed separately?

Not always. Washed separately, the cover may shrink or change colour and then no longer match the rest of the sofa. First check the label and send it to us.

Lighter patches are visible on our velour sofa. Are they stains?

Not always. With use, velour develops pressure marks and differences in tone, and that is a property of the fabric. Send a photo taken in daylight, and the specialist will tell you whether it is dirt.
