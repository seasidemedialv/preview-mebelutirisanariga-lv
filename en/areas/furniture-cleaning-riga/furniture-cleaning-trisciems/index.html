A mattress at home: why it must not be soaked

Mattress cleaning in Trīsciems depends on the mattress material. In foam, excess water can damage the structure or stay in the core, in a spring mattress damp can cause rust, and latex is harmed by direct sun and heat. So a mattress must not be soaked, and a latex mattress should be kept out of direct sun.

Before you send a request, read the manufacturer's care text. For example, TEMPUR states that this material must not be washed, sponged or dry-cleaned, so such a mattress suits only dry surface care, and for any other care you should ask the manufacturer first.

Also write in the form whether the mattress is used on one side or both. The second side costs +50%. There is more on working at your home on the page mattress cleaning at home.

If a child has wet the mattress or there is blood on the bed

A fresh stain is easier to treat than an old one, so the first minutes matter. Gently blot the liquid with a clean cloth without rubbing. For blood use only cold water, because heat can set the stain. Do not soak it, so that the liquid does not get into the filling.

Do not use chlorine or strong substances, because they can change the colour of the fabric. Once you have blotted the stain, air the room and send a photo. There is separate advice for these cases: child's urine on a mattress and blood stain on a mattress.

If the stain is small and fresh, this first aid is often enough, and you do not need us. If it has soaked in or a smell stays, send a photo.

After cleaning in a house by the woods

Woods and nearby water are part of Trīsciems, but that says nothing about the damp in your house. After cleaning, the main thing is the air in the room: the drier it is and the more it moves, the better. In damp weather moisture leaves fabric more slowly, so we do not promise a drying time.

Open a window, if the weather allows, and leave the door to the next room open. Do not cover the mattress with a sheet until it is completely dry. If you have a terrace, do not take the sofa cushions out into direct sun, and keep leather furniture away from heaters.

If the sofa stands in the relaxation room of a sauna, write in the form whether that room can be heated and aired, because fabric needs dry air after cleaning.

Child car seat and car seats at your house

A child car seat (from €20) has its own rules: for the straps and buckle, the seat's own instructions decide. Manufacturers warn that soaking and strong soaps may weaken the strap fibres. Send the instructions, if you have them. We make no claims about the seat's protective properties after cleaning.

For adult seats, see the page car seat cleaning, and child seats have a separate page, child car seat cleaning. One car seat costs from €12.

What may stay

A child's urine that has soaked into the mattress filling may leave a smell or a mark even after cleaning. Likewise, an old yellow ring and a blood stain treated with hot water may not disappear. The specialist will tell you what to expect.

Frequently asked questions

There is hardly any public transport. Do you come to Trīsciems?

Yes. Trīsciems is in Riga, and travel is included. Write in the form how to get there, especially if the access road is harder in some seasons.

Can a child car seat be cleaned on the same visit as the sofa?

Yes, in one order. Write the make of the seat and add a photo. For the straps and buckle, the manufacturer's instructions decide.

Does the mattress have to be lifted off the bed?

If one side is being cleaned, the mattress can stay on the bed. If both, it has to be turned over, so write how heavy it is and whether there is room by the bed.

Can a sofa and a mattress be cleaned on the same day if both are in one room?

They can, but think about where you will sleep, because both will be damp for some time. If possible, leave one for another time or prepare another place to sleep.
