Multi-storey residential buildings began to go up here in the 1950s, and the residential part lies in the centre and south-east of the neighbourhood. In a smaller flat one room is often the bedroom too, and then the sofa bed is used every day.

A sofa that is also a bed

If a sofa is pulled out every evening, it wears differently from an ordinary sofa. At night it serves as a mattress, by day as a sofa, and each role has its own load. The seat and the edge may turn shiny, and crumbs and dust gather under the folding part.

Before sofa bed cleaning, unfold it and take a photo of both parts. The bedding box may also hold dust, which can be vacuumed beforehand. Take out what is in the bedding box, so that the specialist has access.

A sofa bed costs from €40, so to reach the minimum you can add cushions (from €2 each) or a chair.

After cleaning, when you have to sleep in the same room

In a one-room flat you cannot simply leave the sofa after cleaning. Plan so that you have somewhere to sleep that evening, because the filling will not always be dry by then. How long it takes to dry depends on the humidity, ventilation, room temperature, fabric and filling, so we do not promise a number of hours.

Air the room with a through draught, if the windows allow it. If wind from the port side makes it hard to keep the window open, open it for shorter periods but more often. If you have a fan, that helps too. Do not put bed linen on a damp sofa.

Winter is no obstacle if the heating is on and the window can be opened briefly several times.

Faux leather and cheap fabric in a rental flat

If the flat has a faux leather sofa, there is no separate price for it: we count it as a fabric sofa, from €35 for a two-seater. The faux leather coating may peel over time, and cleaning cannot repair that. If it is already flaking, send a close-up, so you do not pay for work that solves nothing.

Cheaper fabric with foam has another problem: the seat sags. That is wear in the filling, not dirt, and cleaning will not put it right. The surface will get cleaner, but not firmer. More: faux leather sofa cleaning.

Fabric must not be made too wet. Too much water can leave a water ring or make the colour bleed.

Damp in the flat

On 31.05.2025 LSM wrote about a television programme in which it was said that every fifth building in Latvia has damp problems. That does not mean it applies to your flat in Vecmīlgrāvis, but it is worth checking if a corner is cold or the windows steam up.

If the sofa stands by an outside wall, leave a small gap. If there are dark dots on the fabric and you can smell mould, cleaning will not solve it. The cause of the damp has to be dealt with first, and the piece itself may have to be replaced. See mould in a sofa.

Between tenants

If you let a flat in Vecmīlgrāvis, read on the page furniture cleaning for rental flats what can and cannot be expected after tenants. Write in the form when the flat is empty and add photos.

If the previous tenants smoked indoors, expect the smell to be in the fabric and filling. In a study, cotton and polyester still held smoke substances 19 months after exposure. Cleaning may reduce the smell, but it may come back. More: cigarette smoke smell in a sofa. On the same visit you can request mattress cleaning in Vecmīlgrāvis: single from €30, double from €45.

If the sofa smells and is not valuable, consider whether to pay for cleaning. If the smell comes from the filling, cleaning may not help fully, and the specialist will tell you so from the photo and your description.

Frequently asked questions

I live on the fifth floor with no lift. Will the sofa have to be carried down?

No. The work is done in the flat, and the sofa stays where it is.

I have no other bed. Can I sleep on the sofa after cleaning?

Sleeping on damp filling is not advisable, and the drying time cannot be stated exactly in advance, so think about another place to sleep.

Will you also clean a used sofa I bought through an advert?

Yes. First see the page on used sofa cleaning. If you see insects or signs of them, that is a job for pest control.

The sofa sags and smells. Will it be like new after cleaning?

No. Cleaning may reduce the smell and clean the surface, but sagging foam will stay as it is. If the filling of a sofa is worn out, it is more honest to say so before the work.
