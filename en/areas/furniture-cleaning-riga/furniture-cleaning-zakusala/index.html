Office chair fabric: what to check

There is often a manufacturer's label on the underside of an office chair. Its code tells you whether water-based foam may be used (W), only a waterless solvent (S) or no liquid at all (X). The codes are voluntary, so some chairs do not have them. In that case the specialist assesses the fabric from a photo or on site.

A row of chairs often includes chairs of different years and batches, so one chair may differ. Mark the ones bought later. On coloured fabric too much moisture can make the colour bleed, so such a chair needs more caution than a plain grey one.

Leather chairs need different care from fabric chairs. The coating on faux leather may peel over time, and cleaning cannot repair that.

If the chairs have removable seat covers, do not wash them separately: they may shrink or change colour and then no longer match the rest.

In television and event spaces

In rooms used to a schedule, what matters is not the fabric but the time. If the hall's chairs must be free before the next day, say so in advance, because the drying time depends on the humidity, ventilation, temperature and material. We do not promise specific hours.

In a room with mechanical ventilation the air moves more evenly, but that is no promise of a specific time. If the ventilation switches off at night, mention it: the air then moves less.

Coffee, grease and wear

With office chairs, expect coffee. Gently blot a fresh stain with a clean cloth without rubbing, and do not use hot water. Then write what it has been treated with, if someone has tried. That helps the specialist.

Grease and oil on fabric stay for longer. If a colleague's chair has gone shiny on the armrests, that may be wear, and cleaning will not remove it. Fabric that has changed colour will not become new after cleaning.

Sofas in break areas

A break area sofa is used like a home sofa, only more people use it. A three-seater sofa is from €45, a corner sofa from €50. Coffee and food marks are a common case, so send a close-up.

If the sofa has a light fabric, do not plan cleaning right before a meeting or a visit from guests, because the sofa needs time for airing. Plan cleaning for a time when nobody is using it.
