Zasulauks is a residential and railway neighbourhood of Pārdaugava between Āgenskalns and Imanta, where new wooden buildings began to go up in the 1820s after the fire of 1812, and part of the area was divided into plots in the mid-19th century. An old building raises different questions from a new flat.

High rooms and older furniture

In an old wooden or brick building the ceilings may be higher, and the room may warm up more slowly. Furniture will then take longer to dry after cleaning, but that says nothing about your particular flat. With older furniture the extra question is the filling: it may not be only foam.

Before sofa cleaning in Zasulauks, send a photo from underneath or from a spot where the filling can be seen. If the piece is old, the care label is usually gone. The codes are voluntary, and without them the specialist has to assess the fabric by its appearance.

Stove and airing

If a stove heats the room, do not push the furniture up against it after cleaning: heat dries out and deforms leather, and it can set a stain. An even temperature and moving air are better.

Air the room when the outside air is drier. On a damp day fabric dries more slowly. If the windows face the railway or Kalnciema iela and the noise bothers you, open them for shorter periods but more often. In a room that stands empty, do not leave the cleaned sofa locked away, because the air does not move there.

Also check how the sofa stands against the wall. If there is a damp corner right behind it, leave a gap and deal with the cause of the damp first. Cleaning does not remove mould in the filling. On damp and furniture: mould in a sofa.

Stove heating does not get in the way of cleaning if the room is heated and can be aired. Write in the form how warm the room is and whether it is damp.

Water rings and old marks

Old furniture often has marks from earlier cleaning on the fabric. Too much water can move the fabric's finish and leave a ring, and not every ring disappears later. If the piece already has such rings, send a photo, because they are often irreversible.

Another point is removable covers. Do not wash them separately if you do not know what the label says: they may shrink or change colour and then no longer fit. Liquids must not be used at all on fabric with an X code, and that is better discussed before the work.

Near the railway and main roads, dust on fabric is no surprise. It can be vacuumed before cleaning, so that the dirt does not mix with moisture in the fabric.

Cold stairwell and work in the flat

Nothing has to be done in the cold stairwell, because the furniture stays in the flat. If the sofa stands in a tight spot between the wall and the bed, write that in the form and clear the space before the specialist arrives.

  2. Floor Which floor, and whether the building's front door has a code.
  3. Heating Whether the room has a stove and whether it is lit.
  4. Parking Where it is possible to stop nearby, because access by the railway and main roads may vary.

The biggest question: is old furniture worth cleaning

If the sofa is sound and the fabric is holding up, cleaning usually makes sense. If springs have come through, the filling has collapsed or the fabric is tearing, cleaning repairs nothing and may only bring out the wear.

If an older piece matters to you, send photos from several sides. The specialist will tell you what to expect, and you will decide whether it is worth it. More: what comes clean and what may stay.

What may stay on old furniture

Old furniture will not become new. Fading, worn armrest edges and the smell of an aged filling may stay. Smoke smell from a stove or cigarettes may lessen, but may come back. So send a photo, and the specialist will tell you before the work what to expect.

Smell treatment is from €15 extra, but we do not promise the result in advance.

Frequently asked questions

The sofa is older than I am. Can it be cleaned at all?

Often it can, but the fabric and filling have to be strong enough. Send a photo, and the specialist will tell you whether there is a risk.

We heat our room with a stove. Should we heat it more after cleaning?

An evenly heated room helps, but do not push the furniture up to the stove, and do air the room.

The stairwell is cold and there is a draught from it. Is that a problem?

Not for the work, because the furniture stays in the flat. But after cleaning it matters that the flat is warm and the air moves, not that cold, damp air comes in from the stairs.

The old sofa has dark rings from the previous cleaning. Will they come out?

Not always. Water rings may be permanent, because they come from the fabric's finish moving. Send a photo.
