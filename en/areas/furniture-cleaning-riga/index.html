Riga's neighbourhoods are very different, so the same sofa raises different questions in the centre and on a housing estate. Send a photo, and the specialist will tell you the price and what to expect from the cleaning.

One city, several kinds of home

Riga is not uniform: a dense historic centre, wide apartment districts, neighbourhoods of detached houses, office centres and the Daugava islands. You cannot judge a particular building from the name of the neighbourhood, but it is possible to say what usually needs thinking about in each.

  • Pre-war buildings, for example in Avoti, Grīziņkalns or Āgenskalns: high rooms, older upholstered furniture, a cold stairwell. Airing after cleaning matters more here than any hurry.
  • Housing estates, for example in Purvciems, Imanta, Zolitūde or Ķengarags: if there are few rooms, a sofa bed may also serve as a bed. The fabric then wears both on the seats and in the sleeping area, and different stains land on it by day and by night.
  • Neighbourhoods of detached houses, for example Mežaparks, Beberbeķi or Bukulti: one visit may cover several pieces, terrace cushions too, so the minimum order is less often an obstacle there.
  • Flats in Centrs and Vecrīga: homes, short lets, cafés and offices may be in one block, sometimes even in one building.

You will find your neighbourhood in the list above. Each page has more on what to bear in mind there.

Where parking matters in Riga

Paid zones R, A, B and C cover only part of the city: Vecrīga (R, A, B), Centrs (A, B, C), Avoti (B, C), Brasa (C), Grīziņkalns (C, also D) and the northern part of Maskavas forštate (B, C). In these places we ask you to state in the request where a car can stop: yard, gate, code, nearest permitted street.

Zone D is the rest of Riga, and it is payable only where road signs stand, for example in Ķīpsala and in individual stretches of Torņakalns and Ziepniekkalns. In Vecāķi the seasonal zone V applies from 1 May to 30 September. In other neighbourhoods the Rīgas Satiksme map shows no paid streets.

Riga City Council binding Regulations No. 206 (likumi.lv) and the Rīgas Satiksme parking map, checked 07.11.2026. Zones may change: check the road signs.

Tall buildings, offices and hotels

Riga has multi-storey residential and office buildings, including the “Saules akmens” high-rise in Ķīpsala. The floor changes nothing for cleaning, because the furniture does not have to be carried anywhere. Access matters more: whether a pass is needed, who opens the door, when the rooms are free.

For an office, hotel or café the unit prices are the same, and larger volumes are agreed separately. Start with office chair cleaning in Riga or hotel furniture in Riga. In the form, enter the number and type of fabric; that is enough for a first price.

After cleaning: air, not heat

How long furniture takes to dry depends on the humidity, ventilation, room temperature, fabric and filling, so we do not promise figures. Foam holds moisture for longer than fabric. If the weather allows, open a window and let the air move; a fan helps too.

In the high room of an old building with one window, the air moves differently from a through draught between two sides of a housing estate flat. If the flat tends to be damp, say so in the form. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) calls 40–60% comfortable humidity, and above 80% there is a high risk of mould. Direct heat harms leather furniture, so do not push a heater up to it.

Mould inside the upholstery is another question: it usually means that replacement has to be weighed up and the cause of the damp dealt with, and cleaning does not solve it.

Frequently asked questions

I live in a courtyard behind a gate in the centre. What should I write in the form?

Write how to get to the door: the gate code or bell, whether the entrance is from the yard or the street, and where a car can be left nearby. That makes it possible to arrange a time without needless messages.

Will I have to pay extra for travel on the edge of Riga?

No. Travel is included throughout Riga, including Bolderāja, Mangaļsala and other outlying neighbourhoods. The charge from €10 only starts beyond about 15 km outside Riga.

The tenant is moving out in a week. Can cleaning be arranged for a specific time?

In the form, write the date and whether the flat will be empty. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes and we settle the time together.

Can I order just one office chair?

You can, but the minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45. So for one chair from €10 it does not pay. Count how many chairs and sofas in the office are worth cleaning in one visit.
