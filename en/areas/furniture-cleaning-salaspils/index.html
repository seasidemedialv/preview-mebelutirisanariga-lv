Salaspils is close to Riga, about 4 km from its boundary, so travel is included. The main question here is not distance but what happens with an older sofa in a small room.

Separate housing estates, and yet one address

In the 1950s and 1960s Salaspils grew into a town with several separate housing estates. The Daugava, the railway and the A6 form a linear structure, and some parts of the town are linked by a longer route than it looks on the map.

This does not change the price, but it does change the request: enter the street and house number, and where a car can stop in the yard. There may be no space in an apartment building's yard, so a back-up street is useful too.

Older sofa, small room

A sofa chosen for a Soviet-layout room is usually just big enough to fit, and has served for a long time. Cleaning makes sense if the fabric is sound and the foam has not crumbled. On seats and armrests dirt usually comes out, but a worn-in seat stays worn in, because that is the shape of the furniture, not a stain.

Older sofas may no longer have labels. That is no obstacle, but it calls for more caution: care codes are voluntary, and without them as little moisture as possible has to be used, so that no water rings, shrinkage or colour changes appear.

The page fabric sofa cleaning in Salaspils says more about fabrics. If the sofa is small and the price is under the €45 minimum, see whether there is a pouffe, chair or cushions next to it.

Stairs, lift and damp

The lift and stairs do not matter for cleaning, because the furniture does not have to be carried. The air after the work matters more. In a one-room flat, where everything is in one room, open a window and, if possible, the door, to create an air flow. A fan helps too.

If the room tends to be damp, say so in advance. Comfortable humidity according to the Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) is 40–60%, and above 50% dust mites already do better. A small hygrometer can be bought cheaply, and it shows whether the problem is in the room rather than the furniture.

It is similar with a mattress: mattress cleaning at home in Salaspils is done on site, and afterwards it needs air. A single mattress starts from €30, and two together from €60.

Institutes, conference chairs, institutions

Salaspils has scientific institutes and commercial sites, and such institutions usually have a lot of furniture: chairs in the meeting room, sofas in the corridor, office chairs. The unit prices are the same, and larger volumes are agreed separately.

  • Conference room chair cleaning: if there are many chairs.
  • Office chairs: from €10.
  • School chair cleaning and kindergarten furniture.

Send one photo of a chair, the number, and when the room is free. Furniture needs time to dry, so cleaning between events is worth planning with time in hand.

Letting and selling

A flat in Salaspils may be let or sold, and in both cases the sofa is the first thing people notice. For a tenant the mattress matters, for a buyer the look and the smell. If the flat is being shown, do not clean the sofa the evening before a viewing: damp furniture will please nobody, and time for drying has to be allowed.

Choose what is closest to your case: furniture cleaning for rental flats, sofa cleaning before selling or furniture cleaning before moving in. For a flat after renovation, there is also furniture cleaning after renovation.

Cigarette smell is not so simple: it may cling to the fabric, and usually it can only be reduced.

If it is a detached house, not a flat

Salaspils also has areas of detached houses. There the task is different: several rooms, garden furniture cushions, a car with a child seat. So furniture cleaning in a private house is useful. All jobs on one visit are added together, and a corner sofa from €50 reaches the minimum on its own.

Price and an honest answer

Travel in Salaspils is included. The minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45. The price is affected by size, fabric, soiling and smell, and we agree it before the work. The full list is on the page prices.

Worn-in seats, old stains, urine smell and fabric wear may stay. If this can be seen in the photo, we say so in advance.

Frequently asked questions

Our neighbours want a sofa cleaned too. Can we send a request together?

Enter both addresses in one form. The specialist will tell you what can be combined, and each job is charged at the unit price.

A child has spilt juice on a light chair. Should I wait until evening?

No, gently blot it at once with a clean cloth without rubbing, and do not use hot water. If a mark stays, send a photo, and we will tell you the price.

The sofa has no label. What now?

The codes are voluntary, so this happens. Send a close-up of the fabric and tell us how old it is. The specialist will tell you what to expect.

I live in a one-room flat. Where do I sleep if the sofa is damp?

Think about another place to sleep, because sleeping on damp filling is not advisable. We do not promise a drying time.

An institute needs chairs cleaned outside working hours. Is that possible?

Write that in the form, and the specialist will tell you what can be arranged.
