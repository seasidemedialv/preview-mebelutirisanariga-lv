Sigulda is a major tourism centre in the Gauja valley, so one town brings together the family home and the room whose guests keep changing. Travel costs from €10, and we agree the price before the work.

A guest house: the time between guests

Sigulda is a place of tourism, sport and hospitality, and a guest house or short-let flat has its own problem: the furniture serves many people, but the free time between guests is short. Drying depends on the humidity, ventilation, temperature, fabric and filling, so we cannot promise how many hours will be enough.

It is more practical to plan cleaning for a time when a room stays free, and to do several rooms on one visit. In the form, enter the number of rooms, the mattress sizes and the types of sofa. The unit prices are the same, and larger volumes are agreed separately.

If a guest has noticed bed bugs, that is not a job for cleaning but for pest control, and it has to be ordered first.

What to write in a guest house request

For guest rooms the price can be given from photos, if enough is known. Useful details:

  • how many rooms and what furniture in each: sofas, armchairs, headboards;
  • mattress sizes, and whether one side or both need cleaning;
  • which rooms are free and for how many nights;
  • whether there is anything special: a cat in the room, a smoker, a new stain;
  • who will let the specialist in and how to get there.

Photos of one typical room and close-ups of the worst spots are enough. With fabric of one colour there is no need to photograph every chair.

Wine, coffee and cream on a guest sofa

In guest rooms, stains happen in the evening and are found in the morning. The rule for staff is simple: blot gently with a clean cloth without rubbing, do not soak and do not use hot water. A wine stain can be lightly wiped with cold water and blotted again.

The label tells you what liquid the fabric can take at all, so keep the furniture's care information. Greasy cream is a different thing from wine, and water is usually not enough for it. A photo will show what to expect: wine and coffee stain on a sofa, grease stain on a sofa.

A detached house on a slope

The terrain of the Gauja valley, the bridges and event traffic may substantially affect local routes, and Sigulda differs from the flat suburbs of Riga. If the house is on a slope or by a gravel road, say so in the form and state where a car can be parked. If there is an event in town on that very day, it is better to mention it.

In a detached house it is easier to combine several items on one visit. A corner sofa from €50 and an armchair from €20 together cost from €70. One two-seater sofa from €35 does not yet reach the minimum order on its own, so it pays to add another piece. More: furniture cleaning in a private house in Sigulda.

Sport, wet clothes and the car

In a sports town, car seats and child seats get their share: damp clothes, mud on a child's boots. A car seat starts from €12, a child car seat from €20. For a child seat's straps and buckle the instructions decide: manufacturers warn that soaking and strong soaps may weaken the fibres. We promise nothing about the child seat's properties after cleaning: that is for the manufacturer to determine. See car seat cleaning in Sigulda.

A damp sports bag on the sofa or a wet towel on the mattress is no great misfortune if you notice in time. Take it off and air the spot. Surface mustiness is usually an easier job than an old stain.

A flat in Sigulda

In apartment buildings the questions are simpler: the furniture stays in the flat, the stairs are no problem, and free space around the sofa and an open window after the work are enough. Frequently asked questions

Someone has smoked in the room. Will cleaning be enough?

Usually the smell can be reduced, but not always removed, because smoke traces cling to fabric and other surfaces. Cleaning applies only to textiles.

How many rooms have to be done to get a volume price?

There is no fixed number. Enter how many rooms and what furniture, and the specialist will tell you the price.

The road to the house runs down a slope. Is that a problem?

Not if a car can stop there. Say so in the form, and we will work out the best way to get access.

Can a sofa be cleaned between two guests on the same day?

Better not to count on that. If the next guest arrives on the same day, the fabric may be damp. Plan cleaning for a time when the room stays free.
