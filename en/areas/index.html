What differs between neighbourhoods

Furniture is cleaned the same way, but the home changes what you need to bear in mind. In panel-block housing estates, such as Purvciems, Pļavnieki, Imanta and Ķengarags, one room often serves as both living room and bedroom, and the sofa bed is used every day. Space is short, and airing after cleaning matters.

In pre-war wooden and brick buildings, as in Āgenskalns, Grīziņkalns and Avoti, ceilings may be higher and rooms cooler, and furniture may take longer to dry. In neighbourhoods of private houses, such as Mežaparks, Bieriņi and Berģi, and in villages in the Riga region, several pieces can often be combined in one visit, and then the minimum order is not a problem.

These are general observations about the buildings, not about a particular house. One neighbourhood can be very varied, so give the floor in the form and briefly describe where the furniture stands.

The centre, offices and short lets

In central Riga and in mixed areas, flats sit alongside offices, cafés and guest flats. If cleaning is needed between guests or outside working hours, say in the form when the premises are free, and we will agree the time when we speak. For businesses: furniture cleaning for businesses.

Paid parking in Riga

The paid zones with the letters R, A, B and C are in Vecrīga, Centrs, Avoti, Brasa, Grīziņkalns and the northern part of Maskavas forštate. If you live in these places, say in the form where a car can be parked. It helps us agree the time.

Frequently asked questions

Is the price in the Riga region the same as in Riga?

The price of the job is the same. Only travel differs: it is included up to about 15 km outside Riga; further out it is from €10.

My address is not on the list. Can you come?

The list has the largest neighbourhoods and towns. Enter the address in the request, and when we speak we will tell you whether we can come and what travel costs.

Do the services differ on the place pages?

No, the services and prices are the same. The place pages show what housing is most common there and what to know before the job.

Why are there two rules in Jūrmala?

Jūrmala is long. Bulduri is about 11 km from the Riga boundary, but Sloka, Kauguri and Ķemeri are more than 15 km away, so travel there costs from €10.
