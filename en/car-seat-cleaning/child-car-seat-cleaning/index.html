The straps and buckle are a separate matter: they are safety parts, so follow the seat's manual for them. We make no claims about the seat's safety or compliance after cleaning. A child car seat costs from €20, minimum order €45.

The manual decides what may be cleaned

Every seat has its own rules, and you need to check them in the manual for your model. If the paper manual is gone, it can usually be found on the manufacturer's website by model name. Here is what three manufacturers say on their official pages:

Manufacturer Cover Straps and buckle
Britax According to the label; many are hand-washed in cold water and dried flat or on a line. Straps are wiped with lukewarm water and mild soap, not immersed. The buckle is rinsed under lukewarm running water, without soap or solvents.
Cybex According to the manual; fabric parts are usually washed separately, in cold water, on a gentle cycle. Straps are wiped with a cloth, water and a mild detergent.
Maxi-Cosi According to the label or manual. Fixed straps only with a soft cloth or sponge and lukewarm water.

These are the manufacturers' general instructions, checked 07.11.2026. They do not replace the manual for your seat.

Why the straps and buckle must not be cleaned like fabric

Britax warns that immersing straps or using harsh soap may weaken the fibres, while soap, solvents and lubricants may affect how the buckle works. So cleaning the straps and buckle is not part of a “deep clean”: it is done only as the specific manual allows.

If a strap is worn or heavily soiled, Britax advises replacing it rather than trying to clean it. Dirt may also interfere with how the seat works, so check that the buckle works, following the manual. If you have doubts about the seat's condition, ask the manufacturer or seller.

Does dry cleaning suit a child car seat

People use the phrase “child car seat dry cleaning” widely, but they usually mean any cleaning by a specialist. In the literal sense, dry cleaning is not allowed for some seats: Britax in Australia states that covers must not be dry-cleaned or bleached. So check the label: a crossed-out circle means this treatment must not be used. Letters in a circle indicate processes for professional textile care, while lines under the symbol call for gentler treatment.

What to prepare before cleaning

  • Find the manual or enter the model name in the form.
  • Take photos of the seat, the stain, the cover label and the straps.
  • Write down what was spilt and how long ago.
  • Take the seat out of the car.
  • If you remove the cover, first take a photo of how the straps are threaded.

After cleaning, the cover and straps go back exactly as shown in the manual. Check this before the first journey.

What to expect from cleaning

Usually comes out

  • crumbs and dirt in the fabric parts
  • fresher drink and food stains on the cover
  • some of the smell from the fabric

May stay

  • colour changes and old stains
  • milk or urine smell in the upholstery
  • dirty straps that the manual does not allow you to clean
  • wear and damage

Moisture may change the fabric, so Britax advises drying it flat or on a line to prevent shrinking. More on the limits of cleaning: what comes out and what may stay.

Frequently asked questions

Will you certify after cleaning that the seat can be used as before?

No. We give no certification of the seat's safety or compliance. Cleaning concerns the fabric and the dirt, not the seat's protective properties.

When can the seat be used again?

When the cover and upholstery are completely dry and the cover has been fitted according to the manual. We do not promise a time in advance, because drying depends on air humidity and the fabric.

Does the seat need to come out of the car?

Usually yes, so that all the fabric parts can be reached. Afterwards, refit it as shown in the manual.

There is dried juice in the buckle. What should I do?

See the manual. Britax advises rinsing the buckle under lukewarm running water, without soaking it and without soap or lubricants. If the buckle still works poorly, ask the manufacturer.
