Seats only, not the whole interior

We say this clearly to avoid misunderstandings. Full car interior cleaning is a different service, and we do not offer it. We clean seats, child car seats and prams. If you need the whole interior done, this is not the right page.

Seat cleaning suits a specific problem: a stain on the passenger seat, a dirty back seat or a child car seat after a child's meal. The car does not have to go anywhere. The specialist works at your place, so write in the form where the car is parked: in a yard, a garage or a car park.

Usually comes out

  • Everyday dirt on fabric seats
  • Fresh coffee and food stains
  • Dust and hair on the surface of the fabric
  • Stains that have not soaked into the foam

May stay

  • An old stain set by heat
  • Resin, tar or ink, which are hard to judge from a photo
  • A smoking smell, which may come back from the filling
  • Worn leather, cracks and colour changes

Fabric seats

A car seat is built on foam, so the furniture rules apply here too: too much liquid may stay in the foam, and drying takes longer. How a seat dries depends on humidity and airing, so after the job open the windows or doors if the weather allows. We do not promise a number of hours.

Blot a stain with a clean cloth first, do not rub it and do not pour hot water on it. If the car's manual has instructions on seat care, they take priority, so tell the specialist.

Leather car seats

Leather calls for caution: direct water can damage most leathers, heat dries and distorts it, and stains soak into the leather itself. More detail: leather car seat cleaning. If the leather is worn or cracked, cleaning does not repair it: that is leather repair, not cleaning.

Child car seat: straps according to the seat's manual

Cleaning a child car seat is different from cleaning a car's own seats. The straps and buckle are safety parts, so they are not deep-cleaned and are looked after only as the manual for that particular seat allows. This is usually spot cleaning.

Britax writes that soaking and harsh soaps can weaken the strap fibres, and that soaps and oils can affect how the buckle works. Cybex and Maxi-Cosi also point to the manual and to care with a cloth or sponge. So send us the model of the seat and, if possible, the manual.

The removable cover is cared for according to its label or the manual. If a strap is frayed or very dirty, Britax recommends replacing it. We make no statement about the safety of a child car seat after cleaning: that is a question for the manufacturer. More detail: child car seat cleaning.

Pets, smoking and selling a car

Hair can usually be removed from the surface of the fabric, but urine and vomit may have soaked into the foam, and the smell may stay. Smoke residue stays in the fabric and filling, and the effect of cleaning may be short-lived; besides, the roof lining and panels are not our job.

Before a car is sold, seat cleaning can improve the look, but it is not an interior valet. If you need just one stain on a seat cleaned, send a photo, and the specialist will tell you whether it is worth it.

Prams

A pram costs from €20. We clean the fabric, but the mechanism, wheels and brakes are not part of this job. If the pram has a care label or manual, photograph it. More detail: pram cleaning.

Car seat dry cleaning: what it means

In everyday speech, car seat dry cleaning means cleaning on the spot, not handing the seat in at a dry cleaner's. The seat does not have to be taken out of the car. Car seat cleaning at home means that the specialist comes to you, not that you have to take anything anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Do you clean the whole car interior?

No. We clean only seats, child car seats and prams. The roof lining, floor, panels and boot are not our job.

Do the seats have to be taken out of the car?

No, the seats stay in the car. All that is needed is a place where the doors can be opened and the seats reached.

Can you wash the straps of a child car seat?

No, the straps must not be immersed, so follow the seat's manual for them. It usually allows only spot wiping with a cloth. If a strap is frayed or very dirty, the manufacturer recommends replacing it.

Will a smoking smell come out of the seats?

It may lessen, but we do not promise that. Smoke residue stays in the fabric and filling, and the effect of cleaning may be short-lived. The rest of the car interior is not our job either, so the smell may also come from elsewhere.
