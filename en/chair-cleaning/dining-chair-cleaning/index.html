What is special about a dining chair

At the table, food, grease and drinks land on the seat, especially in homes with children. A padded seat cannot be turned over like a cushion, so stains stay visible even though the chair is still sound.

How the chair is built affects the price. A chair with only a padded seat is a simpler job than one with an upholstered backrest. So send one photo from the front and one from the side, and say how many chairs are in the set. The label, if there is one, is usually under the seat.

Stains from the table: what to do straight away

Stain First step What to expect
Wine blot, sponge with cool water, do not soak fresh often fades, old may stay
Coffee, tea blot, check the label often fades; depends on the fabric
Grease, sauces blot, do not use solvent may stay; solvent can damage the foam
Blood cold water only hot water sets the stain

The general rule: blot without rubbing and never use hot water, as it can set the stain. Do not wet the seat, so that liquid does not reach the foam. More detail: wine and coffee stains on a sofa and grease stain on a sofa.

Faux leather and wooden chairs

Faux leather chairs have no separate price: the dining chair price applies. If a stain is fresh and small, wiping it with a damp cloth is often enough. The coating can peel over time, and cleaning cannot repair that.

On a wooden chair with a padded seat we clean the padded part; we do not restore the wood. If the seat fabric is worn, it may need reupholstering.

What comes out of dining chairs and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • fresh food and drink stains
  • greyness and darker patches on seats
  • crumbs and dust in the seams

May stay

  • old grease stains
  • stains that have already been rubbed or heated
  • worn fabric and tears
  • peeling faux leather

If chairs have been in use at the table for years, the darkest patches are usually the front edge of the seat and the top of the backrest. These often get lighter, but they may not fully match the untouched fabric.

Frequently asked questions

Do the chairs have to be brought to one place?

Say in the form where the chairs stand and whether they can be put side by side. Agree the rest with the specialist when they get in touch.

Can just the seat be cleaned, without the backrest?

Often yes, but the clean seat may then differ from the backrest. A photo will help you decide whether it is worth doing.

How long will the chairs be out of use?

That depends on the fabric, filling, humidity and ventilation, so we do not promise an exact time. Tell the specialist if you need the chairs on a particular day, for example for guests.

Can I unscrew the chair seat and wash it?

The seat can often be removed, but it should not be washed. Overwetting can leave a ring on the fabric and wet the foam, which is hard to dry.
