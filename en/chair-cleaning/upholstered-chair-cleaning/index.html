The fabric matters more than the shape of the chair

Two identical chairs in different fabrics can be completely different jobs. So the most important photos are a close-up of the fabric and the label under the seat, if there is one.

  • Velour and velvet: water and pressure can crush the viscose pile for good; this kind of fabric must not be rubbed.
  • Chenille: a pile fabric in which pressure marks and differences in shade are normal.
  • Cotton and linen: natural fibres react to moisture; sources differ on linen, so the label decides.
  • Faux leather: a coating that can peel over time; cleaning cannot repair that.
  • Microfibre: the label decides; there is no general rule.

If there is no label, the general advice is to test in a hidden spot first or to ask a specialist. Velour and faux leather chairs have no separate price: the fabric chair price applies.

Velour chairs: pressure marks are not dirt

Velour chairs often show lighter and darker areas, especially on the seat. Manufacturers call this a property of the fabric, not damage. It looks like a stain, yet cleaning does not remove it. If that is the only thing bothering you, send a photo: it may turn out that cleaning is not needed.

If liquid has been spilt on velour, blot it from the outer edge inwards and do not rub. Once the fabric is completely dry, lightly brush the pile in its direction.

What comes out of upholstered chairs and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • dust and household dirt on the fabric
  • darker patches on the backrest and seat
  • fresher stains on hard-wearing fabric

May stay

  • pressure marks and differences in shade in velour
  • worn fabric and pulled threads
  • old stains
  • a water ring, if the fabric was overwetted in the past

Frequently asked questions

Can you clean a chair with a removable cover?

Yes, but the cover does not always need to come off. Washing it separately can shrink it or change its colour. Check the label first and send a photo.

Can a pouffe be cleaned together with chairs or a sofa?

Yes, and that usually works out better. A pouffe costs from €10, but on its own it does not reach the minimum order. If the pouffe has a cover, photograph the label: it decides what may be done.

Can you clean just one chair?

Yes, but you will pay the minimum order of €45. It is usually combined with a second chair, a sofa or a mattress.

Why has my chair gone blotchy after cleaning it at home?

Often it is a water ring: the finish of overwetted fabric shifts and stays behind as a ring. Sometimes it can be reduced, but we cannot promise that. Before trying again, send a photo.
