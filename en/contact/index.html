What to put in the request

  • Address: street and house number, town, floor.
  • What needs cleaning: a sofa, chairs, a mattress, another piece of upholstered furniture or car seats, and how many.
  • Size: two-seater, corner, single or double.
  • Material, if you know it: fabric, velour, leather or faux leather.
  • The problem: a stain, a smell, urine, and how long ago it happened.
  • What the stain has already been treated with, if anything.
  • When you need the job done and where a car can be parked.

What photo to send

A photo is optional, but with one we can give a more exact price. Three shots are useful: the whole piece of furniture, the stain up close and the care label, which is usually under the seat cushion. More detail: how to photograph a sofa for a price. The photo does not need to show people or documents.

Where we work

We work in Riga and the Riga region: all places. Travel is included in Riga and up to about 15 km outside Riga; further out it is from €10. If you are not sure whether your address falls within this area, give it in the request. For prices, see the prices page.

Frequently asked questions

Why is there no phone number?

We take requests only through the form, so that no detail gets lost and the specialist can reply with a specific price. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes. In the description you can write when it suits you to take a call.

What should I write if I do not know the type of fabric?

Write what you see and add a photo, for example “soft grey fabric”. A photo of the label helps even more. The specialist will find out the rest.

Can I write the request in Russian or English?

Yes. You can write your request in Latvian, Russian or English.

Can I request furniture cleaning for someone else?

Yes. Give the address where the furniture is and the phone number of the person the time should be agreed with, if they have consented to that.
