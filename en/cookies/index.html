Cookies

This page describes what cookies the site mebelutirisanariga.lv uses and how you can manage them. We do not use advertising cookies on the site and do not build visitor profiles for advertising.

Prepared by Mēbeļu Tīrīšana Rīga, updated 7 November 2026. Sources: 1

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a site saves in your browser. It lets the site remember, for example, your choice about cookies. Some cookies disappear when you close the browser; others stay for a set time. The browser's local storage works in a similar way, and the same rules apply to it.

What cookies we use

At present the site mebelutirisanariga.lv does not use cookies: there are no statistics, advertising or social media tools, and the request form works without cookies. That is why no cookie notice is shown on the site.

If we add visit statistics in future, they will work only with your consent, a cookie notice with the option to change your choice will appear on the site, and this page will be updated.

Storing information on a user's device is permitted with the user's consent; consent is not needed if storage is necessary to provide a service the user has requested. Source: Law on Information Society Services, Section 7.¹ (likumi.lv). Checked 03.11.2026

Photos added to the form

If you add photos to the form, your browser briefly saves them in this tab's session storage so that they are not lost while you take a picture with your phone camera. This data stays on your device until you send the form or close the tab, and it is not used for any other purpose. After sending, the site deletes it. This storage is necessary for the form to work as you requested, so consent is not required for it.

How to manage cookies in your browser

In all the most common browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge), the privacy settings let you:

  • view saved cookies;
  • delete all or individual cookies;
  • block cookies from particular sites or all sites;
  • set cookies to be deleted when you close the browser.

Personal data

How we process the details and photos given in the form is described on the privacy policy page.

Changes

We update this page if the technical solutions used on the site change. Version: 07.11.2026
