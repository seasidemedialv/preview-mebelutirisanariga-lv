What sets a banquet chair apart from a conference chair

A conference chair wears down gradually, but a banquet chair gets everything at once in a single evening: wine, sauce, cream and greasy fingermarks. The stains are uneven. Two chairs in a row may be clean and the third may have a red ring on the seat.

The second difference is storage. A chair is stacked straight after the event, and the stain stays in the fabric until the next booking. The older the stain, the lower the chances of removing it. So after an event it is worth looking the chairs over rather than carrying them straight to storage.

  • Before stacking: look over the seats and set aside the chairs with stains.
  • Fresh wine: blot gently with a clean cloth without rubbing, dampen with cool water and do not soak the fabric.
  • Grease: blot gently without rubbing and do not use solvents, as they can damage the foam.
  • Note which chairs have been treated and with what.

Do not stack damp chairs

If the fabric is damp, the chairs must stand separately until they are dry. Stacked chairs do not air well, and moisture may stay in the foam for longer. So before work starts, think about where the chairs can stand separately until the fabric dries.

How long they need to stand depends on air humidity, temperature, ventilation, the fabric and the filling. We do not promise a number of hours. Leave a margin before the next event.

What to put in the form

  • The number of chairs, the model and whether they are all of one type.
  • Photos: one chair, a close-up of the seat, the most stained chairs, the label.
  • Where the chairs stand: in the hall or in storage, and how much space is available for the work.
  • When the next event is and when the chairs are needed.
  • Whether the chairs have fabric covers and what kind.

If the chairs have decorative fabric covers, mention it. The specialist will tell you whether they are part of this work and how they should be cared for according to the label.

Fabric, faux leather, covers

If the chairs are faux leather, bear in mind that the coating may peel over time, and cleaning cannot repair that. So photograph the edges where the chairs rub when stacked. For fabric chairs, the care code sets the cleaning method, if there is one. Washing upholstery covers separately is usually not recommended, as the fabric may shrink and the colour may differ.

What to expect after cleaning

Usually comes out

  • Fresh wine and coffee stains, if not treated with hot water
  • Food marks on the seat surface
  • Everyday dirt

May stay

  • An old red wine stain, especially in light fabric
  • Grease that has soaked into the foam
  • Rub marks from stacking
  • Cracked faux leather

The history of a stain matters: if you know what was spilt and whether anyone has tried to clean it, tell us. On red wine: wine and coffee stains.

Frequently asked questions

Only 20 of our 300 chairs are dirty. Is it worth cleaning them?

Yes, if you know which ones they are. Set them aside and tell us the number. Bear in mind that the cleaned chairs may look different from the rest if they have not been cleaned for a long time.

Can the chairs be brought to you?

No. We clean chairs at the client's premises in Riga and the Riga region.

Can the chairs be stacked straight after the work?

Only when they are completely dry. Damp fabric dries slowly in a stack, and that is not advisable. Agree the place with the specialist.

Can you clean between two events in the same week?

Agree that with the specialist. Tell us the dates of both events and the number of chairs, and the specialist will say whether it is realistic.
