It is not disinfection, and we do not promise to meet a clinic's hygiene requirements. A dining chair costs from €7, an office chair from €10. Tell us when the rooms are free and add photos: the specialist will give you the price and say what you can expect.

What we do and what we do not do

A clinic's own requirements govern how its premises and treatment are run. Our work is fabric and upholstery: it reduces dirt, but it does not replace the institution's hygiene procedure. If you have written requirements for specific rooms, add them to your request, and the specialist will tell you what can be done and what cannot.

  • Waiting room: upholstered chairs in rows and sofas used by many people.
  • Reception: chairs for staff and visitors.
  • Consulting rooms: doctors' and consultation chairs, if they are upholstered.
  • Treatment rooms: here we first need to establish whether the furniture is our kind of work at all. Tell us about these separately.

Treatment tables and couches often have a wipe-clean cover. We have no general rule for such covers: the manufacturer's instructions and the institution's requirements decide.

How a clinic is different

An office chair is used by one person, but a waiting room chair by dozens of people a day, so everyday dirt is spread evenly. The stains differ here too: blood and other liquids. A fresh blood stain is treated with cold water, as heat sets it, so staff should not use hot water.

The rooms are usually busy all week, so the work has to fit the appointment schedule. Tell us when there are no appointments. If a room cannot be closed, say which part can be free.

What to put in the form

  • A list of rooms and the amount of furniture in each.
  • Photos: the furniture, the material, the label, stains.
  • When the rooms are free of patients.
  • Whether the institution has written requirements for work on the premises, so that the specialist can say whether they can be met.
  • Address, entrance and contact person.

The label and several branches

On waiting room chairs the label is often under the seat. The code sets whether water-based foam or a water-free solvent may be used on the fabric, and with code X only vacuuming and light brushing remain. The codes are voluntary, so some chairs do not have one.

If the clinic has several branches, split the list by address. That way the specialist sees what fits into one visit and how to coordinate the appointment schedules.

What a clinic can expect

Usually comes out

  • Everyday dirt on seats
  • A greyish look on chair armrests
  • Fresher stains, if not treated with hot water

May stay

  • Old blood stains that have set in the fabric
  • Smell, if the liquid has reached the filling
  • Cracks in a wipe-clean cover
  • Microorganisms: cleaning is not disinfection

Cleaning is not a health measure. It does not treat allergy and does not promise that no microorganisms or mites will remain in the fabric. More on stains: blood stain on a mattress.

Contract and documents

If the clinic needs a contract or specific documents, say so in your request, and the specialist will tell you whether that is possible. We do not publish fixed terms on this page.

Frequently asked questions

Will the furniture be disinfected after cleaning?

No. We clean upholstery and do not promise disinfection. If the institution needs it, that is handled separately.

Do you clean dental or gynaecological chairs?

We do not promise that here. They are not ordinary upholstered furniture, and they are subject to manufacturer and institution requirements. Send a photo and the specialist will say whether it is our kind of work.

Can you work with patients present?

Better not. Tell us when the rooms are free. If such a time is hard to find, agree it with the specialist.

Can you clean the chairs in just one department?

Yes, a request can cover one room or department. The minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45.
