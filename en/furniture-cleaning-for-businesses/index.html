What to expect from furniture used every day

In a business one piece of furniture serves many people, and dirt forms in the same places: the middle of the seat, the armrests and the top edge of the backrest. Cleaning usually freshens these areas well. It does not, however, restore worn fabric, and after years of use a seat will not look as it did when new.

Usually comes out

  • Everyday dirt and dark patches on seats
  • Fresh food and drink stains
  • Dust on the surface of the fabric
  • A more even look across the room

May stay

  • Wear and faded fabric
  • Old grease stains that have soaked into the upholstery
  • Ink and marker on light fabric
  • A smell from the filling, such as smoke or urine

We say what may stay before the work starts. That matters if the result will later be judged by a manager, a guest or an owner. If a piece of furniture is no longer worth cleaning, it is better to find that out from a photo than after the job. Then you can decide what to clean, what to replace and how big the job is.

Offices, hotels and restaurants: what differs

In an office it is usually the chairs and the sofas in the meeting area that need cleaning. On an office chair the seat and armrests suffer most, and the rules are set by the manufacturer's care instructions, if there are any.

In a hotel or hostel the main items are mattresses, armchairs and headboards. A mattress must not be soaked, so plan room occupancy to let it dry after cleaning. In a restaurant or café, food and oil end up on the fabric. A grease stain is harder to deal with than a drink stain, and solvents can damage foam upholstery, so an old stain may stay.

An office contract and regular cleaning

Does your office need a contract or regular cleaning? Say so in your request, and the specialist will tell you what can be arranged. We do not publish fixed terms here, because they depend on the number of pieces and on how often cleaning is really needed.

The number and type of pieces often matter more than the name of the room. An office with 40 identical chairs and a restaurant with upholstered benches are different jobs, so we give the price from photos, not by sector.

What to prepare before the job

  • A list of the furniture: type, number and approximate size.
  • Photos: one of each type of furniture and the dirtiest spot up close.
  • The label or the manufacturer's care instructions, if available.
  • The times when the premises are free.
  • Where a car can be parked and how to get into the building.

After cleaning, the furniture has to dry, and that depends on humidity, airing, temperature, the fabric and the filling. So plan the cleaning for a time when the furniture is not needed straight away. More detail: how long a sofa takes to dry after cleaning.

What cleaning does not do for a business

Cleaning is not disinfection, and we do not call it that. For a clinic, salon or kindergarten this line matters: if you need a provable hygiene process, that is a separate job. Nor do we claim anything about a fabric's suitability for children or patients after cleaning, because we cannot prove it.

Bedbugs in a hotel or hostel are not a cleaning matter: they are a pest control job, and the US Environmental Protection Agency recommends bringing in a pest control professional early. Mould in upholstery means the furniture has to be assessed and the cause of the damp dealt with. We do not clean carpets, so for office flooring look for another service.

Frequently asked questions

Is the price for a business different from the price for a home?

The unit prices are the same, but for larger volumes we agree the price separately. We do not publish discount percentages, because they would be invented figures: the price depends on your furniture.

Can you work when the office is empty?

Say in the request when the premises are free, and we will agree the time when we speak.

Can you clean only some of the chairs?

Yes. The minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45. Even so, clean chairs look different next to dirty ones, so it is usually worth assessing the whole set.

Do you clean leather and faux leather office chairs?

Yes, send a photo. Cleaning does not repair a peeling faux leather coating, and the specialist will tell you which chairs are worth cleaning.
