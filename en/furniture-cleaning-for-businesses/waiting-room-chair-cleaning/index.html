A room that is never really empty

An office chair can be taken away when the employee is on holiday. In a waiting room the seats are needed for as long as the institution is open. So the simplest plan is to clean outside visitor hours or in parts, leaving the other row in use.

If the chairs are linked on a shared frame, they cannot be moved one at a time. A photo will show how many seats there are in a block and whether the block is fixed down.

  • Garage: dark marks on seats and backs from work clothes and road dirt.
  • Bank and institution: many people for a short time, dirt evenly spread.
  • Sports club and swimming pool: wet bags and towels, so smell too.
  • Clinic: there is a separate page on this, clinic furniture cleaning, as the requirements there are different.

What to put in the form

  • The number of seats and how they are arranged: separate chairs, blocks of 3 or 4, sofas.
  • Photos: one row, a close-up of one seat, the worst spot, the label if there is one.
  • When the room is closed and which part can be out of use at a time.
  • Material: fabric, faux leather, leather or a wipe-clean cover.
  • Address, floor and how to get into the building outside visitor hours, if such work is possible.

Fabric or a wipe-clean cover

If the seats are faux leather or have a wipe-clean cover, its condition matters most. A faux leather coating may peel over time, and cleaning cannot repair that, so photograph any cracks. For wipe-clean covers such as PVC we have no general rule: the manufacturer's instructions decide.

For fabric, the care code sets the cleaning method. The codes are voluntary, so in a waiting room they often cannot be found. If there is none, the specialist assesses the fabric from photos or on site.

If you need the price for comparison

An institution or company often needs the price before anyone makes a decision. Say so in the form. From the number of seats and the photos the specialist can give a price, and you then have time to get it approved.

What to do about a stain during working hours

If something is spilt on a chair, blot it gently with a clean cloth, without rubbing, and do not pour hot water on it. Then put a sign on the chair or move it aside so that nobody sits on it while the fabric is damp. This is a simple way to keep liquid from being pressed into the filling.

What you can usually expect

Usually comes out

  • An everyday greyish look on seats
  • Dust in seams and between blocks
  • Fresh stains

May stay

  • Dark marks from work clothes that have rubbed into the fabric
  • Old stains
  • Peeling faux leather
  • Wear and tears

On an evenly dirty row, the result usually shows better than on one old stain. More: what may stay after cleaning.

Frequently asked questions

Can you clean while the room is in use?

That depends on the room and on how many seats you are left with. Tell us which part can be cleared and agree it with the specialist.

Our chairs are bolted together. Do they need to be taken apart?

Usually not. Send a photo and the specialist will say whether all the seats in the row can be reached.

Is it worth cleaning just one row?

It is possible, but next to a cleaned row the dirty one may look darker. Ask the specialist what they would advise from the photos.

Does it make sense to clean chairs with a wipe-clean surface?

If the surface is intact, routine cleaning is often enough. If it is cracked, cleaning will not repair it.
