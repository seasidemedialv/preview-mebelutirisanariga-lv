We do not promise that everything will come out

Furniture cleaning at home usually removes everyday dirt, dark patches on seats and fresh stains well. Even so, not every stain comes out and not every smell goes. An old stain that has been rubbed many times, or urine that has reached the foam, may leave traces even after careful cleaning.

So we tell it as it is. The fabric, the age of the stain and whatever it has already been treated with decide the result more than any promise. A specialist tells you, from a photo or on the spot, what you can expect, and you decide whether to order the job. There is more on this on the page what comes out and what may stay.

There are also things cleaning does not do. It does not cure allergies, does not destroy mould inside foam and does not remove bedbugs. Bedbugs and fleas are a job for pest control, while mould in the filling of a mattress or sofa often means the piece has to be replaced and the cause of the damp dealt with.

The care label says how the fabric may be cleaned

On a lot of upholstered furniture the care label is hidden under the seat cushions or on the underside. It may carry a letter: W means water-based foam and sparing moisture, S means cleaning without water, WS allows both, and X means no liquids, only vacuuming and light brushing. Manufacturers give these codes voluntarily, so some furniture has none.

The label matters because too much water on the wrong fabric can leave a ring, shrink the fabric or make the colour run, and moisture can reach the filling. Photograph the label along with the sofa if you can find it. If there is no label, tell the specialist.

If the stain is fresh

First aid is simple. Gently blot the stain with a clean cloth or paper towel, without rubbing. If needed, lightly dampen the cloth in cold water, but do not soak the fabric, as the liquid can reach the filling. Hot water, a hairdryer and an iron can set the stain, especially blood. Chlorine bleach can change the colour.

If the stain is small and fresh, that is often enough. If something remains, photograph the stain and send it to us. More detail: stains on a sofa and stains on a mattress.

Urine, pets and smells

A urine stain is the typical case where the honest answer is “it depends”. If the urine has stayed in the fabric, the result is often good. If it has soaked into the foam, the smell may stay or come back after a while, and we say so before the job. Cat specialists note that the smell area can be up to three times larger than the visible stain.

Sofas and mattresses have separate pages: cleaning urine from a sofa and mattress cleaning after urine. A smoking smell can usually be reduced, but not always removed: cigarette smoke smell in a sofa.

What to prepare before the specialist's visit

  • Clear the space around the furniture and take blankets and personal items off it.
  • Say what the stain has already been treated with. This helps to judge what may happen to the fabric.
  • If the mattress or sofa is wet, blot it with a dry, clean cloth and do not heat it.
  • In the form, write the floor and whether a car can be parked close to the building.

After cleaning: drying and airing

How long furniture takes to dry depends on humidity, airing, room temperature, the fabric and the filling, so we do not promise a number of hours. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) considers a relative humidity of 40–60% comfortable. If the weather allows, open a window and let the air move. Keep leather and latex out of direct heat. More detail: how long a sofa takes to dry after cleaning.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly will you get in touch after a request?

After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes. In that conversation we confirm the furniture and the stain, give the price and agree a time when the specialist can come.

Does the furniture have to go to a workshop?

No. We clean furniture at your place in Riga or the Riga region, so it does not have to be carried down the stairs or driven anywhere. We only need to know the address, the floor and where a car can be parked.

Can you remove any stain?

No, and we do not promise that. A fresh stain often comes out well, but an old one, one that has soaked in or one set by heat may stay. If you send a photo, the specialist will tell you what you can expect.

Can the price change after the photo?

A photo helps us give a more exact price, but it can still be affected by size, fabric, dirt, smell and distance, which are easier to see on the spot. We agree the price before the job, and we start only once you accept it.

Why is there a minimum order?

The specialist has to come to you even if only one chair needs cleaning. That is why the minimum order is €45, and you can book several pieces of furniture in one visit until that sum is reached.

Do you clean leather sofas too?

Yes, leather sofa cleaning is on our price list: two-seater from €40, corner from €50. Water and heat can harm leather, so we look at the type of leather first. More detail: leather sofa cleaning.
