The first question is usually whether you need both sides: often the side you sleep on is enough. Cleaning covers the surface, not the mattress core. Send a photo and give the size, and the specialist will tell you the price.

One side or both sides

Start with whether the mattress is meant to be turned at all. Many mattresses have only one sleeping side and the other is a base, and then there is usually no point in cleaning it unless there is a stain. If the mattress is two-sided and you turn it, or liquid has spilt over the edge, it may be worth cleaning both sides.

Situation What people usually choose
One-sided mattress, stains on the sleeping side one side
Two-sided mattress that gets turned both sides
Stain in one place only, other side clean one side
Stain visible on both sides or on the edge too both sides, but assume liquid has got inside too

If in doubt, send photos of both sides. The specialist will tell you whether the second side is worth it.

What is different about a double mattress

The work area is larger, but the rules are the same: the surface needs as little liquid as possible, and a stain should be treated on its own, not by dampening the whole mattress. Too much water may damage the foam structure or leave moisture in the core, and in a spring mattress it may cause rust.

Then there is the weight. A double mattress is hard for one person to turn, and in some beds it sits between high sides. If you order both sides, give the bed type in the form and whether the mattress can be lifted easily. Before the work, remove the bedding, mattress topper and pillows.

Until the mattress has dried, the bed cannot be used, and drying depends on air humidity, ventilation and the filling. So think in advance about where to sleep in the meantime.

Latex and memory foam mattresses are assessed separately, because they have their own care: see the page on orthopaedic mattress cleaning. If there is a separate mattress topper on top, it is cleaned as a separate job: mattress topper cleaning.

Two mattresses in one bed

A double bed often has two single mattresses side by side. They are not one double mattress: one side of each mattress costs from €30, so together from €60 for one side of both. If only one of them has stains, you can clean just that one, but then the minimum order of €45 applies. Dirt often collects in the gap between the mattresses, so show that in a photo too.

What to expect from the result

Usually comes out

  • dust and dirt on the large surface
  • fresher drink and food stains
  • part of an everyday smell from the fabric

May stay

  • old stains that have soaked in deeply
  • a smell, if liquid has reached the filling
  • dips worn in where people sleep
  • mould inside

A worn-in dip is wear, not dirt, and cleaning does not repair it. An old stain may stay as a pale shadow even after cleaning. More: stains on a mattress.

Frequently asked questions

Can only the side of the bed with the stain be cleaned?

In principle yes, a stain can be treated on its own, but the price is for the mattress surface, not for a part of it. From a photo, the specialist can tell you whether a line will show between the cleaned and uncleaned parts.

How do I give the size if I do not know it exactly?

Width and length in centimetres are usually enough, for example 160 × 200. If the label is no longer legible, measure with a tape measure and enter it in the form.

Does the underside of a mattress need cleaning at all?

If the mattress is one-sided and there are no visible stains or mould underneath, usually not. Dark patches underneath are a different matter: check whether moisture is collecting by the bed base.
