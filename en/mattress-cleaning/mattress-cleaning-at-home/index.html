Why a mattress is cleaned from the surface only

A mattress is thick, and its inside dries very slowly. So manufacturers recommend caring for the surface with as little liquid as possible and treating only the stain, not the whole mattress. How careful you need to be depends on what is inside:

  • foam: it is sensitive to moisture, and too much water may damage the structure or leave moisture in the core;
  • springs: moisture in the spring unit may cause rust;
  • latex: only a slightly damp cloth suits it, strong products may make it brittle, and it must be kept away from direct sun and heat;
  • coconut fibre: it breathes well but is sensitive to moisture and dust.

If a mattress gets thoroughly soaked, it is almost impossible to dry it completely before mould starts to grow. This is the limit, and it is why a mattress is not cleaned like a sofa cover.

Tempur: the manufacturer states that TEMPUR material must not be washed, sponged or dry-cleaned. First check the manufacturer's care instructions. See the page on orthopaedic mattress cleaning. Source: TEMPUR UK, checked 07.11.2026.

How to let a mattress dry

We do not promise a drying time, because it depends on air humidity, ventilation, room temperature and the filling. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) gives 40–60% as comfortable indoor humidity and recommends airing the bedroom every morning for at least 15 minutes. The US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) notes that fans and a dehumidifier speed up drying. Do not put a latex mattress by a heater or in direct sun.

If the mattress lies directly on the floor, lift it so that air reaches the underside too. This is especially important for a coconut fibre mattress, because on the floor moisture cannot escape.

What home cleaning can and cannot do

Usually comes out

  • dust and dirt on the surface
  • fresher stains in the fabric
  • some of the smell, if it is in the fabric

May stay

  • old stains that have soaked in deeply
  • a smell, if liquid has reached the filling
  • mould inside the mattress
  • sunken areas and worn foam

Cleaning does not reach the mattress core, and it is better to say so straight away. If the smell comes from inside, surface cleaning may reduce it, but it may stay or come back. We have written separately about why mattresses smell: why a mattress smells. If there is mould in the mattress, it often has to be replaced and the cause of the damp dealt with. Frequently asked questions

Does the mattress need to go anywhere?

No. The mattress is cleaned at your place, in Riga or the Riga region, and it does not have to be carried out of the flat. All we need is access to the mattress and a bed without bedding.

Does home cleaning help against bedbugs?

No. Bedbugs are a job for pest control, not cleaning. A register of service providers can be found on the Health Inspectorate's website.
