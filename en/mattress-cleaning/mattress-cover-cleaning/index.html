Mattress cover cleaning

Mattress cover cleaning is needed when the cover cannot be removed, the label does not allow washing or a stain has stayed after washing.

If the cover is removable and the label allows machine washing, you usually do not need a specialist. Cleaning a cover on the mattress costs the same as cleaning the mattress surface, from €30. Send a photo of the label, and the specialist will tell you which case is yours.

Is the cover removable, and what does the label say

Situation What to do
Removable, label allows washing Wash according to the label; a specialist is usually not needed.
Removable, label forbids washing Send a photo; the specialist will tell you what is possible.
Not removable, sewn in or without a zip The cover is part of the mattress and is cleaned as the mattress surface.
Stain left after washing Send a photo of the stain and say what it is and how it was washed.

A zip alone does not mean the cover may be washed. On upholstered furniture covers, New Mexico State University warns that washing them separately may cause shrinkage, backing damage or colour change. Even TEMPUR, which allows many removable covers to be machine-washed, states that some cover lines may not be. So the label is what decides.

How to read the care symbols on a cover

  • A crossed-out symbol (St Andrew's cross) means this treatment must not be used.
  • Lines under the symbol mean gentler treatment.
  • Letters in a circle indicate solvents and processes for professional textile care.
  • The letter W in a circle is professional wet cleaning. It is not the W code on upholstery fabric: these are two different systems.

If the label is gone, do not assume the cover will withstand anything. Fabrics react to moisture in different ways: colours may run, the fabric may shrink, and water rings may remain. Take a photo of the cover and, if you know it, enter the mattress type. Before you remove the cover, take a photo of the label so you have it to hand.

What happens to the mattress when the cover is off

Use the chance to look at the mattress surface. If a stain has gone through the cover, a clean cover does not solve the problem: the smell and stain stay underneath. Then you need mattress cleaning at home or, if it is urine, mattress cleaning after urine.

Put the cover back on only when both it and the mattress are completely dry. If you have a protective cover against dust mites, be aware that in studies these covers have not always given clinical improvement either. More: dust mites in a mattress and sofa.

What to expect from cover cleaning

Usually comes out

  • dust and surface dirt
  • fresher stains on the fabric
  • some of the smell from the fabric

May stay

  • colour changes from earlier washing
  • shrunken fabric
  • a stain set by heat
  • everything under the cover

A stain that has been through a hot wash or dryer is usually harder to remove: heat may set it. Describe how you have already tried to clean the cover, because what to expect depends on that.

How much mattress cover cleaning costs

There is no separate price for a cover. If the cover is on the mattress, the price is the mattress surface price: single from €30, double from €45 (one side), second side +50%. The price depends on size, fabric, how dirty it is, smell and distance, and we agree it before the work.

The minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45. Travel is included in Riga and up to about 15 km outside Riga; further out it is from €10. All prices are on the prices page.

Frequently asked questions

Can the cover be cleaned so that the mattress stays dry?

If the cover is on the mattress, some moisture may get underneath, so the mattress still has to be left to dry. That is exactly why the surface needs as little liquid as possible.

The cover shrank after washing. Can it be fixed?

Shrunken fabric does not regain its original size after cleaning. Some fabrics change size with moisture, and this usually stays. Send a photo if you do not know what to do with it.

Can I send the cover somewhere to be cleaned?

We work only at the client's place, in Riga and the Riga region. A removed cover can usually be washed according to the label or taken to a textile care company, if the label calls for professional treatment.

Can the cover be washed at 60 °C to fight dust mites?

Only if the label allows it. The advice to wash at 60 °C or above applies to bedding, not to every mattress cover. Washing in hotter water than allowed may damage the fabric.
