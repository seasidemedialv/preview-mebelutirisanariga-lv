Orthopaedic mattress cleaning

Foam, latex and springs differ in how sensitive they are to moisture, and some manufacturers do not allow wet cleaning at all. One side costs from €30 for a single mattress and from €45 for a double. Send a photo of the label, and the specialist will tell you what is possible.

First read the manufacturer's care instructions

An expensive mattress usually comes with the manufacturer's care instructions: on the label, in the purchase documents or on the website. They matter more than any general advice, including ours. If the text forbids moisture, wet cleaning is not suitable for this mattress, even if the stain looks simple.

Tempur: the manufacturer states that TEMPUR material must not be washed, sponged or dry-cleaned, and the mattress must be kept away from moisture. If something is spilt, soak up as much as possible with towels. Many removable covers can be machine-washed if the label allows it, but not all. So a Tempur mattress owner should first check the manufacturer's text. Source: TEMPUR UK, checked 07.11.2026.

What is inside the mattress and what it means

Filling What to know
Foam, including cold foam Sensitive to moisture. Too much water may damage the structure or leave moisture in the core. Dry vacuuming suits delicate materials such as cold foam.
Memory foam See that manufacturer's own text. Moisture is not allowed on Tempur material.
Latex Dry vacuuming or a slightly damp cloth. Strong products may make it brittle. Keep away from direct sun and heat.
Springs Moisture in the spring unit may cause rust, so surface care only, with as little liquid as possible.
Coconut fibre Sensitive to moisture and dust. Do not put the mattress directly on the floor, where moisture cannot escape.

If you do not know the composition, take a photo of the label or enter the model name in the form.

The year of purchase is also useful in the form, as is whether the mattress has a removable cover. If it does, see the page on mattress cover cleaning: sometimes that is enough.

What to do with a stain until the specialist has seen it

Gently blot the liquid with a clean cloth or towel, without rubbing. Dampen only the stain itself, not the area around it, and never use hot water on blood: it sets the proteins. If the manufacturer forbids moisture, stick to blotting and contact the manufacturer or us. Other stains: stains on a mattress.

Without a stain, dry care is usually enough for such a mattress. IKEA recommends dry vacuuming specifically for delicate materials such as latex and cold foam. Airing the room also helps when the mattress has not been cleaned.

What cleaning does not do for an orthopaedic mattress

Usually comes out

  • dust on the surface
  • some surface stains, if the material allows it
  • some of the smell from the cover fabric

May stay

  • a stain, if the manufacturer does not allow moisture
  • anything that has soaked into the foam layers
  • mould inside the mattress
  • a sunken or worn support layer

Cleaning does not restore a mattress's support. If the foam has sunk or the layers have collapsed, a clean mattress will not change how it feels to sleep on. Mould in the core is not a cleaning matter at all, and such a mattress usually has to be replaced: see the page on mould in a mattress. On the limits of cleaning in general, see the page what comes out and what may stay.

Frequently asked questions

Can you clean a Tempur mattress?

We cannot say without seeing the manufacturer's text for this model. TEMPUR states that the material itself must not be washed, sponged or dry-cleaned, so it is usually a question of dry surface care or the cover according to the label.

Does cleaning affect the manufacturer's obligations?

That depends on the manufacturer's terms. If the mattress is new, read them before making a request. Send the terms or a photo along with the form, and the specialist will tell you whether cleaning is worthwhile.

Can an orthopaedic mattress be turned to clean the other side?

The manufacturer decides that. If only one side is for sleeping on, the other side usually does not need cleaning.
