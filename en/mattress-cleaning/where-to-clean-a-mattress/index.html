One side of a single mattress costs from €30, of a double from €45. But cleaning is not the right answer for every mattress, and below we explain when it is not.

Where to turn in each situation

Situation Solution
Stains or smell on the mattress surface Cleaning at your home
Stain only on a removable cover, label allows washing Washing according to the label, no specialist needed
Cover not removable, or label forbids washing Cleaning at your home, first a photo of the label
Tempur or another mattress whose manufacturer forbids moisture First the manufacturer's care instructions
Mould inside the mattress The mattress usually has to be replaced and the cause of the damp dealt with
Bedbugs Pest control, not cleaning

A small, fresh stain does not always need a specialist: blotting, cool water and airing are often enough. More: stains on a mattress.

Why a mattress usually does not need to be moved

A mattress is heavy and large, and moving it alone takes time and a suitable vehicle. But the main point is something else: cleaning reaches the surface, not the core, and that can be done on the spot. After the work, the mattress has to dry in the same ventilated room, and it does not have to be transported damp. More on how the work goes: mattress cleaning at home.

What to send to get a price

  • a photo of the whole mattress and a close-up of the stain;
  • a photo of the label, if it has survived;
  • the size, and one or both sides;
  • what happened and roughly how long ago;
  • the address, so we can tell you whether travel is included.

The more the specialist sees, the fewer surprises there will be on the spot. They will tell you whether a photo is enough or whether the mattress needs to be seen in person.

What to ask before you order cleaning

  • What is the price for my size, and is it for one side or both?
  • What is the minimum order, and is travel included?
  • Do the mattress material and the manufacturer's care instructions allow cleaning?
  • Which of these stains may stay?

The answer to the last question matters most. If someone promises to clean everything, ask them to explain how they will reach the mattress core.

What to expect from cleaning at your place

Usually comes out

  • dust and dirt on the surface
  • fresher stains
  • some of the smell from the fabric

May stay

  • old, deeply soaked stains
  • a smell from the filling
  • mould inside
  • wear and worn-in dips

From a photo or on the spot, the specialist will tell you which items on this list apply to your mattress. If you still have doubts about the limits of cleaning, read what comes out and what may stay or why a mattress smells and what to do.

Frequently asked questions

Can I bring the mattress to you?

No. We clean only at the client's place, in Riga and the Riga region.

How much does it cost if I live more than 15 km from Riga?

Travel further than about 15 km outside Riga costs from €10. Give the address in the form, and we will tell you the price before the work.

Is it worth cleaning an old mattress, or is it better to buy a new one?

If the mattress still supports the body well and the problem is a surface stain, cleaning makes sense. If it has sunk, smells from the inside or has mould in it, cleaning will not fix it, and the mattress needs to be replaced.

Is the price from a photo final?

From a photo, the specialist gives the price and says what it depends on. If the situation on the spot turns out to be different, we agree the price before the work starts, not after.
