These are starting prices for one piece, so below we explain what changes them, how the minimum order works and what travel costs. Send a photo, and a specialist will tell you the price for your furniture.

What makes up the price

  • Size: two-seater or corner sofa, single or double mattress.
  • Fabric: leather is priced differently from fabric, but velour and faux leather have no separate prices.
  • Dirt: how long ago the furniture was cleaned and how widespread the dirt is.
  • Smell: smell treatment costs extra, from €15.
  • Distance: in Riga and up to about 15 km outside Riga, travel is included.

The published prices say “from” because two sofas of the same size can need different amounts of work. We agree the price before the job, and we start cleaning only once you accept it.

Minimum order €45

The minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45. We state this here so that there are no surprises after the job.

In practice it pays to book everything that needs cleaning in one visit: you can add a chair to a two-seater sofa, the second side to a mattress, a pouffe to chairs. If the total is €45 or more, you pay according to the price list.

Travel

Travel is included in Riga and up to about 15 km outside Riga; further out it is from €10. In Riga this applies to all neighbourhoods. If the address is far away, we tell you the price when we speak, before the job.

Travel is included in places around Riga such as Mārupe, Salaspils, Ķekava and Ādaži. It costs from €10 for places such as Ogre, Jelgava and Sigulda. In Jūrmala travel is included up to about 15 km outside Riga, but in Sloka, Kauguri and Ķemeri it costs from €10. The list of all places: where we work.

Smell treatment from €15

Smell treatment is an extra job, not a promise. A smell may fade, but it may also stay or come back after a while, especially if urine or smoke has reached the filling. Before the job the specialist tells you whether it is worth ordering in your case. More detail: what comes out and what may stay.

Frequently asked questions

Can the price change on the spot?

It can change only before the job. If it turns out on the spot that the furniture is bigger, the fabric is different or the dirt is not what the photo showed, we say so and agree the price before we start.

Does a velour sofa cost more?

No, there is no separate velour price: we use the fabric sofa prices. The same goes for faux leather.

Can you give a price without a photo?

Yes, if you describe the furniture, its size and the fabric in the form. Even so, with a photo we can give a more exact price.

Are the prices the same for everyone?

Yes, the prices are as written here, with no crossed-out figures. We agree the price separately only for larger business volumes.

Why can't you give the final price straight away?

The price is affected by size, fabric, dirt, smell and distance, and these can only be seen on the particular piece of furniture. So from a photo we give the price, or say what it will depend on.
