How to choose a service

Choose by the piece of furniture, not by the problem. A sofa, a chair and a mattress are different jobs, because the fabric, the filling and the drying time differ. If several pieces need cleaning, put them in one request: that makes it easier to give a total price and reach the minimum order.

If the main problem is a stain or a smell, start with our advice pages: stains on a sofa, urine smell in a sofa or why a mattress smells. There you will find what to do straight away and when cleaning may not help. You can also simply describe the situation in the form, for example “the cat urinated on the corner sofa”, and the specialist will advise you where to start.

What all the jobs have in common

  • We clean at your place: at home, in the office or where the car is parked. The furniture does not have to go anywhere.
  • A photo is optional, but it makes the price more exact: send a photo, and a specialist will tell you the price.
  • We agree the price before the job.
  • Before the job we say what may stay. We make no promises about stains and smells.
  • The label decides the cleaning method. If there is none, tell the specialist.

What is not our job

  • Carpets. We do not clean carpets.
  • The whole car interior. We clean only seats, child car seats and prams.
  • Bedbugs and fleas. That is a job for pest control, and ordinary cleaning does not solve it.
  • Mould inside furniture. This may not be a cleaning problem: often the piece has to be replaced and the damp dealt with.
  • Upholstery repair and re-covering.

If you are not sure whether it is our job, send a request, and the specialist will tell you whether we can help.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do if the furniture does not fit any service?

Choose “Other question” in the form and describe the furniture or add a photo. The specialist will tell you whether we can clean it and what it will cost.

Can several jobs be combined in one visit?

Yes. A sofa, chairs and a mattress can be booked in one visit, and we work out the total from the price list. Car seats can be added too, if the car is parked at the house.

Do you also clean pillows and duvets?

We clean removable sofa cushions together with the sofa, from €2 per cushion. There is no separate service for bed pillows and duvets.
