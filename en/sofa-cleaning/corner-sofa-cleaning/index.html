What is different about a corner sofa

On a straight sofa the load is spread across the seats. On a corner sofa a family usually picks one spot: the chaise section where someone lies, or the corner facing the television. There the fabric darkens, while the end module looks almost new for a long time. So the first decision is whether to clean the whole sofa or only the heavily used part.

The second difference is the places you do not see day to day: the seam between modules, the gap under the cushions, the storage box and the edges by the wall. Crumbs and dust collect there, and sometimes that is exactly where a hard-to-find smell comes from.

The care label is often hidden under the cushions or on the underside of the sofa. On a big sofa it takes time to find, but the label is what determines whether water may be used on the fabric. The codes are explained on the page fabric sofa cleaning.

What to prepare at home

Empty the storage box and take off decorative cushions and throws. Clear access to the sofa along the walls, if that is easy for you. If the corner sofa stands tight in a corner and cannot be moved, say so in the form so that we can agree the price and scope of work in advance. After cleaning, ventilate the room until the fabric is completely dry.

What to expect from cleaning

Usually comes out

  • everyday dirt and greyness on seats and armrests;
  • crumbs and dust in the seams between modules;
  • many fresh food and drink stains.

May stay

  • worn fabric: that is damage, not dirt;
  • an old stain that has been rubbed several times;
  • smell, if liquid has reached the foam;
  • the difference between a much-used and a little-used module.

If only one module is cleaned, the line where it meets the uncleaned part may show. So if the difference between the parts is large, a specialist will tell you what that means for your sofa.

Frequently asked questions

Can only the chaise section be cleaned?

Yes, you can order that, but the minimum order stays at €45. The cleaned part may differ from the rest, especially on light fabric. Send a photo and a specialist will tell you whether it will show.

Do the modules need separating before cleaning?

You do not need to separate them if you do not know how they are fixed. If the modules simply stand side by side or are joined with a hook, say so in the form. Do not take apart bolted modules before this has been agreed.

How can I tell whether my sofa is a large corner sofa?

Give the length of both sides in the form and add a photo. We have no set threshold in centimetres, so a specialist tells you the category and price by size.

Is a sofa with a storage box cleaned differently?

The box must be emptied before the visit. Its inside is often a different material from the outside of the sofa, so if you want the box cleaned too, say so in the form.
