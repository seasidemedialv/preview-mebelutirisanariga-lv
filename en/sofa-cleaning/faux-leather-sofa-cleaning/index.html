What faux leather is and why it is not leather

“Eco-leather” is a trade term for faux leather. It usually means fabric with a polymer coating, most often polyurethane or PVC. The sofa description may also say “PU leather” or “artificial leather”. The word “eco” does not tell you what your sofa is actually made of, so check the label or the purchase document.

It should not be confused with real leather. The rules on the page leather sofa cleaning do not automatically apply to faux leather, and the price table is different too: for faux leather we use fabric sofa prices.

Why the coating peels and why that is not dirt

Research on polyurethane shows that polyester polyurethane breaks down with moisture and time, and the material becomes weaker. A study of polyurethane coatings on fabrics left outdoors for two years describes hydrolysis and delamination. This work is not about sofas, so it cannot be applied directly to sofas, but the direction is clear: the coating ages.

With faux leather clothing, the most common failure observed is in the coating film or adhesive: the surface sticks to itself, blisters or wrinkles. Some imitations do not withstand dry cleaning.

We do not claim that cleaning causes or prevents peeling. There is no verified source on PU furniture, and we have none at all on PVC. So we assess a peeling coating from photos and on site, without promises. Checked 07.11.2026.

How to judge whether a faux leather sofa is worth cleaning

Condition What it means
Coating intact, only dirty a normal case for cleaning
Fine cracks in the folds cleaning can remove the dirt, but the cracks stay
Coating lifting or peeling this is material damage, cleaning does not fix it, and a specialist will tell you whether there is any point
Coating gone over large areas cleaning usually makes no sense: the cover or the sofa has to be replaced

Re-covering or new upholstery is not our work. But it is better to learn before paying that dirt is not the sofa's main problem.

What to send before your request

  • photos of the seat and back in daylight;
  • a close-up of the spot where the coating is most damaged;
  • the label or purchase description, if you still have it;
  • what has been used on the sofa before, if you know;
  • whether the sofa stands by a radiator or in the sun.

What to expect after faux leather cleaning

Usually comes out

  • the layer of dirt on seats and armrests;
  • dust in seams and folds;
  • fresh food and drink stains that have stayed on the surface.

May stay

  • peeling, blisters and cracks;
  • dye transfer from clothing that has soaked into the coating;
  • pen marks;
  • smell, if liquid has reached the upholstery through cracks.

Frequently asked questions

Can cleaning stop faux leather from peeling?

No. Peeling is the material ageing, and cleaning neither stops nor repairs it. Nor do we claim that cleaning speeds it up or prevents it, because there is no source for such a claim.

Can it be cleaned if the sofa stands in the sun and the coating has gone sticky?

Stickiness may mean the coating is already breaking down. Cleaning will not undo that. Send a close-up photo and a specialist will tell you whether there is any point.

Can faux leather be cleaned the same way as leather?

No, they are different materials. Faux leather has no leather structure; it has a coating. If the label gives instructions, follow them. We have no verified rule on PVC coatings.

How can I tell faux leather from real leather?

The purchase document or label is the best guide. If you have neither, send a close-up photo and one of the cut edge or underside, if it can be seen. Sometimes we can only tell on site.
