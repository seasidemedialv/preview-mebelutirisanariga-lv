What happens under the fabric

Send a photo, and a specialist will tell you the price and what you can expect from that fabric and stain. Everyday dirt and fresh stains usually come out, but an old stain or a urine smell that has soaked in may stay. We say so before the job, not after it.

What you can expect from sofa cleaning

A sofa does not get dirty evenly. Seats, armrests and the place where the head rests suffer most. Cleaning usually freshens these areas well. It is harder with anything that has soaked in deeper than the fabric or has already changed its colour.

Usually comes out

  • Dark patches on seats, armrests and the backrest
  • Fresh coffee, wine and food stains
  • Dust and hair on the surface of the fabric
  • A household smell that is in the fabric, not in the filling

May stay

  • An old stain that has been rubbed many times or treated at home
  • Urine smell if urine has reached the foam
  • Colour changes from sunlight or bleach
  • Wear, pressure marks in velour and shrunken fabric

Pressure marks and shade changes in velour and chenille are a property of these fabrics, not a stain, so cleaning does not remove them. Cleaning does not restore worn fabric either: a clean sofa will be clean, but not new. More on the limits: what comes out and what may stay.

The care label decides how a sofa may be cleaned

The label is usually under the seat cushions or on the underside of the sofa. On many fabrics it carries one of four codes:

Code What it means
W Water-based foam, used sparingly, without overwetting the fabric. Dry-cleaning solvents are not suitable.
S Only a mild solvent without water, in a well-ventilated room.
WS Both methods work: a mild solvent or gentle water-based foam.
X No liquids at all, only vacuuming and light brushing; any other cleaning is assessed separately.

Manufacturers give these codes voluntarily, so some sofas have none. In that case, before cleaning, you first have to find out how the fabric reacts, and it is worth writing in the form that there is no label. Do not confuse these letters with the care symbols on clothes: there, a circle with a W means professional wet cleaning, and that is a different system.

The meaning of the codes follows a Virginia Cooperative Extension bulletin and textile manufacturers' care instructions. Sources differ on code X, so we give the most cautious meaning. Checked 07.11.2026.

Sofa dry cleaning and cleaning at home

“Sofa dry cleaning” is an everyday term. It usually means cleaning the sofa on the spot, at your home, not a trip to a dry cleaner's. Whether a fabric needs a water or a waterless method depends on the code on the label, not on the name of the service.

A sofa should not be soaked. Too much water can leave a ring, shrink the fabric or make the colour run, and moisture can reach the filling. That is also why we do not recommend washing removable covers separately: New Mexico State University warns that the fabric can shrink, its backing can suffer or the colour can change.

Stains, urine and smells

First aid for a fresh stain is to blot it with a clean cloth, not to rub it and not to use hot water. Then send a photo: stains on a sofa, wine and coffee stains on a sofa, grease stain on a sofa.

Urine is a separate job, because the liquid often goes through the fabric to the foam, and the smell may stay or come back. See cleaning urine from a sofa and sofa cleaning after a cat or dog. If a cat keeps returning to the same spot, it is advisable to consult a vet first, as cleaning does not deal with the cause.

For a sofa with an unknown past there is used sofa cleaning, and before a sale sofa cleaning before selling is useful. Bedbugs and fleas are not a cleaning job but a pest control job.

Frequently asked questions

Does the sofa have to be taken anywhere?

No. We clean the sofa at your place in Riga or the Riga region, and it does not need to be moved anywhere else.

Will the sofa be like new after cleaning?

No, we do not promise that. Cleaning removes dirt but does not restore worn fabric or colour, and some stains may stay. The specialist tells you from a photo what to expect from your sofa.

Can I order cleaning for just one cushion or one stain?

Yes, but then the €45 minimum order applies. If only one spot is cleaned, a ring may be left on the fabric, so the specialist will tell you whether that is a good idea.

Can I wash removable covers in a washing machine?

We usually do not recommend it unless the label clearly allows it. A cover washed separately can shrink or change colour, and then it no longer matches the rest of the sofa.

Can you clean a sofa that has mould in it?

A stain on the surface can be assessed, but mould in the filling often means the sofa has to be replaced and the cause of the damp dealt with. More detail: mould in a sofa.
