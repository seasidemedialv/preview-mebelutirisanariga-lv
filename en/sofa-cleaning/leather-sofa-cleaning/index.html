Why fabric rules do not apply to leather

Leather is highly absorbent. A stain does not stay on the surface but soaks into the leather itself, and methods that suit fabric can damage its structure. Direct contact with water also damages most leathers.

The second risk is heat: it dries leather out, makes it brittle and distorts it. So do not dry a wet leather sofa with a hairdryer or keep it by a radiator. If a stain is fresh, blot it with a clean cloth and do not rub.

The facts about leather come from museum and conservation sources (the Canadian Conservation Institute, the US National Park Service) and Iowa State University extension advice on leather clothing. They must be applied to furniture leather with caution. Checked 07.11.2026.

Saddle soap does not suit leather because it is alkaline. So if the sofa has already been cared for, say in the form what has been used on it before.

Owners do not always know what kind of leather their sofa has. The purchase document or a label helps, but if you have neither, send a close-up photo in good daylight. A specialist will tell you what can be assessed from a photo and what can only be seen on site.

What a leather sofa usually needs

The most common problem is not a single stain but wear where the leather touches the body: the front edge of the seats, the armrests, where the head rests. There the leather turns darker or shinier, while dust collects in the seams and folds.

Before you send a request, look at these places up close and photograph them. If the surface is peeling, it may not be real leather: see faux leather sofa cleaning. A fuller explanation of whether leather can be cleaned at all: can a leather sofa be cleaned.

What usually comes out and what may stay on leather

Usually comes out

  • the layer of everyday dirt on seats and armrests;
  • dust in seams and folds;
  • fresh food and drink stains that have not soaked in.

May stay

  • pen ink, which often cannot be removed from leather;
  • a grease stain that has already soaked into the leather;
  • cracks, scuffs, faded colour;
  • scratches from dog or cat claws.

Cleaning is not restoration. If the sofa needs its colour restored or cracks repaired, that is a different job.

What not to do with a leather sofa before you send a request

  • do not wet the leather or rub the stain;
  • do not use saddle soap, oil or cream to “restore” it;
  • do not dry it with a hairdryer or put it by a radiator;
  • do not use chlorine bleach or strong cleaning solutions, as they change the colour.

Frequently asked questions

Does a leather sofa need cream after cleaning?

The source on finished furniture leather advises against creams, saddle soap and oils. If the manufacturer recommends its own care product, follow the manufacturer's instructions. Without that, do not apply anything just because the leather looks dry.

Can a pen mark be removed from a leather sofa?

That cannot be promised. Ink soaks into leather and often stays, so on no account rub it with alcohol or any other unknown liquid. Send a photo and a specialist will tell you honestly what the chances are.

Will a leather sofa get damp?

Leather does not need much liquid. If the surface is damp after the work, keep heat away from it. We do not promise a drying time: it depends on the leather and the room.
