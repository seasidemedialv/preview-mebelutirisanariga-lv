Why mistakes cost more on light fabric

A dark sofa is forgiving. Light fabric shows what you would not notice elsewhere: a ring around a stain, the edge of a seat, a shadow where the fabric has been rubbed. Where it gets too wet, the fabric's finish can migrate and leave a ring, and on white the first guest will see it.

So even a small stain on a light sofa often makes you look at the whole seat. If only one spot is cleaned, the clean spot may show against the rest of the fabric. Before the work a specialist will tell you what is more sensible: cleaning the stain, the seat or the whole sofa.

White fabric is not bleached

The first thing that comes to mind for a white sofa is bleach. Chlorine bleach and strong cleaning solutions can change the colour, and on upholstery fabric that can mean a blotch rather than cleaner fabric. So on a white sofa too, the deciding factor remains the label code, not the colour.

A fresh stain is the easiest case, and the most you can do is blot it, not rub it and avoid hot water. More on wine and coffee: wine and coffee stains on a sofa. On grease: grease stain on a sofa.

What usually comes out and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • everyday greyness on seats and armrests;
  • fresh food and drink stains;
  • dust in seams and under cushions.

May stay

  • old yellowing with no clear cause;
  • a bluish band from jeans or other coloured clothing;
  • a ring from earlier cleaning;
  • sun fading and wear.

White fabric that has been used for years does not usually return to its original shade. That is not a fault of the work but the age of the fabric.

How to photograph a light sofa

Take photos in daylight, without flash and without a yellow lamp: a phone's colour correction can hide exactly what needs to be seen. Fit both the stain and an undamaged part of the fabric into one shot so that a specialist can compare them. Add the label. More about the shots: how to photograph a sofa for a price.

Frequently asked questions

Will a white sofa be white again?

It usually gets lighter, but we cannot promise the shade it had in the shop. Wear, fading and old yellowing may stay. A photo in daylight lets you judge this before ordering.

Can a visible edge stay after a stain is cleaned?

It can, especially on white fabric, if the spot got too wet or the fabric has a finish. That is why it matters not to wet it too much, and sometimes to clean a larger area.

Does a light-coloured sofa cost more?

No, there is no separate price for colour alone. Size, how dirty it is or smell can raise the price, and we agree it before the work.

Can I try bleach before the visit?

No. Chlorine bleach can change the colour for good, and cleaning will not put that right. Blot a fresh stain and leave the rest until the photo assessment.
