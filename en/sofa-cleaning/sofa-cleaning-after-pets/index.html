Furniture cleaning after pets: the cause comes first

About one cat in ten at some point relieves itself outside the litter tray, and vets advise ruling out health causes first. If a cat does it repeatedly, consult a vet before cleaning. A cat's marks are usually on vertical surfaces, in small amounts, with a sharp smell; that means the back and sides of a sofa, not the seat.

We have no verified source on dog behaviour, so we give no rules. If a dog repeatedly urinates indoors, that is a question for a vet. Deal with the cause first and only then order cleaning: that is cheaper than cleaning twice.

Sources: ASPCA, MSD Veterinary Manual, Feline VMA, International Cat Care. Checked 07.11.2026. They apply to cats; there are no verified sources on dogs.

Cat urine: why it is a separate job

A cat's mark contains extra chemical signals that smell sharp to people. Veterinary sources recommend using enzyme products intended for animal smells in such places, and testing in a hidden spot first. Ammonia is not used, because to a cat it smells of urine. The rest is on the page cleaning urine from a sofa.

International Cat Care advises that heavily soaked fabric should be thrown away if possible, because it stays attractive even after cleaning. So repeated marks in one place may mean the cat will return to the sofa. More: cat urine smell in furniture and dog urine smell in furniture.

Fleas: this is not a cleaning job

If an animal has fleas, that is a pest control job, not furniture cleaning. The animal is treated first, and the places where it spends time may need treatment too. Fleas can keep hatching for up to two weeks. Order cleaning after that.

What usually comes out and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • paw prints and mud;
  • a lot of hair from the surface and gaps;
  • sleeping-place smell from the top layer.

May stay

  • the smell of a mark, if it is in the upholstery;
  • hair stuck in the weave of the fabric;
  • scratches and tears;
  • the animal's interest in the same spot.

Frequently asked questions

Will the cat come back to the same spot after cleaning?

It may. Heavily soaked fabric can stay attractive even after cleaning. So the cause has to be dealt with together with a vet, and sometimes it is worth considering a different piece of furniture or a protective cover.

Can an animal get on the sofa straight away?

First let the sofa dry completely and air the room. We do not say that cleaning is suitable for animals: if yours has sensitive skin or licks the sofa, write that in the form.

Can dog smell be cleaned out of a sofa?

Smell can usually be reduced in fabric, but we do not promise it will go. If the dog keeps sleeping in the same spot, it will come back. A removable cover that can be washed according to its label is a simpler solution.

Do you also clean pet beds?

We have no published price for pet beds. Write about it in the form and a specialist will tell you whether it can be added to the order.
