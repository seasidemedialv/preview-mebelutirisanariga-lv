Why covers should not be washed separately

A zip on a cushion does not mean the cover may be washed. New Mexico State University materials warn that washing it separately can cause shrinkage, damage the fabric backing or change the colour.

A cover that has shrunk by a few centimetres no longer closes over the foam, and a washed cushion stands out from the others. Cleaning can no longer put such a cover right. If this has already happened, send a photo and a specialist will tell you what can still be done.

New Mexico State University, G-402: do not remove zipped covers for separate washing, as shrinkage, backing damage and colour change are possible. If the label allows something else, follow it. Checked 07.11.2026.

Seat, back and decorative cushions

Cushion What it is usually like What to check
Seat cushion the heaviest load, more often foam-filled moisture must not reach the foam
Back cushion dirt where the head and shoulders rest filling and seams
Decorative cushion smaller, often a different fabric or decoration a different label, sometimes no code

Foam is sensitive to moisture: too much water can damage its structure or leave moisture inside. This fact comes from a mattress manufacturer, but many seat cushions contain foam too. We have no verified rule on feather or fibre filling, so check the label and send a photo.

Cushions and sofa: why they are usually cleaned together

A cushion that has been used more often or left in the sun may differ from the sofa. If only one cushion is cleaned, it may stand out. So it helps to look at the whole set: which cushions came as a set with the sofa, which can be turned over and which are different.

If the sofa cushions can be turned over, look at both sides before cleaning. Often only one side needs cleaning and the other looks fine. If the smell comes from a cushion, check it separately from the sofa: why a sofa smells and what to do.

What usually comes out and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • everyday dirt and greyness on the cover;
  • dust and crumbs in the seams;
  • fresh stains that have not soaked through.

May stay

  • a shrunken cover: cleaning does not fix it;
  • fading that differs from the rest of the sofa;
  • a smell from inside, if liquid has reached the foam;
  • dents in the foam: that is wear.

A fresh stain on a cushion: what to do

Do not take off the cover or hold it under the tap. Blot the liquid with a clean cloth, do not rub, and if needed lightly dampen the cloth in cool water. Hot water can set the stain. If the cushion is soaked, place it so that air can reach it from all sides, and get in touch with us before doing anything further.

How to prepare

  • count the cushions and photograph them together with the sofa;
  • photograph the worst one separately;
  • find the label: on the cushion and in the sofa beneath it;
  • do not take the covers off before the visit;
  • write down the size if a cushion is large.

Frequently asked questions

Should a cushion be dried on a radiator after cleaning?

No, do not put it near heat. Let it dry in a ventilated room so that air reaches it from all sides.

Can I order only the cushions, without the sofa?

You can, but the minimum order of €45 applies. So if there are only a few cushions, it is usually better value to add the sofa or chairs.

What if the cover has already been washed and no longer closes?

Cleaning does not undo shrinkage. Do not force the zip. Send a photo and a specialist will tell you what can be done with the other cushions so that the difference does not grow.

Do you clean decorative cushions with beads or embroidery?

There is no separate rule. Check the label and send a photo: decorations and embroidery can limit what may be done, and a specialist will tell you whether it is sensible.
