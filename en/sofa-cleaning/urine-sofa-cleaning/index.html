What happens under the fabric

The fabric is the easy part; if the liquid has reached the upholstery foam, the smell may stay or come back even after cleaning. We clean at your home in Riga and the Riga region, a sofa from €35, smell treatment from €15 extra. Send a photo, write how long ago it happened, and a specialist will tell you what to expect.

What to do in the first hour

  1. Blot Gently dab with a clean, absorbent cloth or paper, without rubbing. Change the cloth until it no longer gets wet.
  2. Cool water, not hot If needed, lightly dampen the cloth in cool or lukewarm water and keep blotting. Hot water can set the stain.
  3. Do not soak A large amount of water carries the liquid deeper into the upholstery.
  4. Avoid ammonia To a cat it smells of urine.
  5. Do not heat Do not dry with a hairdryer or put it on a radiator.

Fresh, old or repeated: the difference in the result

Situation What you can usually expect
Fresh, small, blotted at once the stain usually comes out, the smell often lessens
Dried, but a single incident what you can see usually lessens, the smell depends on the depth
A large amount, gone through the fabric the smell may stay, because it comes from the foam
Repeated, old, in one place cleaning has a limit: the upholstery or the sofa may have to be replaced

This is not a promise but a guide. International Cat Care writes that heavily soaked fabric should be thrown away if possible, because it stays attractive to the cat even after cleaning. No source gives a numerical limit for foam, so we do not name one either.

Material from a feline veterinary association says the smell area can be up to three times larger than the visible stain. That applies to cat urine, but it explains well why cleaning only the visible spot may not be enough. Checked 07.11.2026.

Why the smell may stay

Foam holds liquid and is hard to dry. Mattress manufacturers warn that moisture can stay in the core, and sofa upholstery often contains similar foam. So there may be a smell under the fabric even when the surface looks clean. Check by bending down to smell the spot once it has dried.

The smell may also come back, so we say that before the work. More about the smell: urine smell in a sofa.

What usually comes out and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • the visible stain on the fabric, especially a fresh one;
  • smell from the top layer;
  • a yellowish edge, if it is not old.

May stay

  • smell from the foam, if the urine has gone through;
  • an old yellow stain on light fabric;
  • a smell that returns after a while;
  • a ring around the spot, if it was soaked earlier.

Frequently asked questions

Will the smell go completely?

We do not promise that. If the urine is on the surface, the smell usually lessens. If it has reached the foam, the smell may stay or come back. A specialist will tell you from the photo and description what it is reasonable to expect for your particular sofa.

Is there a difference between human and animal urine?

Cat urine has its own smell: marking urine contains extra substances that smell sharp to people. With a child's or an adult's urine the limit of cleaning is the same: how deep it has soaked in. For animals, see also sofa cleaning after a cat or dog.

Is an old urine stain worth cleaning at all?

Often yes, if the sofa is otherwise in good condition, but expectations must be realistic. Send a photo and write how many times it has happened. If there are many stains and the smell is strong, a specialist may advise against cleaning it.

Are mattresses cleaned the same way?

No, moisture is an even bigger problem in a mattress, so that is a separate job: mattress cleaning after urine.
