What to ask before you choose who to trust with your sofa

  • What is the minimum order?
  • Is the “from” price my price or only the starting point?
  • What size does it apply to, and is travel included?
  • What may stay, and will I be told before the work?
  • Will you ask about the care label?
  • What happens if a stain does not come out?

If someone promises to clean everything, ask how they know without seeing the fabric. Stains, smells and fabrics vary, and an honest answer depends on the photo. Our answer: what comes out and what may stay.

When it is not worth looking for somewhere to clean a sofa

Sometimes the right answer is not to clean. Cleaning will not help if there is mould in the upholstery: porous materials that have gone mouldy may have to be thrown away, and the cause of the damp has to be fixed. Nor will it help against bedbugs or fleas, because that is a pest control job. If the fabric is worn down to the backing or the foam has sagged, dirt is not the main problem.

If the sofa's value is close to the minimum order, compare cleaning with new furniture. Send a photo even if you are not sure: a specialist will tell you what to expect, and you decide whether to order.

What usually comes out and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • everyday dirt and greyness;
  • many fresh stains;
  • dust and crumbs in the seams.

May stay

  • an old stain that has been rubbed or heated;
  • smell from the upholstery;
  • wear and faded colour.
