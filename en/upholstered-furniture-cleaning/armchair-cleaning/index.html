How an armchair differs from a sofa

On a sofa, different people sit in different places. An armchair is usually used by one person, in one position, for a long time. So dirt builds up in narrow zones: on the headrest, the ends of the armrests and the front edge of the seat. The chair as a whole can look fine while these spots are visibly darker.

An armchair also often has a mechanism: a reclining back, a pull-out footrest or electric adjustment. Say in the form whether yours has one, and add a photo from the side. Unplug an electric armchair before work starts.

The care label decides what can be done to the chair

The upholstery code is usually on a label under the seat cushion or on the underside of the chair. Photograph it and add it to your request, because the code decides whether the fabric can take water at all.

  • W: water-based foam, sparingly, without overwetting the fabric.
  • S: only a water-free solvent or dry-cleaning product.
  • WS: either one is allowed, testing first in both cases.
  • X: no liquids, only vacuuming and light brushing; any other cleaning has to be assessed separately.

The codes are voluntary, so many chairs do not have them, especially older ones. If there is no code, the general advice is to test on a hidden spot first or to ask a specialist. Too much water can leave a ring on the fabric, cause shrinkage, change the colour and wet the filling.

What comes out of an armchair and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • dust and household dirt on the fabric
  • the darkest patches on armrests and the headrest, if the dirt is on the surface
  • fresher drink and food stains

May stay

  • pressure marks in velour and chenille
  • worn fabric or a rubbed leather surface
  • a stain set by heat or rubbing
  • a smell, if liquid has reached the foam

Velour and chenille armchairs usually show pressure marks and differences in shade on the seat. Manufacturers call this a property of the fabric, not a defect, and it is not dirt. So a chair can look clean after cleaning, while the place where you sit will still look different.

If there is a fresh stain on the chair

Gently blot the liquid with a clean, absorbent cloth, without rubbing. You can lightly rinse the stain with cool water, but do not soak the fabric, so that liquid does not reach the filling. Do not use hot water, a hairdryer or chlorine bleach. Do not try to remove a greasy stain on the headrest with solvent: solvents can damage upholstery foam. Leather, velour and faux leather armchairs

A leather armchair needs a different approach from fabric, because direct contact with water damages most types of leather. More on our leather furniture cleaning page. Velour and faux leather armchairs have no separate price: the fabric armchair price applies. A faux leather coating can peel over time, and cleaning cannot repair that.

Frequently asked questions

Can a removable cover go in the washing machine?

Even if a cushion has a zip, the cover is not always meant to be washed separately. It can shrink, its backing can be damaged or its colour can change, and then it no longer matches the rest of the chair. Check the care label first.

Can an electric armchair be cleaned?

The specialist will tell you from a photo, as it depends on the fabric and on where the mechanism is. Say in the form that the chair is electric, and add a photo from the side and from below, if you can take one.

How long will the armchair stay damp?

We do not promise an exact time, as it depends on the fabric, filling, humidity and ventilation. In damp air everything dries more slowly. If the weather allows, open a window and keep the air in the room moving.

Can a smell be removed from an armchair?

A smell can often be reduced, but we cannot promise that it will go. If liquid has reached the foam, the smell may stay or come back. Describe in the form what caused it, and the specialist will tell you whether treatment makes sense.
