How a camper differs from a home

In a camper, the bed, seating and kitchen are in one small space that is often closed up. So after a trip the fabric holds food smells, sand and moisture from wet clothes. In winter a camper usually stands unheated, and the autumn damp stays inside.

Removable cushions and mattresses are usually made of foam. Foam can hold moisture in its core, so it matters that it dries after cleaning. In a small, closed space the air hardly moves, and in damp air everything dries more slowly, so after cleaning leave the windows or door open if the weather allows.

What to say in the form

  • Where the camper is parked: at your house, in a car park or elsewhere in Riga or the Riga region.
  • Whether it is a camper or a caravan, and whether it can be reached by car.
  • How many seat cushions, what size of mattress and how many cab seats need cleaning.
  • Whether the cushions can be taken out and put outside or indoors.
  • The material: fabric, faux leather or something else.
  • Mould, urine or another particular problem, with a photo.

If the seats are faux leather, bear in mind that the coating can peel over time, and cleaning cannot repair that. Faux leather has no separate price: the fabric price applies.

Mould and a damp smell after winter

If a camper smells of damp in spring, first check where it is coming from: the window seals, the roof vents or condensation on the walls. Cleaning does not remove the cause, and the damp can come back. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) says that relative humidity above 80% means a high risk of mould.

Mould in a mattress or foam is usually a matter for replacement, not cleaning: cleaning does not kill mould in foam. Spots on fabric may stay even if the surface gets cleaner. More detail: mould in a mattress. After a trip, air the camper and leave the mattresses and cushions so that air can get underneath them.

What comes out of a camper's soft parts and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • sand, dust and seasonal dirt on cushions
  • food and drink stains on fabric
  • ordinary dirt on cab seats

May stay

  • mould spots and a smell in the foam
  • peeling faux leather
  • fabric faded by the sun near windows
  • an old kitchen and smoke smell coming from the filling

We clean the soft parts, not the whole camper. Walls, plastics, the kitchen unit and the toilet are not our job. For the cab seats: car seat cleaning.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to bring the camper to you?

No. We clean at your location. Say in the form where the camper is parked, and the specialist will tell you whether work is possible there.

Can you clean a rental camper between customers?

Yes. Say in the form when the camper is free. If you let it out and need cleaning regularly, tell us: for larger volumes the price is by agreement.

When is it better to clean: spring or autumn?

Both make sense, but in autumn it is important that everything dries completely after cleaning, before you put the camper away for winter. Damp cushions in a closed camper are exactly what causes mould.

Can the cab seats be cleaned as well?

Yes. They count as car seats, and one seat costs from €12. We do not do full car interior cleaning, so the floor, ceiling and panels are not our job.
