Clean or replace: it depends on the situation

What happened What it usually means
A little clean water spilt, the fabric got damp, the foam is dry cleaning usually makes sense; a water ring may stay on the fabric
Clean water from a pipe, the seat is soaked, the foam is wet the furniture must dry first; it is often a matter of replacement
Dirty water: from a basement, river or sewer often has to be replaced; surface cleaning does not clean the filling
The mattress is soaked through usually has to be replaced, as it is almost impossible to dry before mould starts to grow
The furniture has stood wet for several days, there is a mouldy smell or spots usually has to be replaced; cleaning does not kill mould in foam
Leather furniture has got soaked dry in the air, without heat; the specialist assesses from a photo

The table is a general assessment, not a decision about your furniture. An expensive sofa with removable filling and a cheap sofa with a single block of foam are different cases. So send photos of the seat and underside, and of the highest point the water reached.

Why soaked foam is a separate matter

Foam absorbs water and holds it in its core, so the surface can be dry while the inside is damp. The US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) says that wet materials must be dried within 24–48 hours so that mould usually does not form on them, and that this can be hard to achieve with upholstered furniture. Porous material with mould may have to be replaced, because mould fills the empty spaces and may be impossible to remove completely.

This also applies to mattresses. North Carolina State University writes that after soaking it is almost impossible to dry a mattress completely before mould starts to grow. In spring mattresses, moisture can also cause rust in the spring core.

In the EPA guidelines, 24–48 hours is the limit for preventing mould, not our cleaning or drying time. Checked 07.11.2026

What to do during the first day

  1. Take photos Before moving the furniture, photograph it and the water level. This will be useful if you report the damage to a neighbour or the property manager.
  2. Lift it off the wet floor Move the furniture to a dry place or onto pallets, and take out removable cushions so that they dry separately.
  3. Soak up the water Gently blot the fabric and mattress with dry towels, without rubbing, and soak up as much as you can.
  4. Air the room Open the windows if the weather allows. The EPA says that fans and a dehumidifier speed up drying.
  5. No heat Do not keep leather or latex directly by a heat source. Do not use chlorine bleach or put light fabric next to dark, as the colour can run.

What comes out after a flood and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • fresh dirt on the surface of fabric after a clean-water leak
  • mud marks on hard-wearing fabric, if the foam is dry
  • sofa cushions, if they were dried quickly

May stay

  • water rings and colour runs on fabric
  • shrunken fabric
  • a mouldy smell from the foam
  • anything that has reached the filling from dirty water

Cleaning is not disinfection, and we do not promise that furniture will be hygienically clean after a flood. Drying the room and removing the cause of the damp are not our job. If the walls are wet, the furniture will not dry in that room, so the room must be sorted out first. More on mould: mould in a sofa and mould in a mattress.

Frequently asked questions

Can furniture flooded by sewage be cleaned?

Honestly: often not. Surface cleaning does not clean the filling. In these cases it is usually advisable to replace the furniture or mattress. Send a photo if the furniture is valuable or if the water reached it only from below.

The sofa looks dry. Does that mean everything is fine?

Not always. Foam can hold moisture in its core even when the fabric is already dry. If a mouldy smell appears after a few days, that means it is damp inside, and then cleaning does not solve the problem.

Can a water ring on fabric be removed?

Sometimes it can be reduced, but we cannot promise that. A ring forms when water shifts the finish of the fabric, and on some fabrics it stays. It also depends on the care label.

Who pays if my neighbour flooded my flat?

That is a matter between you, your neighbour and the property manager, and it is not for us to decide. Photos taken before the furniture was moved will be useful to you.
