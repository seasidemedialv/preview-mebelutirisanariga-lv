Furniture cleaning after renovation

Furniture cleaning after renovation means two different jobs: dust that has settled into the fabric, and individual stains from paint, filler or glue.

When to order: only after the last dusty jobs

Order furniture cleaning at the very end, when the floor has been sanded, the walls painted and no more dust is being created. If a sofa is cleaned and the next day someone cuts plasterboard next to it, the job has to be done again. Before making a request, vacuum the thickest layer of dust off the furniture and air the room, so that new dust does not settle on it.

Before the specialist arrives, make sure there is room to move around and that the furniture is not piled with tools or materials. If plastic sheeting is still on the sofa, take it off carefully and carry it outside, so that the dust does not fall back onto the fabric.

What you see What to expect

Furniture with code X on the label may only be cleaned with a vacuum cleaner and lightly brushed. If that is your sofa, the options for removing dirt are limited. That is exactly why you should photograph the label first: it is usually under the cushions or on the underside of the furniture.

What not to do with fresh paint and glue on fabric

  • Do not use solvent from the renovation materials: solvents can damage upholstery foam.
  • Do not rub: that works the stain into the fabric and spreads it.
  • Do not heat with a hairdryer or use hot water: heat can set the stain.
  • Do not use chlorine bleach: it changes the colour.
  • Do not wet the fabric more than necessary: liquid can reach the filling.

If a stain is fresh and small, it is enough to blot it carefully with a clean cloth, without rubbing, and then photograph it. Leave larger stains alone. More on how fabric reacts to different types of cleaning: dry or wet furniture cleaning.

After renovation the air is often more humid

If plaster, levelling compound or paint is still drying in the room, the air there may be more humid than usual, and furniture then dries more slowly after cleaning. Airing and moving air help. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) calls 40–60% relative humidity comfortable. If you have a hygrometer, check the humidity before ordering; if the room is still very damp, it is better to wait before cleaning.

What comes out after renovation and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • light building dust on the surface of fabric
  • a grey tone on light fabric, if it is not from hardened stains
  • some of the dust in cushions and seams

May stay

  • hardened paint, glue, filler, expanding foam
  • dust deep in the filling
  • stains someone has already tried to clean with solvent
  • fabric damaged by work with tools

If the renovation is still going on, cover the furniture and, if possible, carry it out of the room where cutting or sanding is going on.

Frequently asked questions

Do you also clean floors and windows after renovation?

No. We clean only furniture and mattresses. After renovation, general cleaning companies put the rooms in order, and furniture cleaning is best ordered after that, so that dust does not settle on clean fabric.

The sofa stood under plastic during the renovation. Is that enough?

It often protects against most of the dust, but fine dust can get under the plastic too. If the fabric looks clean once the plastic is off, vacuum it first and only then decide.

Does the smell of renovation soak into a sofa?

It can linger in the fabric, so air the room first. If the fabric still smells after that, describe it in the form, and the specialist will tell you whether smell treatment makes sense.
