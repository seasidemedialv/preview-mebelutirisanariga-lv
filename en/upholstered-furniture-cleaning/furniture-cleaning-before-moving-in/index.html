How to check someone else's sofa and mattress

The biggest difference from your own furniture is that you do not know its history. So before ordering, spend a few minutes on it and look it over in an empty, bright room.

  • Lift the sofa cushions and look under and between them.
  • Take off the sheet and look at the mattress from both sides, not just from the top.
  • Sniff the fabric up close, especially if the room has been shut for a long time.
  • Look at the back and underside of the sofa, if it stands against an outside wall.
  • Look at the seams and edges for tiny dark dots.

If the smell is sharp, if stains on the mattress have spread in large rings or if you see signs of mould, photograph it and tell the specialist. More detail: why a mattress smells and why a sofa smells.

When to clean: before your belongings arrive, not after

The best moment is while the flat is still empty. The furniture can be reached from all sides, and no boxes need moving. After cleaning it has to be left to dry, so allow time before bringing in your belongings, and do not put blankets and boxes on a damp sofa.

If the flat is unheated in winter, that slows drying, so turn the heating on before the cleaning. If renovation is also under way, clean the furniture after it: furniture cleaning after renovation. If you are a tenant, agree with the landlord in advance who pays for the cleaning: that is a matter for the tenancy agreement.

What comes out of someone else's furniture and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • dust and ordinary household dirt
  • everyday stains on the sofa and chairs
  • a greyish look on the fabric, if it is on the surface

May stay

  • someone else's urine smell in the filling
  • a cigarette smell that may come back
  • mould in a mattress and sofa foam
  • old stains of unknown origin

Cleaning does not disinfect furniture, it is not a treatment for allergies, and it does not remove dust mites from a mattress. If that matters to you, consider a new mattress.

Frequently asked questions

Can I order cleaning before I have moved in?

Yes. Just say in the form that the flat is empty, and who will open the door. If the furniture has to be moved or assembled before cleaning, do that beforehand.

The mattress is almost new, but I do not want to sleep on someone else's. Is cleaning worth it?

Often it is, if there are no stains or smell. Cleaning makes the surface cleaner, but it will not reveal what has happened inside the mattress. If that does not put your mind at rest, it is better to consider a new mattress.

Is the smell in the flat coming from the furniture?

Not always. It may come from the walls, floor or ventilation. We clean only textiles. If the smell stays throughout the room even without the furniture, the cause is elsewhere.
