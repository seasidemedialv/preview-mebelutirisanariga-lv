What to know about working in a flat

In a flat the work happens in the room itself, so how the furniture can be reached and how it will dry afterwards often matter more than its size. Describe these things in a few words in the form, and the specialist will not have to ask twice.

  • Neighbourhood and floor, and whether there is a lift, a door code or an intercom.
  • Whether a car can get into the yard and where you can usually park.
  • Whether the sofa can be reached from all sides, or the corner sofa stands tight against the wall.
  • Which furniture needs cleaning and which piece is the main problem.
  • Where the furniture stands: in a room, the kitchen or a room with no window.

Before the work, take covers, belongings and decorative cushions off the furniture and clear access to it. If there is a pet in the flat, say so in the form.

Airing a flat after cleaning

In a flat, drying is largely decided by the air. It depends on humidity, ventilation, room temperature, the fabric and the filling. In damp air everything dries more slowly, so in autumn and winter, when laundry is dried in the flat, drying can take longer than on a dry day. We do not promise an exact number of hours.

The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) says that comfortable relative humidity indoors is 40–60%. If the weather allows, open a window, let the air move and do not put blankets on a damp sofa. More detail: how long a sofa takes to dry after cleaning.

The Health Inspectorate: the indoor comfort range is 40–60% relative humidity, and up to 70% is mentioned as acceptable in humid climates. Checked 07.11.2026

What comes out of furniture in a flat and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • dust and household dirt on the fabric
  • darker patches on seats and armrests
  • fresher food and drink stains

May stay

  • old stains that have set into the fabric
  • a smell, if liquid has reached the foam
  • a cigarette smell that may come back from the filling
  • wear and faded fabric

In flats with poor ventilation it is sometimes too damp by the outside walls. If you see mould on the back of a sofa or on furniture standing against a wall, the cause of the damp must be dealt with first. In a damp corner, mould can come back after cleaning. More detail: mould in a sofa.

Frequently asked questions

Does the sofa have to be moved before the specialist arrives?

It is usually enough to clear access to it and take off covers and belongings. If a corner sofa stands tight against the wall and the back needs cleaning too, say so in the form, and the specialist will tell you what is needed.

There is no lift in the building. What should I put in the form?

Give the floor and say that there is no lift, so that the specialist knows what to expect. The price depends on size, fabric, how dirty it is, smell and distance, and we agree it before work starts.

The furniture is in a room with no window. Can it be cleaned?

Yes, but drying will then depend on whether the door can be opened and air brought in from another room. Say so in the form, so that the specialist can tell you what to expect.

Can you clean if the air in the flat is damp?

Yes, but the furniture will take longer to dry. If the windows are steamed up or there is mould on an outside wall, first reduce the humidity and air the flat. Otherwise cleaning may do more harm than good.
