Several pieces on one visit

In a house, furniture is spread across floors and rooms, so a list works better than separate requests. Before filling in the form, walk round the house and note what needs cleaning, where it stands and what concerns you about it. Add one photo of each piece and give the floor.

Pouffes and chairs can be brought into one room if that suits you. If the list is longer, the €45 minimum order is no obstacle. Say in the form whether a car can get up to the house and whether everything is in one building, or there is also a separate sauna or summer house.

Furniture in a sauna lounge, summer house or unheated room

This furniture is a separate case, because it stands in damper air than furniture in a living room. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) says that high relative indoor humidity, above 80%, means a high risk of mould, while on leather furniture mould can form from above 65%. In winter, the air in an unheated summer house can be exactly like that.

So before ordering, look at the back and underside, especially of sofas standing against an outside wall. If the smell reminds you of a basement or spots are visible, say so straight away. Mould in the filling often means the furniture has to be replaced. The cause of the damp must be dealt with first. More detail: mould in a sofa.

What comes out of furniture in a house and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • dust and household dirt on the fabric
  • pet hair and dirt on the surface
  • fresher stains on sofas and chairs

May stay

  • mould and its smell in the filling
  • a smell of basements and damp from unheated rooms
  • a smoke smell that may come back from the filling
  • fabric faded by the sun near large windows

In homes with a fireplace or stove, upholstery also takes on a smell of smoke. It may fade, but we cannot promise that it will go: smoke residues stay in fabric and filling for a long time, and the smell may come back.

Frequently asked questions

When should I clean furniture in a summer house that stands empty in winter?

When the house has already been heated and aired. In a damp room furniture dries more slowly, and the mould risk is higher. So in spring, air the house first.

Can furniture in a sauna lounge be cleaned?

Yes, if the room is not wet and the furniture has not gone mouldy. If a sofa in the sauna smells of damp, send a photo and a close-up of the back, and the specialist will tell you whether cleaning makes sense.

There are large dogs in the house. Does that change the price?

It may, as the price depends on how dirty the furniture is and on smell. Say which furniture the animals like best and whether there are urine stains: that affects both the price and the result.
