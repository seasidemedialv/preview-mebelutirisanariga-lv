What we clean and where the limits are

Upholstered furniture means anything with upholstery. On top there may be fabric, velour, leather or faux leather, and underneath foam or another filling. So the first thing is to establish what the piece is and whether it has a care label.

Usually comes out

  • Everyday dirt on the seat and armrests
  • Dust and hair on the surface of the fabric
  • Fresh food and drink stains
  • A smell that has not soaked into the filling

May stay

  • Traces of old stains, especially on light fabric
  • A smell from the filling: urine, smoke or long-term damp
  • Colour faded by the sun, and wear
  • A peeling faux leather coating

For old furniture with an unknown filling, the specialist tells you what you can expect before the work starts. If the fabric is fragile or the filling is damaged, the honest answer may be that an upholstery repair is needed instead of cleaning. We do not do that kind of work.

Furniture dry cleaning: what it usually means

Furniture cleaning is often called dry cleaning, although that usually means cleaning on the spot rather than taking the furniture to a dry cleaner's. The method is set by the fabric and the label: W means water-based foam, S means cleaning without water, WS allows both, and X means no liquids. More: dry or wet furniture cleaning.

The label may be under a cushion or on the underside of the furniture. Old furniture often has none, and manufacturers give the codes voluntarily. What if there is no label? Photograph the piece and say in the form that there is no label, and the specialist will assess it on the spot. There is no rule that suits all furniture.

Leather furniture

Leather furniture cleaning differs from fabric cleaning. Direct contact with water can damage most leathers, heat dries and distorts leather, and stains soak into the leather itself. Stains on suede are better left alone, and ink on leather may stay.

Be careful after a flood: the US Environmental Protection Agency notes that upholstered furniture can be hard to dry quickly enough, and porous material with mould may have to be thrown away.

Smell from upholstered furniture

Smell is not one job. A household smell is often on the surface of the fabric, but smoke and urine may be in the filling. In one study, substances from cigarette smoke stayed in cotton and polyester for a further 19 months, while carpet cleaning, repainting and wiping surfaces indoors gave only a temporary effect. So a smell can usually be reduced, but we cannot promise that it will go.

If the smell comes from damp, the damp has to be dealt with first, otherwise the smell will return. Read: why a sofa smells, cigarette smoke smell in a sofa and pet hair on a sofa.

After cleaning, the furniture is still drying

Drying depends on humidity, airing, temperature, the fabric and the filling, so we do not promise a time. The comfortable indoor humidity given by the Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) is 40–60%. If the furniture is in a damp room, such as a summer house or a basement, drying can take longer. The US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) notes that fans and a dehumidifier speed it up. Keep leather and other sensitive surfaces away from direct heat sources. Frequently asked questions

Do you clean very old furniture?

We can look at a photo and tell you what you can expect. If the fabric is fragile or faded, or the filling has crumbled, cleaning will not restore it, and the specialist will say so before the job.

Do you clean antique chairs?

For valuable and antique furniture, send a photo first and tell us what is known about it. If there is any doubt about the condition of the fabric or the value, it is better to ask a restorer, and we will say so openly.

Can I book several pieces of furniture for one visit?

Yes. List them in one request so that the specialist can give a total price. That way even small pieces together reach the €45 minimum order.

Will the furniture have to be moved?

It is enough to clear access to the furniture: put away fragile objects and personal items. In the form, say where the furniture stands and how much space there is around it.
