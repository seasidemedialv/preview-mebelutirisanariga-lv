Why cleaning is done on site, not in a workshop

A sofa and a mattress are hard to transport: they are large and heavy, and the stairwell is often narrow. So in Riga this work is usually done on site rather than in a workshop, and the furniture stays where it is.

Working on site also has limits. Cleaning does not take the foam out or change the filling, and it is not furniture restoration. If the problem is in the filling, cleaning does not solve it. The specialist will tell you this from a photo or on site.

What to check before ordering if the trip is longer

If the address is more than 15 km outside Riga, it makes sense to order everything that needs cleaning in one go rather than one piece: one trip, and the €45 minimum order is no obstacle. Add photos of anything you are unsure about too: if something cannot be cleaned, it is better to find out before the trip.

If the place is a summer house or a home used only in season, say whether it has heating and whether windows can be opened. After cleaning the furniture dries in the air, and in a cold, damp room that takes longer.

What usually comes out on site and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • household dirt on sofas, chairs and mattresses
  • fresher stains on fabric, if the care label allows it
  • dust and surface dirt on furniture that has stood unused

May stay

  • anything that has reached the filling: urine, a large spill, mould
  • old stains
  • wear, fading and tears
  • a smell, if the source is in the foam

Frequently asked questions

Do you also collect the furniture and bring it back?

No. We only clean at your location. If furniture has to be moved to another address, you arrange that.

Can you clean furniture kept in a garage or storage unit?

Yes, if it can be reached and the space is not damp. Say so in the form, as drying depends on the temperature and air. If the furniture has been kept somewhere damp, check it for mould.

What if the address is only a little beyond 15 km?

Enter it in the form, and the specialist will tell you whether travel is included. We write “about” because the limit is set by the actual road, not a circle on a map.
