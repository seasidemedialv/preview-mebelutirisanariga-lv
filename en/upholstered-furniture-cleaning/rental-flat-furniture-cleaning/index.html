What to check when a tenant moves out

Walk through the flat while it is still empty, and look at the furniture more closely than you would when handing over keys. Photograph the condition straight away: these photos will be useful both to the specialist and to you.

  • Sofa: lift the cushions, look at the seams and the places people usually sit.
  • Mattress: take off the sheet, look at both sides and the edges.
  • Smell: coming in from outside, sniff the fabric up close, because someone who has lived there no longer notices it.
  • Signs of pets: hair in the seams, scratches, a smell under the cushions.
  • Smoking: a yellow tone in light fabric and a smell in the curtains.
  • Damp: look at the back of furniture standing against an outside wall.

Clean or replace: a decision for each piece

Furniture Usually worth cleaning Usually consider replacing
Sofa everyday dirt, food and drink stains urine in the foam, a strong smoke smell, mould, a broken frame
Mattress light stains, dust, no smell large urine or blood stains, mould, a sagging hollow
Dining chairs seats with food stains torn fabric, peeling faux leather
Armchair worn but sound surfaces fabric worn through, a smell in the filling

The deciding piece is usually the mattress. Cleaning a double mattress on both sides is already a sizeable sum, and a new tenant will not sleep easily on someone else's stain. If the mattress is old and deeply soiled, it may be cheaper to replace it and clean only the sofa.

Smoke and pets: smells the new tenant will notice

In studies, cotton and polyester still held substances from cigarette smoke after 19 months, while carpet cleaning, repainting and wiping surfaces in the rooms gave only a short-lived result. So if the flat has been smoked in, do not promise the new tenant that there will be no smell. The condition of the fabric may improve, but the smell may come back, especially if it is also in the walls and curtains.

With urine, the smell patch can be up to three times larger than the visible stain, and if the liquid has reached the foam, cleaning does not always reach it. Heavily soiled fabric stays attractive to cats even after cleaning. If the next tenant brings a cat, that may mean new problems. More detail: cigarette smoke smell in a sofa and urine smell in a mattress.

The order of work between tenants

  1. Inspection and photos Once you have the keys back, inspect the furniture and photograph it. Dated photos are also your evidence of its condition.
  2. Decision and repairs Decide what to replace. Painting, floor work and other dusty jobs must be finished before the furniture is cleaned.
  3. Request Fill in the form with photos and say who will open the door if you do not live in the flat.
  4. Airing After cleaning, the furniture has to dry. In an empty flat in winter, the heating must be on and the room aired, because furniture dries slowly in damp, cool air.
  5. Photos for the listing Photograph the flat and show it to tenants only once the furniture is dry.

What comes out of rental furniture and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • everyday dirt on the sofa and chairs
  • food and drink stains, if they are not old
  • a greyish look on the surface of a mattress

May stay

  • a cigarette smell that may come back from the filling
  • a urine smell in the foam
  • old stains that have set in
  • tears, scratches and wear

If you see bedbugs in the furniture, that is a job for pest control, and it has to be done first.

Frequently asked questions

Can the cost of cleaning be deducted from a tenant's deposit?

That depends on your tenancy agreement and the law, and it is not for us to decide. We only clean the furniture. Photos taken straight after the move-out help both sides see what condition it was in.

Can you clean the whole flat, not just the furniture?

No. We clean sofas, upholstered furniture, chairs and mattresses. Floors, windows, the bathroom and the kitchen are not our job, so look for a general cleaning service for those.

Do I have to be there during the cleaning?

Someone has to open the door. Say in the form who will do that and whether the flat is empty. Agree the rest with the specialist when they get in touch.

Can I promise the new tenant there will be no smell?

Only make that promise once the furniture has dried and you have smelled it in the empty, aired flat. The smell may also come back later, especially in damp weather.
