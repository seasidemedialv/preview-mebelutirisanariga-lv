Why a headboard looks dirty sooner than the rest of the bed

Every evening a headboard is touched by hair, skin and your back as you read or look at your phone. The fabric darkens gradually there, so it is often noticed only when the patch is already clearly visible. Unlike a mattress, a headboard is vertical, stands against the wall and cannot be turned over or covered.

The fabric is the most important thing to know. If the bed has a care label, it is usually on the underside or the back of the frame. The codes W, S, WS and X are the same as for upholstered furniture, and they decide whether the fabric can take water at all. If there is no label, take a close-up of the fabric.

What comes out of a headboard and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • dust and household dirt on the headboard and frame
  • darker patches, if the dirt has not soaked in deeply
  • fresher stains on the fabric

May stay

  • a dark patch where a head has rested for years
  • pressure marks and differences in shade in velour
  • worn or crushed fabric
  • a water ring, if the fabric was overwetted in the past

Velour is a demanding fabric for a headboard. Water and pressure can crush the viscose pile for good, and pressure marks in velour are a property of the fabric, not a defect. So with a velour headboard it is especially important not to rub a stain at home: blot liquid from the edges towards the centre and let it dry.

Headboard, mattress or both

If the problem is only visual, the headboard alone is usually enough. If the sleeping area has stains or a smell, or if neither has been cleaned for years, it makes more sense to order both at once. That way the minimum order is no obstacle and both jobs are done on one visit. More on mattresses: mattress cleaning and how often to clean a mattress.

Before the specialist arrives, take off the bedding, pillows and decorative items. Dust mites live in mattresses and upholstery, but cleaning is not a treatment for allergies. The number of mites is affected more by humidity below 50%, washing bedding at 60 °C or more and regular vacuuming than by a single clean. If you see bedbugs, that is not a cleaning job: it is a job for pest control, and it has to come before any cleaning.

Frequently asked questions

Can a headboard be cleaned if it is fixed to the wall?

Often yes, but it depends on the fabric and how close the wall is. Photograph the full height of the headboard together with the wall, and say how it is fixed.

Does the bed have to be taken apart before cleaning?

Usually not, but take off the bedding and decorative cushions. Say in the form whether the bed can be reached from both sides and whether it stands against a wall. The specialist will agree the rest when they get in touch.

The headboard fabric is wet from spilt tea. What should I do?

Gently blot it with a clean cloth, without rubbing. You can rinse the stain with cool water, but do not soak the fabric, and never use hot water. If the fabric is velour, blot from the outer edge inwards.
